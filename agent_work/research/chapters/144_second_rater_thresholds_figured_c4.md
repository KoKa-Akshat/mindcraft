## CXLIV.1 Why this chapter exists

SAFE-PROMOTE foreshadowed an **optional** second rater (ambiguous IR, parent-facing ambition, prior demote) and reserved PROMOTE-3 for single vs dual. Missing: the **threshold rule**. Without it, ops drifts into always-dual costume (FILLETA explosion + “double-checked” ads), never-dual soft-pass (pretty SVG → Map poison), Dual Rater Score™ vanity, mammography cosplay ACT theater, second-rater override of failed seals, or ETA optics that skip dual when backlog is hot.

Parents who buy labeled FormatId holes deserve **stricter review when ambition is high** — not theater dual on every MC or silent single-rater admits on diagrams that write Map.

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** Near-peer tutors can be first raters under a fixed checklist; a **second rater or SME escalate** is load-bearing for figured C4 admits that authorize parent-facing “we diagnosed diagram on X” — dual is a **trigger**, not a brand costume.

**Claims we refuse:** always-dual everywhere; never-dual on figured parent-facing C4; Dual Rater Score™ / Kappa % NS; seal-waiving dual; mammography-cosplay guarantees; ETA-skipped dual; Discord-emoji≡second rater; ACT/complete-visual guarantees from dual packaging.

## CXLIV.3 Higher-ambition uses need more backing — dual is use evidence, not politeness

**FACT:** Kane (2013, *Journal of Educational Measurement, 50*(1), 1–73, doi:10.1111/jedm.12000) — validate **interpretations and uses**; more ambitious claims need more backing; rejecting a use need not kill a weaker interpretation.

**FACT:** AERA, APA, & NCME (2014, *Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing*) — each intended use needs relevant evidence.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Reveal-Practice is lower ambition. C4→Map→parent “diagram diagnosed on concept X” is higher. Dual review is additional **use evidence** when the item’s pictorial ground truth is hard to re-check after the fact — not a courtesy ritual and not required for every low-ambition text stem.

**Kill:** Dual as brand politeness; single rater for all ambitions.  
**Survive:** Dual intensity scales with use ambition and figure risk.

## CXLIV.4 Chance-corrected agreement is a process tool — not a product North Star

**FACT:** Cohen (1960, *Educational and Psychological Measurement, 20*(1), 37–46, doi:10.1177/001316446002000104) — percent agreement ignores chance concordance; kappa adjusts for chance on nominal categories.

**FACT:** McHugh (2012, *Biochemia Medica, 22*(3), 276–282, doi:10.11613/BM.2012.031, PMC3900052) — kappa is widely used for IRR; Landis/Koch-style “moderate” bands can be too lenient for high-stakes contexts; many texts treat percent agreement below ~80% and kappa below ~0.60 as inadequate for serious decisions (healthcare framing; transfer with caution).

**FACT:** NCES / NAEP Technical Documentation (Constructed-Response Interrater Reliability; last updated 2024) — second scoring is used to monitor rater consistency; kappa targets cited as higher than .7 for two-/three-point items and higher than .6 for four- to six-point items; items below threshold **prompt investigation** — criteria are NAEP-design-specific, not universal product law.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** MindCraft should **sample dual** promote decisions to watch disagree/repair catch rates and (for yes/no essential-for-C4) chance-corrected agreement. Failures trigger rater retraining or checklist rewrite — not Dual Rater Score™ splash, not “our kappa is 0.9 so Map is true,” not student-facing Agreement Personality™.

**Kill:** Kappa / percent-agreement as marketing NS or ACT proof.  
**Survive:** IRR as ops health signal with investigation thresholds (inspired by NAEP’s investigate-below-target pattern; do not copy NAEP cutoffs as brand law).

## CXLIV.5 Expert “essential” judgment can be dual — CVR is method, not badge

**Reuse (SAFE-PROMOTE):** Lawshe (1975, *Personnel Psychology, 28*(4), 563–575, doi:10.1111/j.1744-6570.1975.tb01393.x) — Content Validity Ratio aggregates SME essential/useful/not-necessary marks; panel size affects critical values; employment-testing origin → **transfer limits**.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** For parent-facing figured C4, the checklist’s “essential for C4 diagnostic use?” bit is the right dual object — not beauty. Two raters marking essential independently is closer to Lawshe’s panel idea than one rater’s thumbs-up. Full CVR panels of 5–40 SMEs are **cost theater** for every FormatId stem at startup scale; reserve multi-SME panels for archetype templates / first-of-cell blueprints, not every isomorphic clone.

**Kill:** CVR Score™ splash; 15-SME panel per item as default.  
**Survive:** Dual essential-bit on high-ambition figured admits; larger panels only for template/archetype gates.

## CXLIV.6 Double reading elsewhere: gains, costs, policy — transfer carefully

**FACT:** Brown, Bryan, & Warren (1996, *BMJ, 312*(7034), 809–812, doi:10.1136/bmj.312.7034.809) — in one UK screening unit, consensus double reading detected more cancers than single reading and could reduce recall/total cost vs single; non-consensus double reading raised cost.

**FACT:** Dinnes, Moss, Melia, Blanks, Song, & Kleijnen (2001, *The Breast, 10*(6), 455–463, doi:10.1054/brst.2001.0350) — systematic review: double reading can raise detection (~3–11 per 10,000 screened); recall impact depends on **recall policy** (consensus/arbitration vs either-reader).

**FACT:** Posso, Carles, Rué, Puig, & Bonfill (2016, *PLOS ONE, 11*(7), e0159806, doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0159806) — programme analysis: double vs single detection similar; incremental cost per extra detection substantial; cost-effectiveness is **not automatic**.

**Transfer limits:** Mammography ≠ tutoring item QA (base rates, harms, labor markets differ). Borrow only: second readers catch misses; consensus vs either-policy matters; always-dual is not free excellence.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Dual promote ≈ consensus: agree on essential-for-C4 **and** stem–figure–key; discord → reject/repair, not either-admits. No hospital cosplay ads. Do not assume always-dual wins when seals already catch most IR failures.

**Kill:** “We double-read like hospitals ⇒ ACT-ready.” **Survive:** Consensus dual on triggered high-risk admits; cost in FILLETA capacity.

## CXLIV.7 Trigger rule (doctrine core)

**HYPOTHESIS — mandatory second rater when ANY of:**

| Trigger | Why |
|---------|-----|
| **T1 Figured FormatId → C4** | Pictorial ground truth; hard post-hoc student re-check (SAFE-FIGKEY) |
| **T2 Parent-/Map-facing diagnosis ambition** | Higher Kane use (hole fill that claims FormatId probed) |
| **T3 Prior demote / critical REPAIR history on item or stem-family** | Proven failure mode |
| **T4 Ambiguous or non-machine-checkable figure IR** | First rater is eye-test-adjacent; escalate |
| **T5 First sealed admit for a concept×FormatId cell** | Blueprint risk; later clones may inherit single if isomorphic hash trusts template (see id 145) |
| **T6 First-rater self-flags uncertainty** | Honest escalate > fake certainty |

**Optional (single OK) when ALL of:** text/symbolic (no required figure), Practice-only or Reveal use (not C4), seals green, no prior demote, not first-of-cell blueprint, first rater confidence unmarked.

**Arbitration:** Disagree on essential-for-C4 or joint truth → **do not promote**; open REPAIR or reject; third SME only if ops policy budgets it — never average to admit.

**Capacity:** Dual minutes enter FILLETA estimator. Hitting a parent band **never** clears T1–T6.

## CXLIV.10 Doctrine — SAFE-DUALRATE (provisional)

1. **Dual intensity follows use ambition + figure risk** (Kane), not brand politeness.  
2. **Written triggers** (T1–T6) — never always-dual costume, never never-dual soft-pass.  
3. **Consensus dual** — agree to admit; discord rejects.  
4. **Seals > raters** — humans cannot waive FIGKEY/GENQ.  
5. **IRR is ops hygiene** — investigate; do not sell kappa.  
6. **Capacity is real** — dual minutes in FILLETA; no ETA waiver.  
7. **HITL first rater + escalate** — no Ivy≡dual, no Discord≡panel.  
8. **No Dual Rater Score™ / Agreement Minutes / Kappa % NS**.  
9. **No mammography-cosplay ACT / complete-visual guarantees**.  
10. Copy: “Stricter review when the picture writes the Map — not double theater on every stem.”

**Confidence:** High on Kane/Standards/Cohen/McHugh/NAEP-as-process-pattern and on mammography facts *with transfer limits*. Medium on exact T1–T6 set and whether T1 alone (all figured C4) is too broad vs T1∧T2. High that always-dual and never-dual are both commercially toxic (FILLETA explosion vs Map distrust).
