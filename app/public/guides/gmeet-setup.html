Set up Google Meet in 4 moves.

One permanent room. Every booked session without its own link uses it. Simple for parents. Reliable for you.

  1. Go to meet.google.com while signed into your school Google account.
  2. Click New meeting → Create a meeting for later.
  3. Copy the meet.google.com link. Keep this room. Do not delete it.
  4. In MindCraft Tutor Dashboard, open Google Meet, paste the link, and save.

Reuse the same room for every student. Rename the calendar event each time if you want a cleaner history.
