## CXVI.1 Why this chapter exists

Maya still does most real math on **paper and whiteboard**. Typed equation editors and always-on phone solvers are competitors’ defaults — and traps. Parents want proof she practiced; tutors want her work visible; growth wants “AI that reads your notebook.” The commercial trap is **capture-as-cheating-pipe**: photo features that collapse into Photomath (answer delivery) or phone-open mid-attempt residue (SAFE-ATTN), while marketing pretends OCR fluency is identity change.

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** Offline media can reduce interface extraneous load and preserve spatial math — *if* capture is post-attempt, verified, and non-solver — not a camera that finishes the problem for her.

**Claims we refuse as doctrine:** Capture Score™ / Photo Minutes NS; OCR / handwriting recognition ≡ mastery; photo→worked-solution as default Practice rail; always-on camera during attempts; phone-open “study mode” as focus; Photomath-clone GTM; Capture Minutes as North Star; guaranteed ACT from notebook photos.

## CXVI.3 Handwriting / pen input — fluency gains ≠ automatic learning gains

**FACT (handwriting ITS paradigm):** Anthony, Yang, & Koedinger (2012, *International Journal of Human-Computer Studies, 70*(11), 866–887, doi:10.1016/j.ijhcs.2012.04.003) — pen input for algebra can be **faster, smoother, better liked**, and support **transfer to non-computer tasks** vs typing; recognition is unreliable unless **context** reduces errors; design pattern: handwrite process, type final answer; recovery should focus on **problem-solving error**, not OCR theater.

**FACT (input method school study):** de Morais & Jaques (2022, *Informatics in Education*, doi:10.15388/infedu.2022.03) — 55 seventh-graders; handwriting vs typing on tablet/desktop in step-based math ITS. Handwriting users solved **more equations and were faster**, but **no statistically significant learning difference** vs typing; mismatched device (e.g., handwriting with mouse) **hurt** performance.
**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Prefer paper/pen for **fluency and spatial naturalness**, not as a learning miracle. Value = lower interface friction + medium fidelity — still need FEI. Do not sell “handwriting AI raises scores.”

**Kill:** Handwriting≡deeper learning marketing; Capture Score™ from speed.  
**Survive:** Pen/paper as default attempt medium; device-matched input; recognition designed around tutoring, not vanity OCR accuracy.

## CXVI.4 Longhand encoding — useful caution, not laptop ban religion

**FACT (longhand vs laptop notes):** Mueller & Oppenheimer (2014, *Psychological Science, 25*(6), 1159–1168, doi:10.1177/0956797614524581) — across three studies, laptop note-takers performed worse on **conceptual** questions than longhand; mechanism claimed = verbatim transcription → shallower processing (even without multitasking).

**FACT (replication caution):** Morehead, Dunlosky, & Rawson (2019, *Educational Psychology Review, 31*, 753–780, doi:10.1007/s10648-019-09468-2) — direct replication/extension: performance did **not consistently differ**; meta-analysis of direct replications showed small nonsignificant longhand advantages; concluding universal superiority is **premature**.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Use Mueller as a **warning against shallow digital transcription**, not as “ban screens / Capture Minutes = science.” MindCraft’s offline bet is **problem-solving medium + attempt grain**, not lecture-note cosplay.

**Kill:** “Science says paper always beats digital” absolute ads; Pen Minutes NS.  
**Survive:** Prefer constructive longhand *attempts* over verbatim UI clicking; stay open to null medium effects when FEI holds.

## CXVI.5 Photo-solve apps — the competitive anti-pattern

**FACT (teacher response frame):** Webel & Otten (2015, *Mathematics Teacher, 109*(5), 368–373) — Photomath-style photo→answer+steps forces teachers to **ban, restrict, or redesign goals**; “cheating” is about **who gets credit** for the work, not tools existing; ambitious response = tasks where human reasoning remains the point.

**FACT (platform-scale speed shift):** Sloan-Lynch, Gay, & Watkins (2022, *LAK ’22*, doi:10.1145/3506860.3506868) — ~700 unique exercises, **>82 million** submissions; post–photo-app era shows **dramatic shifts toward ultra-fast correct submissions** on susceptible items, contemporaneous with Photomath proliferation — evidence of **lost engagement**, not mastery fireworks.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Any MindCraft “snap your work” feature that returns **full solutions before attempt** is a Photomath clone and violates SAFE-HELP / SAFE-HWHELP dual-rail (assisted ≠ ready). Capture must ingest **student work after struggle**, then coach/Map — never answer-first.

**Kill:** Photo→steps as Practice hero; “AI notebook that finishes homework”; speed-to-correct as success KPI on capturable items.  
**Survive:** Photo as **evidence pipe** for attempt grain; redesign tasks that survive answer apps (Webel Option 3) inside FEI / transfer probes.

## CXVI.6 Phone as capture tool = attention residue risk

**FACT (attention residue):** Leroy (2009, *Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes, 109*(2), 168–181, doi:10.1016/j.obhdp.2009.04.002) — unfinished prior tasks leave residue that impairs next-task performance.

**FACT (phone presence “brain drain”):** Ward, Duke, Gneezy, & Bos (2017, *Journal of the Association for Consumer Research, 2*(2), 140–154, doi:10.1086/691462) — mere presence of one’s smartphone can reduce available cognitive capacity even when unused.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Capture UX must be a **short park/resume ritual** (SAFE-ATTN): attempt offline with phone away → open app → snap → confirm → phone down. Mid-attempt camera-as-companion invites Photomath side-door + residue. Tutor-held capture (photograph sheets for mediation) can keep phones out of student hands during solve.

**Kill:** Always-on phone study companion; mid-attempt DAU pushes to “capture now”; Focus Score™ from camera uptime.  
**Survive:** Post-attempt capture windows; tutor-mediated photo for live sessions; attempt-window protection metrics.

## CXVI.7 Recognition / key risk — wrong OCR poisons the graph

**FACT (recognition pragmatics):** Anthony et al. (2012) — unreliable recognition is the downside of pen input; systems must use **context** and keep recovery on **math error**, not OCR nagging.

**FOUNDER BELIEF (product):** MindCraft Map writes are high-stakes (mastery, diagnostics). Unverified OCR of messy algebra is a **SAFE-GENQ cousin**: fluent wrongness entering the graph.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Capture pipeline: (1) store image as proof artifact (SAFE-PROOF), (2) optional OCR proposal, (3) **student or tutor confirm** before `/record-outcomes` / Map edge updates, (4) never auto-green from OCR alone. Aligns with hide-correctness diagnostics (C4) — wrong keys corrupt identity evidence.

**Kill:** OCR accuracy % as product hero; silent auto-mastery from photos.  
**Survive:** Human-in-the-loop confirm; image-as-proof even when OCR fails; GENQ-style verify-before-ship for capture.

## CXVI.8 Product law — attempt → capture → confirm → Map

**HYPOTHESIS (SAFE-OFFLINE stack):**

1. **Offline-first attempt** — paper/whiteboard (or stylus) before typed editor when format allows; respect spatial math (SAFE-FORMAT / SAFE-GESTURE draw).  
2. **Commit before capture** — prediction / answer commit or time-on-attempt gate before photo tools unlock (SAFE-PREDICT / SAFE-HELP).  
3. **Brief capture ritual** — open → snap → confirm → close; phone away during solve (SAFE-ATTN).  
4. **Verify before graph write** — student/tutor confirm OCR; image stored as competence evidence (SAFE-PROOF / SAFE-GENQ).  
5. **No answer-dump path** — capture feeds diagnosis/coach chips, not Photomath steps-first (SAFE-HWHELP dual-rail).  
6. **Tutor whiteboard parity** — session photos are AAR/film clips for soft-wrong review (SAFE-AAR / SAFE-FILM), not shame reels.  
7. **Parent honesty** — show aged proof artifacts (“worked on paper Tue”) without tonight-% stalk (SAFE-PDASH).  
8. **Densify** — SAFE-ATTN / HELP / HWHELP / GESTURE / PROOF / GENQ / FORMAT / FADE.  
9. **Ban** Capture Score™ / Photo Minutes / OCR≡mastery / always-camera / photo→answer hero / Capture ACT guarantees.

**Kill:** Notebook-AI answer brand; camera-open Practice.  
**Survive:** Paper-native gym with inspectable capture into Map.

## CXVI.13 Doctrine — SAFE-OFFLINE (provisional)

1. **Medium serves attempt grain** — paper/whiteboard preferred for spatial/natural solve; not a learning talisman.  
2. **Fluency ≠ learning gain** — Anthony / de Morais: speed and liking without automatic posttest superiority.  
3. **Capture after commit** — no camera-first answer path (Webel; Sloan-Lynch speed pathology).  
4. **Residue-aware ritual** — brief snap, then phone down (Leroy; Ward; SAFE-ATTN).  
5. **Verify before graph write** — OCR proposes; human confirms; image is proof (SAFE-GENQ / SAFE-PROOF).  
6. **Dual-rail with Solver** — instrumental help elsewhere; capture is evidence, not Chegg (SAFE-HWHELP).  
7. **Longhand caution, not religion** — Mueller insight + Morehead nulls; FEI adjudicates.  
8. **Densify, don’t fork** — SAFE-ATTN / HELP / HWHELP / GESTURE / PROOF / GENQ / FORMAT.  
9. **No Capture Score™ / Photo Minutes / OCR≡mastery / always-camera / photo→answer hero / Capture ACT guarantees**.

**Confidence:** High that photo→answer capture is commercially and pedagogically unsafe vs FEI; high that unverified OCR must not write mastery; medium that paper-first beats typed-only on transfer once attempt quality is equated — instrument OFFLINE-*; high to kill Capture Score™ / OCR≡mastery / Photomath-hero regardless.
