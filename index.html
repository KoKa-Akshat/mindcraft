Questions, answered straight

The stuff everyone asks first.

Is this just another AI homework bot?

No. A chatbot forgets you by tomorrow; The Desk keeps a record, maps it, and briefs a real person.

Does it do my homework for me?

No. Solver walks you through steps and asks you questions back. The Desk makes you faster, not fake.

How do I know the AI isn't teaching my kid something wrong?

We assume it will and check first; that's what the gate above is for. A failed lesson shows an honest “couldn't make a good one yet.”

Who are the mentors?

College students who remember what junior year felt like. They open your map before the session. Your first one is free.

My kid can't sit through a page of text. Will this work?

That's who we build for first. Same idea, four doors: simulation, story, voice, picture. The engine learns which one works for your kid.

What can I drop on the desk, and where does it go?

Photos, PDFs, docs, screenshots, scans of handwritten work. It all lands in your private binder, yours to export or delete, never sold.

Is it only for the ACT?

The ACT road is the deepest-built part, but the desk works all year on essays, decks, code, and scholarship applications.
