## B. Flip post-mortem (5 bullets)
1. Free async short video for private class groups (~12 years; Microsoft acquired 2018).
2. Killed Sept 30, 2024 — folded partial features into Teams; free tool lost strategy argument.
3. Teachers lost comments/Mixtapes/AR; Google Classroom districts locked out; data deleted within ~30 days.
4. Loved: shy-student voice, near-zero friction, fun ≠ homework, private-by-default, free.
5. Lesson: survive only inside the core product loop (mastery evidence); guarantee export/retention.

## C. Safety checklist
**Must:** private-by-default · tutor/teacher review · moderation · age gate/VPC under-13 · deletion/retention limits · encryption · student-owned export · DPAs  
**Avoid:** public FYP · unmoderated DMs · default share-to-TikTok · targeted ads / selling data · AI train on student media without opt-in · perpetual content license · indefinite retention

## D. Learning science
- Chi et al. 1994 — self-explanation improves mental models (8th grade band)
- Fiorella & Mayer — teaching / self-explaining generative strategies (d≈0.61 / 0.71)
- Chase et al. 2009 — protégé effect
- Hoogerheide et al. 2014/2016 — webcam explainer videos beat restudy; **video > writing** for transfer
- Caveat: learning = explaining, not decorating
