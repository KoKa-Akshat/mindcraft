## CXXVIII.1 Why this chapter exists

SAFE-DISTRACTOR already bans beauty traps and unmapped wrongs for *symbolic* MC. Format-axis items reopen the failure mode in a sharper costume: wrong options that look like “visual engagement” — pretty wrong graphs, stock art beside stems, or option thumbnails that cue by color/length rather than by a known representational error.

Default edtech resolves this badly: (1) decorate the stem with franchise chrome and call it dual coding (SAFE-DUAL / SAFE-STORYLOAD), (2) invent three “almost right” graphs without naming *which* graph schema failed, (3) treat a diagram miss as “weak functions” instead of a FormatId gap (SAFE-FORMAT), (4) ship LLM-drawn options that are fluent but unkeyed (SAFE-GENQ), (5) FormatTrap Score™ / Visual Distractor Minutes as quality theater, (6) marketing “we teach every format” while distractors are decoration-adjacent gotchas.

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** On diagram/graph MC, the commercially load-bearing wrong pick is a **format trap** — a plausible misreading of the vessel — not a random wrong number wearing a picture. MindCraft’s wedge vs symbolic drill packs and ChatGPT text-only fluency is *inspectable representation diagnosis* + conversion remediation.

**Claims we refuse as doctrine:**
1. Decorative stem art / seductive details ≡ FormatId coverage or pedagogy.  
2. FormatTrap Score™ / Visual Distractor Minutes / “richer visuals” NS.  
3. Beauty-trap option art (prettier wrong graph; length/color clang).  
4. Unmapped visual wrongs as “diagnostic FormatId soft-wrong.”  
5. Graph-as-picture / slope–height misses collapsed to topic weakness only.  
6. LLM figure options≡shipped bank without format-trap seal + key verify.  
7. Always-add-picture distractors ≡ dual coding science.  
8. ACT / grade guarantees from visual-distractor packaging.

## CXXVIII.2 Constructs (format trap ≠ pretty wrong picture)

| Construct | Research meaning | MindCraft analogue | Failure mode |
|-----------|------------------|--------------------|--------------|
| **Format trap** | Plausible misread of a representation | Named seal on option (`graph_as_picture`, `slope_height`, `axis_swap`, `legend_split`, …) | Bare “incorrect graph” |
| **Graph-as-picture** | Treat graph as literal scene sketch | Distractor whose shape mimics the story scene | Gotcha hill-drawing without route |
| **Slope–height confusion** | Confuse rate (slope) with value (height) | Option that answers height when stem asks slope (or vice versa) | Topic re-teach only |
| **Conversion failure** | Cannot translate across registers (Duval) | Format-gap CTA: diagram↔symbolic hop | Re-explain concept in strong vessel |
| **Seductive detail** | Interesting but irrelevant adjunct | Story chrome / joke art on stem or options | “Engagement” brand |
| **Structural figure** | Pictorial element encodes the math object | FormatId-necessary diagram/graph/number line | Decoration tagged as diagram |
| **SAFE-FORMTRAP** | Visual distractor craft + QA law | This chapter | FormatTrap Score™ / decoration theater |

**Operational definition (HYPOTHESIS):** A diagram/graph MC item is *SAFE-FORMTRAP complete* when (a) the stem figure is **structurally necessary** (SAFE-DUAL / SAFE-FORMAT), not seductive decoration, (b) every visual distractor carries a **named format-trap intent** (or mis_/slip if non-visual), (c) options are homogeneous vessels (all graphs / all number lines — Haladyna homogeneity), (d) soft-wrong UX names the *representation theory* chosen and routes conversion practice, (e) generated figures fail ship without trap seal + key verify (SAFE-GENQ), and (f) value is proved by format-trap→route hit / `format_hop_solo_transfer_pass` / `retry_120s` — not Visual Distractor Minutes.

## CXXVIII.3 Graphs are learned symbol systems — traps are schema errors

**FACT (functions & graphs review):** Leinhardt, Zaslavsky, & Stein (1990, *Review of Educational Research, 60*(1), 1–64, doi:10.3102/00346543060001001) — interpretation and construction of graphs involve specific misconceptions and intuitions; graphical and algebraic worlds are distinct symbol systems students must connect; teaching/learning research must treat graph comprehension as content-specific, not generic “visual talent.”

**FACT (graph-as-picture & slope–height):** Clement (1985, in Streefland, Ed., *Proceedings of the Ninth PME Conference*, Vol. 1, pp. 369–375) — documents **treating the graph as a picture** (global shape matching a scene; local feature correspondence such as “crossing = meeting”) and **slope–height confusions** on kinematics-style graphs; proposes cognitive accounts of non-standard symbolization strategies rather than random carelessness.

**FACT (graph comprehension factors):** Shah & Hoeffner (2002, *Educational Psychology Review, 14*(1), 47–69, doi:10.1023/A:1013180410169) — comprehension depends on visual characteristics of the display, viewer knowledge of graph schemas, and expectations about content; poor design and weak graph schemas produce systematic misreads — instructional implication: teach graphical literacy, do not assume figures are transparent.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Format-trap distractors should be **authored from these error families** (and MindCraft/Eedi mis_ analogues): graph-as-picture option; slope-when-height (and reverse); swapped axes / inverted ordered pairs; reading a category as a continuous slope; legend–series mismatch. Soft-wrong copy: “You read the graph as a picture of the hill” / “You answered height when the question asked rate” — then a FormatId hop CTA, not “review linear functions.”

**Kill:** Visual miss ≡ topic weakness only; unnamed “wrong graph.”  
**Survive:** Named format-trap seals → Map format gap / conversion Practice.

## CXXVIII.4 Conversion across registers — why FormatId distractors matter commercially

**FACT (semiotic registers):** Duval (2006, *Educational Studies in Mathematics, 61*(1–2), 103–131) — **treatment** (within one register) and **conversion** (across registers) are distinct; conversion failures often decide comprehension even when within-register work looks fine.

**Reuse (SAFE-FORMAT):** Format gap = competence in vessel A with elevated fail in vessel B + remediation that targets reading/producing/converting the weak vessel.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** A correct symbolic twin does not redeem a graph-as-picture pick. Bank events must carry FormatId + trap seal so `/recommend` can prefer format severity over concept-only narratives. Parent/tutor grain: “Missed the graph read, not the algebra idea” when evidence supports it — never Identity Score™ from one miss (SAFE-CONFMISS).

**Kill:** Single-format accuracy ≡ concept mastery; format-count ads.  
**Survive:** Conversion as the remediation object after a format-trap pick.

## CXXVIII.5 Coherence — seductive details are not FormatId distractors

**FACT (coherence / seductive details):** Mayer, Heiser, & Lonn (2001, *Journal of Educational Psychology, 93*(1), 187–198, doi:10.1037/0022-0663.93.1.187) — adding interesting but extraneous multimedia can impair understanding.

**FACT (meta-analysis):** Sundararajan & Adesope (2020, *Educational Psychology Review, 32*, 707–734, doi:10.1007/s10648-020-09522-4) — across dozens of studies, including seductive details tends to **hinder** learning outcomes; moderators include design and delivery — practical implication aligns with the coherence principle: cut irrelevant interesting material.

**Applied (FOUNDER BELIEF → testable):** Story-world chrome that does not encode the probed structure is **banned on diagnostic FormatId stems and options**. A “visual distractor” that is merely prettier wrong art is a beauty trap (SAFE-DISTRACTOR), not a format trap. Decorative thumbnails that clang by color/size violate Haladyna choice guidelines and poison diagnosis.

**Kill:** Seductive stem art; beauty-trap option pictures; “visual engagement” as distractor rationale.  
**Survive:** Structurally necessary figures; trap-mapped homogeneous options.

**Wound:** Prove-rail ACT items may include exam-realistic clutter; learn/diagnostic rails optimize coherence + trap seals — label the dual-rail (SAFE-EXAM / SAFE-DDSCHED).

## CXXVIII.6 Item-writing law still applies — now to vessels

**FACT (MC guidelines):** Haladyna, Downing, & Rodriguez (2002, *Applied Measurement in Education, 15*(3), 309–334, doi:10.1207/S15324818AME1503_5) — distractors from typical student errors; options homogeneous; avoid clues (clang, absurd options, length cues); three effective options often suffice.

**FACT (diagnostic MC):** Wang et al. (2020, arXiv:2007.12061) — each incorrect answer chosen to highlight a common misconception; choice reveals the nature of the misconception for remediation.

**Reuse (SAFE-DISTRACTOR):** Function over fashion; retire dead options; GENQ needs seals.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** For FormatId MC: (1) write trap families first, (2) render options as **parallel vessels** (same graph type, comparable ink), (3) verify the key structurally (SAFE-GENQ figure verify — not fluency of SVG), (4) soft-wrong names the trap + routes conversion, (5) prefer ~3 options when a fourth is decoration or non-functioning.

**Kill:** Four pretty graphs as quality costume; unmapped visual MC as “diagnostic.”  
**Survive:** Trap-first authoring; functioning analysis on visual options too.

## CXXVIII.8 Doctrine — SAFE-FORMTRAP (provisional)

1. **Trap families first** — author from graph-as-picture, slope–height, axis/legend/schema errors; stems serve diagnosis.  
2. **Every visual wrong has intent** — `format_trap_id` (or mis_/slip) — never naked incorrect graph.  
3. **Structure over seduction** — stem/option figures must encode the math object; cut seductive details (Mayer; Sundararajan & Adesope).  
4. **Homogeneous vessels** — options comparable; no beauty/clang cues (Haladyna).  
5. **Soft-wrong names the representation theory** — then conversion Practice, not topic dump alone (Duval; SAFE-FORMAT).  
6. **No FormatTrap Score™ / Visual Distractor Minutes NS** — bank health = seal rate + functioning % + format-hop transfer.  
7. **GENQ equals craft** — generated figures need trap seals + structural key verify (SAFE-GENQ / SAFE-DUAL).  
8. **Dual-rail honesty** — prove-rail may match ACT clutter; learn/diagnostic rails optimize coherence + traps — label which rail.  
9. **Copy:** “Wrong graphs that teach — each option is a reading theory we can route.” Never “more visuals = smarter test.”

**Confidence:** High — Leinhardt et al.; Clement; Shah & Hoeffner; Duval; Mayer et al.; Sundararajan & Adesope; Haladyna et al.; Wang et al. Medium — MindCraft live functioning rates for visual options and auto-trap-seal accuracy (needs FORMTRAP-*). High — decoration theater / beauty visual traps / unmapped “FormatId diagnostic” MC as commercially toxic under FEI + Map honesty.
