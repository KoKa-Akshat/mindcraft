"...light hits the leaf, chlorophyll absorbs it, water gets split somewhere in here?? ATP + that other one made, then the cycle thing uses CO2 to build sugar. ASK: why 6 CO2?"

  • Light reactions run in the thylakoid: light + water in, ATP + NADPH + oxygen out.
  • The Calvin cycle runs in the stroma: it spends that ATP and NADPH to turn CO2 into glucose.
  • Your open question is answered: 6 CO2 because glucose has 6 carbons. One carbon fixed per turn.
