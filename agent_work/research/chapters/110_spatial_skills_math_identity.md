## CX.3 Spatial and math co-vary — moderately, uniquely, not as destiny

**FACT (meta-analysis):** Atit, Power, Pigott, Lee, Geer, Uttal, Ganley, & Sorby (2022, *Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, 29*(3), 699–720, doi:10.3758/s13423-021-02012-w) — synthesizing 45 articles: positive **moderate** association between spatial and mathematical skills (**r = .36**); no significant moderation by gender or grade-level in the association magnitude; fluid reasoning and verbal skills **mediate** part of the link, yet a **unique** spatial–math relation remains.

**FACT (longitudinal STEM):** Wai, Lubinski, & Benbow (2009, *Journal of Educational Psychology, 101*(4), 817–835, doi:10.1037/a0016127) — spatial ability predicts STEM educational and occupational outcomes beyond verbal/math measures in large talent-search and Project TALENT alignments; spatial is a neglected but load-bearing dimension for STEM pathways — **predictive**, not a license for “boys are spatial” product copy (pair with SAFE-GENDER).

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Parent/tutor language may say “the graph format is the bottleneck” when FormatId evidence shows it — never “she’s not a spatial person.” Correlation is real; trait branding is banned.

**Kill:** Spatial Personality™; innate visual-learner destiny.  
**Survive:** Moderate unique spatial–math link as reason to instrument FormatId hops.

## CX.4 Spatial skills are malleable — training works on spatial tasks

**FACT (meta-analysis):** Uttal, Meadow, Tipton, Hand, Alden, Warren, & Newcombe (2013, *Psychological Bulletin, 139*(2), 352–402, doi:10.1037/a0028446) — 217 training studies: average training effect vs control **Hedges’s g ≈ 0.47**; effects durable across delays; transfer to other **spatial** tasks observed; typology along intrinsic/extrinsic × static/dynamic axes. Spatially enriched education is a serious policy bet — **for spatial skill**, not automatically for ACT math.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** MindCraft may improve diagram/graph competence via deliberate FormatId practice; do not invent a separate Spatial Score™ product line that never touches ontology concepts.

**Kill:** “Spatial is fixed talent” copy.  
**Survive:** Malleability → practice design, not destiny ads.

## CX.5 Causal transfer to math is real — and conditional

**FACT (meta-analysis of training→math):** Hawes, Gilligan-Lee, & Mix (2022, *Developmental Psychology, 58*(1), 112–137, doi:10.1037/dev0001281) — 29 controlled pre–post studies (N = 3,765; k = 89): spatial training improves math relative to controls (**Hedges’s g = .28**) and improves spatial thinking (**g = .49**); moderators include **age** (larger math transfer as age rises ~3–20), **concrete manipulatives** > pure computerized training in the meta, and **near vs far** transfer alignment (larger when math outcomes closer to trained spatial content). Mechanisms of transfer remain poorly understood; publication-bias checks nonsignificant in their analyses.

**FACT (commentary framing):** Hawes, Gilligan-Lee, & Mix (2022, *npj Science of Learning, 7*, article 10, doi:10.1038/s41539-022-00128-9) — spatialize curricula carefully; summarize the g ≈ .28 transfer meta; caution WEIRD samples and researcher-developed outcomes may inflate practical claims.

**FACT (positive child RCT grain):** Gilligan, Thomas, & Farran (2020, *Developmental Science, 23*(4), e12909, doi:10.1111/desc.12909; online 2019) — N = 250 eight-year-olds: training spatial scaling and mental rotation produced near, intermediate, and **far transfer to mathematics**; explicit vs implicit instruction largely similar except geometry.

**FACT (elementary grades):** Mix, Levine, Cheng, Stockton, & Bower (2021, *Journal of Educational Psychology, 113*(2), 304–314, doi:10.1037/edu0000494) — first- and sixth-graders: spatial visualization or form-perception/VSWM training beat language-arts control on overall mathematics.

**FACT (null / wound — transfer not guaranteed):** Hawes, Moss, Caswell, & Poliszczuk (2015, *Trends in Neuroscience and Education, 4*(3), 60–68, doi:10.1016/j.tine.2015.05.001) — classroom computerized mental-rotation training (6 weeks, ages 6–8) improved spatial measures but **failed far transfer** to mathematics vs literacy control. Meta-average ≠ product guarantee.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Prefer **embedded** spatial demand inside FormatId missions (co-present equation+graph, structural finger-trace — SAFE-GESTURE) over isolated rotation arcade. Expect larger transfer when math outcomes are representation-aligned than distal symbolic drill. HS ACT Maya is not a preschool spatial RCT.

**Kill:** “Our spatial mini-game raises ACT scores” without SPATIAL-* / FORMAT transfer proof.  
**Survive:** Conditional causal path spatial→math under aligned, often concrete/embedded designs.

## CX.6 Spatial anxiety — affect is part of the identity story

**FACT (scale):** Lyons, Ramirez, Maloney, Rendina, Levine, & Beilock (2018, *Journal of Numerical Cognition, 4*(3), 526–553, doi:10.5964/jnc.v4i3.154) — validated adult Spatial Anxiety Questionnaire with subscales **navigation, mental-manipulation, imagery**; higher anxiety uniquely predicts lower performance/attitudes in corresponding spatial domains for navigation and manipulation; framed as a STEM participation barrier via avoidance of spatial practice.

**FACT (gender × math anxiety mediation):** Sokolowski, Hawes, & Lyons (2019, *Cognition, 182*, 193–212, doi:10.1016/j.cognition.2018.10.005) — undergraduate sample: processes in the **spatial** domain (not math-domain ability/anxiety alone) mediated sex differences in math anxiety after controls; **spatial anxiety** was the strongest mediator vs actual/perceived spatial ability — raising the possibility that gendered math-anxiety gaps partly reflect anxiety about / avoidance of spatial strategies on math tasks.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Diagram / coordinate_graph cold-starts need SAFE-COLD humility + destake + SAFE-DUAL supports — not day-one “prove you’re visual.” Do not ship Spatial Anxiety Score™ as NS; instrument quit/freeze on high-spatial FormatIds. Densifies SAFE-GENDER: remove stereotype costume, keep representation challenge with scaffolds.

**Kill:** Flood-with-graphs as equity; Spatial Anxiety Score™ NS; “girls need easier visuals.”  
**Survive:** Spatial anxiety as a reason for destaked FormatId ladders and strategy choice (not avoidance forever).
