## CXXXIX.3 Transparency is disclosure + clarity + accuracy — not dump or fog

**FACT:** Schnackenberg and Tomlinson (2016, *Journal of Management, 42*(7), 1784–1810, doi:10.1177/0149206314525202) — organizational transparency is usefully treated as stakeholder perceptions of **information disclosure, clarity, and accuracy**; these dimensions are theorized as antecedents that shape perceived ability, benevolence, and integrity (and thus trustworthiness) in Mayer, Davis, and Schoorman’s trust model — transparency is not mere volume of data shared.

**FACT:** Mayer, Davis, and Schoorman (1995, *Academy of Management Review, 20*(3), 709–734, doi:10.5465/amr.1995.9508080335) — trust is willingness to be vulnerable based on expectations of the other’s ability, benevolence, and integrity.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):**

| Failure mode | Transparency break | Parent trust risk |
|--------------|--------------------|-------------------|
| Hide holes | Low disclosure | Integrity hit when Map later shows gaps |
| Jargon matrix dump | Low clarity | Ability/competence fog; abandon reading |
| Pad greens | Low accuracy | Integrity + ability hit when tutor contradicts |
| Empathy-only apology | Disclosure without actionable clarity | Benevolence theater; belief unchanged |

**Kill:** “Transparency” as either raw coverage CSV dump or soft apology without labeled holes.  
**Survive:** Short triad — what was probed, what was not, why the bar exists, one next step.

## CXXXIX.4 Accountability-style honesty beats secrecy — with transfer limits

**FACT:** Rawlins (2008, *Public Relations Journal, 2*(2), 1–21) — employee sample: substantial information, participation, and accountability (vs secrecy) relate positively to trust. **Transfer limit:** not a parent edtech RCT — borrow the construct, not the correlation as ACT WTP proof.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Prefer accountability (“we have not sealed enough diagram items to diagnose that format yet”) over secrecy (omit the slot) or blame (student deficit). Invite parents to witness a later sealed Practice return — not to grade every miss (SAFE-PDASH).

**Kill:** Secrecy-as-conversion. **Survive:** Accountable incompleteness + one MoC CTA.

## CXXXIX.5 Belief correction needs specific facts — not character shame

**FACT:** Bergman (2021, *Journal of Political Economy, 129*(1), 286–322, doi:10.1086/711410) — parents hold upwardly biased beliefs about child effort; biweekly information on missed assignments/grades attenuates bias and raises achievement via more accurate beliefs and lower monitoring costs.

**FACT:** Bergman and Chan (2021, *Journal of Human Resources, 56*(1), 125–158, doi:10.3368/jhr.56.1.1118-9837R1) — weekly automated alerts on misses/grades/absences cut course failures and raised attendance; **no** significant state-test lift — process honesty ≠ standardized-score miracle.

**FACT:** Rogers and Feller (2018, *Nature Human Behaviour, 2*, 335–342, doi:10.1038/s41562-018-0328-1) — cumulative personalized absence facts reduced chronic absenteeism; relative peer framing was **not** the active ingredient in their design.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** The belief object for holes is **product completeness for a named use**, not “Maya’s visual talent.” Prefer: “Diagram format: not yet diagnosed (sealed items unavailable)” over “Your child lags classmates on graphs.” Peer-rank shame around holes recreates COMPARE/PDASH kills and mis-aims Bergman-style correction at the wrong prior.

**Kill:** Classmate visual-rank as hole “truth.”  
**Survive:** Cumulative, personalized, product-honest facts + one improvement object.

## CXXXIX.6 Improvement-framed, autonomy-supportive CTAs — not nag or fear

**FACT:** Kraft and Rogers (2015, *Economics of Education Review, 47*, 49–63, doi:10.1016/j.econedurev.2015.04.001) — weekly individualized teacher→parent messages raised credit-earning; **improvement-focused** notes showed the largest point estimates vs continue-positive messages (study underpowered to fully separate arms).

**FACT:** Xu, Guo, Feng, Ma, Zhang, Núñez, and Fan (2024, *Psicothema, 36*(1), 1–14, doi:10.7334/psicothema2023.92) — meta-analysis: autonomy-supportive homework involvement helps; parental control / frequency dimensions do not deliver the same benefit pattern.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Hole CTA copy should be improvement-framed and autonomy-supportive:

- Prefer: “When sealed diagram practice is ready, Maya can run a short return — ask what felt hard; don’t solve it for her.”  
- Avoid: “Sit until every visual hole is filled tonight.”  
- Avoid: Fear scarcity (“Holes mean ACT risk — buy now”).

Pair with SAFE-SOFTMSG grain: name the FormatId hole + next sealed mission, not Miss Streak theater.

**Kill:** Controlling hole-nag; fear-WTP default.  
**Survive:** One improvement CTA; parent as witness/structure, student owns attempt.

## CXXXIX.7 Ambitious claims still need evidence — holes lower the claim, not the honesty bar

**FACT:** Kane (2013, *Journal of Educational Measurement, 50*(1), 1–73, doi:10.1111/jedm.12000) — validate interpretations and **uses**; more ambitious claims need more support.

**FACT:** Federal Trade Commission (1984). *FTC Policy Statement Regarding Advertising Substantiation* (*Thompson Medical Co.*, 104 F.T.C. 648, 839) — prior reasonable basis required for objective claims before dissemination.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** “Diagnosed sealed FormatIds X,Y; Z not probed” and “Practice has sealed diagrams; C4 does not yet” are allowed when true. “Complete visual diagnostic / all formats mapped” and “honest holes → +ACT points” are banned while holes remain (Kane ambition + FTC basis).

**Kill:** Complete-visual ads; ACT points from hole packaging. **Survive:** Ambition matched to stock.

## CXXXIX.9 Product surface — SAFE-HOLETRUST claim contract

1. Parent gap-scan / MoC surface shows **Probed** vs **Not probed** FormatIds (from SCANCOMP flags).  
2. One-sentence **seal reason** adjacent to holes (“C4 only uses verified visuals”).  
3. If Practice has sealed stock for a FormatId that C4 lacks → show **use-tier split** (“Available in Practice · not yet in diagnostic”) — never collapse to one %.  
4. Exactly **one** primary CTA: sealed Practice grain *or* “we’re verifying more [FormatId] items” with known-finite pipeline language if ETA exists (KNOWNETA discipline) — no fake-precision countdown.  
5. Copy frames the **product hole**, not the child’s character; no classmate visual ranks.  
6. Ban “complete visual diagnostic” / “all formats covered” while any blueprint hole remains for the marketed track.  
7. Telemetry: `hole_shown`, `cta_type`, `hide_attempt`, `shame_frame_flag` — demote Hole Trust Score™ / Coverage Satisfaction / Honesty Minutes.  
8. CBC arms (with WTP): labeled-honest vs hidden vs padded-complete vs shame-framed — pre-register before parent launch emails.  
9. Tutor brief: same hole language parents see — no dual script that reintroduces pad in voice.  
10. Link fill path to promote-to-C4 workflow (id 140) as the *company* response to holes — not parent homework police.

## CXXXIX.10 Doctrine — SAFE-HOLETRUST (provisional)

1. **Holes stay visible** — never hide `format_not_probed` to protect conversion.  
2. **Triad copy** — disclosure + clarity + accuracy (Schnackenberg & Tomlinson).  
3. **Belief object = product use**, not child visual deficit (Bergman-style facts without COMPARE shame).  
4. **One improvement CTA**, autonomy-supportive (Kraft/Rogers × Xu).  
5. **Use-tier labels** when Practice ≠ C4 (COVER × SPLITPOOL).  
6. **Ambition follows stock** — lower FormatId claims while holes remain (Kane × SCANCOMP).  
7. **No Hole Trust Score™ / Coverage Satisfaction / Honesty Minutes NS**.  
8. **No fear-scarcity default** / complete-visual ads / ACT guarantees from hole packaging.  
9. Copy: “We diagnosed what we can seal. Empty slots mean we have not measured that format yet — not that your student failed it.”

**Confidence:** High on transparency/trust and parent-information spines with stated transfer limits. Medium on exact CTA wording and WTP magnitudes (needs HOLETRUST-*). High that hide/pad/shame are commercially toxic once Map/tutor contradict the portal.
