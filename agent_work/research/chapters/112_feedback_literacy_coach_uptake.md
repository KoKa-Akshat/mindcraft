## CXII.1 Why this chapter exists

Edtech defaults: (1) “personalized feedback” as brand badge, (2) longer comments as quality, (3) thumbs-up as proof of learning, (4) Feedback Literacy Score™ as a KPI, (5) treating non-uptake as student deficit rather than design failure. Research spine: feedback works when receivers make sense of it and **use** it; barriers of awareness, decoding, agency, and volition are common; classic meta-analysis finds over one-third of feedback interventions *hurt* when attention is pulled to the self.

MindCraft already owns timing (SAFE-FBTIME), cycle completeness (SAFE-FA), and length discipline (SAFE-EXPLAIN). Open commercial question: does coach/tutor feedback *land*, and can we productize **uptake** without a Literacy Score™ religion?

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** Becoming a mathematical thinker includes becoming someone who *uses* diagnostic information on themselves — not someone who collects green checks and praise paragraphs.

**Claims we refuse as doctrine:** Feedback Literacy Score™ / Feedback Minutes NS; delivery≡uptake; thumbs-up≡used; longer≡more literate; illiteracy-shame without design responsibility; always-more-feedback dogma; feedback-literacy ACT guarantees.

## CXII.2 Constructs (keep literacy ≠ survey theater)

| Construct | Research meaning | MindCraft analogue | Failure mode |
|-----------|------------------|--------------------|--------------|
| **Student feedback literacy** | Understandings, capacities, dispositions to make sense of information and use it to enhance work/strategies | Soft-wrong → decode → act (retry / FormatId hop / destake) | Literacy Score™ |
| **Proactive recipience** | Agentic engagement with receiving/using feedback (not passive inbox) | Required next attempt after coach; seek soft hint before hard dump | Thumbs-up theater |
| **Appreciating feedback** | Seeing feedback as useful process, not only grades | Soft-wrong framed as map-of-gap, not verdict | Grade-only parent stalk |
| **Making judgments** | Comparing work to standards / exemplars | Success criteria + student compare chip | AI rewrite for student |
| **Managing affect** | Not collapsing into ego threat / dismiss | Destake + short task/process feedback | Praise fog / shame reel |
| **Taking action** | Doing something different next | `retry_120s` / `challenge_accept` / wired Map | Dangling essay |
| **SAFE-FBLIT** | Uptake fidelity under FEI, not badge | This chapter | Feedback Minutes NS |

**Operational definition (HYPOTHESIS):** A coach/tutor feedback event is *SAFE-FBLIT compliant* when (a) the message is decodeable at the student’s grain (concept/ingredient/FormatId — not essay jargon), (b) affect is managed (task/process/SR level; no self/praise as the payload), (c) a concrete next action is named or forced in-product, (d) the student performs that action within a short window, and (e) success is measured by FEI events — never Feedback Literacy Score™, Feedback Minutes, or thumbs-up rate.

## CXII.3 Carless & Boud — four features, action is non-optional

**FACT (definitional spine):** Carless & Boud (2018, *Assessment & Evaluation in Higher Education, 43*(8), 1315–1325, doi:10.1080/02602938.2018.1463354) — *student feedback literacy* = understandings, capacities, and dispositions to make sense of information and use it to enhance work or learning strategies. Four features: **appreciating feedback**; **making judgments**; **managing affect**; **taking action**. Peer feedback and exemplars enable literacy; teachers facilitate via design and coaching. Unless comments are taken up, feedback has minimal value.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Appreciate = soft-wrong as diagnostic gift (SAFE-ERRCLIMATE); judge = compare-to-standard chips (SAFE-FA); affect = destake + short coaches (SAFE-EXPLAIN); action = gated retry / fade / bridge hop (SAFE-HINT / SAFE-FADE). Peer enablement densifies SAFE-PEERX only when building-gated.

**Kill:** Feedback-as-delivered-product; appreciating without action.  
**Survive:** Four-feature checklist as UX/HITL fidelity, not a Literacy Score™.

## CXII.4 Sutton — knowing, being, acting (identity is in the loop)

**FACT:** Sutton (2012, *Innovations in Education and Teaching International, 49*(1), 31–40, doi:10.1080/14703297.2012.647781) — feedback literacy as academic literacies with epistemological (**knowing**), ontological (**being**), and practical (**acting**) dimensions; becoming feedback literate is complex and mediated by perceptions of teachers’ educational identities.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Feedback that attacks *who you are* collapses the ontological channel; procedure dumps without knowing/being support fail too. Tutor grain (SAFE-TUTORGRAIN) mediates uptake. Soft-wrong + competence evidence (SAFE-PROOF) supports *being* without empty “math person” praise (SAFE-IDMEASURE ban).

**Kill:** Ability-shame feedback; identity-colonization praise≡literacy.  
**Survive:** Knowing/being/acting as a three-check for coach copy and tutor briefs.

## CXII.5 Molloy, Boud & Henderson — learner-centred competency grain

**FACT (empirical framework):** Molloy, Boud, & Henderson (2020, *Assessment & Evaluation in Higher Education, 45*(4), 527–540, doi:10.1080/02602938.2019.1667955) — secondary analysis of large two-university student feedback data; seven groupings / 31 characteristics of learner feedback literacy (e.g., understanding purposes/roles, seeking information, making judgments, working with emotions, processing/using information for future work). Positions students as active agents; framework for diagnosing interventions and aligning course designs.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Use Molloy et al. as a **design checklist**, not a report-card. Prefer event proxies (seek soft hint; compare chip; affect-regulated retry; enact next mission) over Likert literacy splash screens.

**Kill:** Feedback Literacy Score™ from surveys; competency theater without enact.  
**Survive:** Seeking + judging + emotion work + enact as instrumentable behaviors.

## CXII.6 Winstone et al. — proactive recipience and barriers

**FACT (systematic review):** Winstone, Nash, Parker, & Rowntree (2017, *Educational Psychologist, 52*(1), 17–37, doi:10.1080/00461520.2016.1207538) — review of 195 outputs on learners’ agentic engagement with feedback; introduce **proactive recipience**; taxonomy of recipience processes; interventions that support shared responsibility for making feedback effective.

**FACT (barriers / focus groups):** Winstone, Nash, Rowntree, & Parker (2017, *Studies in Higher Education, 42*(11), 2026–2041, doi:10.1080/03075079.2015.1130032) — students often value feedback yet do not use it (“it’d be useful, but I wouldn’t use it”); barriers from decoding terminology to unwillingness to expend effort; four psychological processes: **awareness**, **cognisance**, **agency**, **volition**. Removing barriers typically requires shared teacher–student responsibility; train proactive receivers.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Map awareness→cognisance→agency→volition: feedback *findable*, *decodeable* (plain + FormatId), *actionable* (one next move), *worth the effort* (short retry path). Shared responsibility = product + tutor coaching, not “fix the illiterate student.”

**Kill:** Passive inbox feedback; jargon dumps; effort-tax essays without action affordances.  
**Survive:** Proactive recipience as FEI-linked UX; barrier taxonomy for HITL QA.

## CXII.7 Kluger & DeNisi + Hattie/Timperley — when feedback hurts uptake

**FACT (meta + FIT):** Kluger & DeNisi (1996, *Psychological Bulletin, 119*(2), 254–284, doi:10.1037/0033-2909.119.2.254) — feedback interventions improve performance on average (d ≈ .41) but **over one-third decrease** performance; Feedback Intervention Theory: effectiveness falls as attention moves up the hierarchy toward the **self** and away from the task.

**FACT:** Hattie & Timperley (2007, *Review of Educational Research, 77*(1), 81–112, doi:10.3102/003465430298487) — powerful but highly variable; where going / how going / where next; task/process/self-regulation levels beat self/praise.

**FACT (updated meta):** Wisniewski, Zierer, & Hattie (2020, *Frontiers in Psychology, 10*, 3087, doi:10.3389/fpsyg.2019.03087) — overall d ≈ 0.48 with large heterogeneity; information content matters.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Literacy without *level discipline* is cosplay. Ego toasts and shame reels both pull attention to self → FIT wound. Densify SAFE-FBTIME / SAFE-EXPLAIN: short where-next, then force action (Carless & Boud’s fourth feature).

**Kill:** Praise-as-literacy; shame-as-motivation; longer≡better.  
**Survive:** Task/process/SR feedback that schedules uptake.

## CXII.8 Soft-wrong product law — literacy is an uptake loop

**HYPOTHESIS (product law):** After soft-wrong (or tutor comment), the default stack is:

1. **Decode (≤1 glance):** Name the gap at concept/ingredient/FormatId grain.  
2. **Affect gate:** Destake language; no ability verdict.  
3. **One next move:** Retry, soft hint, fade completion, or Map hop — not a menu bomb.  
4. **Action forced:** UI does not advance as “done” until attempt or explicit defer with scheduled return.  
5. **Instrument:** `feedback_uptake_act` + FEI events; thumbs-up is vanity only.

**Banned patterns:**
1. Unlimited thorough AI essays after one miss (SAFE-EXPLAIN).  
2. Thumbs-up without retry.  
3. Parent live stalk of every soft-wrong comment (SAFE-PDASH).  
4. Feedback Literacy Score™ / Feedback Minutes NS.  
5. “You dismissed feedback” shame without fixing jargon/length/action affordances.

**Kill:** Delivery≡uptake; helpful-click NS.  
**Survive:** Uptake loop as the unit of coach quality.

## CXII.12 So what for MindCraft commercially

- **Copy:** “We don’t grade your feedback literacy — we make the next attempt impossible to skip after you see what went wrong.” Never Feedback Literacy Score™, Feedback Minutes, or “our AI writes thorough comments” as the hero.  
- **Product:** Soft-wrong and tutor comments encode decode → affect gate → one next move → forced action; instrument uptake events; ban thumbs-as-learning.  
- **Positioning:** Against monologue AI and helpful-click NPS; for inspectable recipience under FEI.  
- **Metric:** `feedback_uptake_act` + FEI — demote Feedback Minutes, Literacy Score™, thumbs-up rate.  
- **Kill list:** Feedback Literacy Score™; Feedback Minutes NS; delivery≡uptake; thumbs-up≡used; longer≡more literate; illiteracy-shame without design fix; always-more-feedback dogma; feedback-literacy ACT guarantees.

## CXII.13 Doctrine — SAFE-FBLIT (provisional)

1. **Literacy = sense + use** — Carless & Boud four features; action is non-optional.  
2. **Knowing / being / acting** — Sutton dimensions; no ability-shame; no empty identity praise.  
3. **Proactive recipience** — design for awareness, cognisance, agency, volition (Winstone et al.).  
4. **Shared responsibility** — barriers are product+tutor failures first, not student moral defects.  
5. **Level discipline** — task/process/SR; ban self-focus payloads that FIT predicts can hurt (Kluger & DeNisi; Hattie & Timperley).  
6. **Uptake loop UX** — decode → affect gate → one move → forced action → FEI.  
7. **Competency as events** — Molloy checklist as design/HITL QA, never Literacy Score™ splash.  
8. **Densify, don’t fork** — FBTIME/FA/EXPLAIN/HINT/HELP/SRL/MONITOR/ERRCLIMATE.  
9. **No Feedback Literacy Score™ / Feedback Minutes NS / delivery≡uptake / thumbs≡used / longer≡literate / illiteracy-shame / always-more-feedback / feedback-literacy ACT guarantees**.

**Confidence:** High that feedback-without-uptake is commercially hollow, and that self-focused / thumbs / length theater are unsafe. Medium that the uptake loop beats essay+thumbs on delayed FEI — instrument FBLIT-* before literacy marketing. High confidence to kill Feedback Literacy Score™, delivery≡uptake ads, and Feedback Minutes NS regardless.
