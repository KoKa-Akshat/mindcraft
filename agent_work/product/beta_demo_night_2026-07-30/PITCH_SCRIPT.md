## Script (~4 minutes)

### 1. Open

Hi. I am Akshat, co-founder of MindCraft.

**MindCraft helps high school students who think they are bad at math by mapping the real gap. Then a college tutor starts there, not page one.**

### 2. Problem

Here is the hour that keeps failing.

A junior says they are not a math person. A parent pays for tutoring. A college tutor spends forty minutes playing detective. By the time they find the real break, the session is over. Next week they start over.

The student was not lazy. The story lost them years ago. Nobody drew the map.

### 3. Insight

After ChatGPT, **answers are free**. What stays scarce is diagnosis, a human who starts on that spot, and a reason to stay with hard work.

One more thing from how buyers actually work: **the student uses MindCraft. The parent buys it.** We design for the student. We sell clarity to the parent.

Most startups die because they never find a market. We picked a narrow one on purpose: Twin Cities ACT Math families.

### 4. Solution

Three moves. The critical few.

1. **Gap scan.** Confidence across ACT concepts. Chart the fog.  
2. **Living map.** Weak spot lights up.  
3. **College tutor.** Starts on the gap. Never page one again.

### 5-7. Product

*[Point. Do not narrate every pixel.]*

Gap scan: our assessment before session one.  
Map: warm-ups, algebra, geometry. Today's work has a reason.  
Tutor: Macalester, St. Olaf, St. Thomas, UNC. Already briefed.

Under the hood: **42 concepts**, **about 1,500 real exam questions**, **about 1,700 named misconceptions**. We diagnose mechanisms, not vibes.

### 8. Why us

Akshat: Macalester, eight years helping students find the first step that finally makes sense.  
Blake: product and data.  
Abhigya: applied math research.

Product is live. Families can apply today.

### 9. Ask

If tonight's form asks who we should talk to, **yes**:

1. A parent with a 10th to 12th grader staring at ACT Math  
2. A counselor who hears “I am not a math person” every week  
3. A college tutor who wants hours that are not detective work  

**joinmindcraft@gmail.com**. Happy to take two questions.
