# Part CXVII — Worked-Example Fading Under High Math Anxiety

**Primary question:** How should MindCraft fade worked examples for high-math-anxiety (HMA) learners without trapping them in permanent full-solution “support,” flooding them into solo struggle, or inventing Anxiety Score™ / Calm Score™ fade gates?  
**Owners:** Affect / Anxiety · Product (Solver / Practice fade ladder) · HITL tutors · Brand · Red Team  
**Commercial job:** Ship **SAFE-FADEX** densifying SAFE-FADE × SAFE-EXPOSE × SAFE-DD: **examples protect WM under anxiety load; fade is still the identity move; hold longer at E0/E1 under destaked stakes, then approach fade as expectancy violation** — never permanent never-fade anxiety track, never day-one aggressive fade-as-rigor, never Calm Score™ unlocks.

**Builds on:** Parts LXXXVIII (SAFE-FADE), LVIII (SAFE-EXPOSE), XLI (SAFE-DD), XXIV (affect), L (SAFE-CALIB), C (SAFE-EXPTIME), XCI (SAFE-HINT), CIV (SAFE-HWHELP). Product seams: Solver E0→E3 ladder, soft-wrong / destake, hint binge as safety behavior, Map grain, parent “she needs every step forever” messaging.

## CXVII.1 Why this chapter exists

SAFE-FADE already kills always-full-worked and never-fade costumes. SAFE-EXPOSE already kills day-one flooding and permanent safety behaviors. The remaining commercial hazard is the **intersection**: HMA Maya’s WM is already taxed (Ashcraft), so full worked examples help her learn (Mesghina et al.), *and* those same full dumps become the safety behavior that blocks inhibitory learning and solo identity. Competitors resolve the tension wrongly: ChatGPT/Photomath-style apps keep the dump forever (“we support anxiety”); struggle-theater apps yank scaffolding on day one (“rigor”); therapy-cosplay apps wait for Calm Score™ before hard steps.

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** For HMA learners, worked examples are *equipment*, not therapy — and fading is *graded approach to carrying the join*, not abandonment. Permanent full examples are commercially identical to ChatGPT; permanent easy-track “for anxious kids” is soft standards dressed as care.

**Claims we refuse as doctrine:**
1. Always-full-worked forever for HMA / “anxious track.”  
2. Aggressive E0→E3 jump as day-one rigor for threatened learners.  
3. Anxiety Score™ / Calm Score™ as fade gate or North Star.  
4. Permanent hint binge / solution peek labeled “support.”  
5. Never-fade scaffolding marketed as empathy.  
6. Fade Score™ / Anxiety-adjusted Fade % vanity NS.  
7. Softened standards / forever E0 for matched anxiety personas.  
8. ACT-point guarantees from “anxiety-aware scaffolding.”

## CXVII.2 Constructs (protect ≠ trap; fade ≠ flood)

| Construct | Meaning | MindCraft analogue | Failure mode |
|-----------|---------|--------------------|--------------|
| **Anxiety WM load** | Worry consumes resources needed for multi-step math | Freeze, peek, rapid exit | Read as laziness / “needs more struggle” |
| **Protective example** | Full/near-full worked study that frees WM for schema | E0 / early E1 learn rail | Costume forever |
| **Safety behavior** | Escape that prevents expectancy violation | Instant full reveal; hint binge | “Support” that blocks learning |
| **Anxiety-aware fade** | Hold longer at high guidance; still step down under destake | Slower E0→E1→E2; prove rail near E3 | Forever track OR flood |
| **SAFE-FADEX** | FADE × EXPOSE doctrine for HMA | This chapter | Calm Score™ theater |

**Operational definition (HYPOTHESIS):** A fade path is *SAFE-FADEX complete* when it (a) offers **protective worked study** early when anxiety load + low schema would make unguided search undesirable difficulty (SAFE-DD), (b) **destakes** evaluation climate (soft-wrong; no streak-as-worth), (c) **holds** at E0/E1 longer than for low-anxiety peers *without* locking a permanent persona track, (d) treats consecutive full peeks as **safety behaviors to fade** (SAFE-EXPOSE / SAFE-HINT), (e) advances fade as **approach + expectancy check** (“I can finish the last join”), (f) proves with `solo_transfer_pass` / delayed mix — not calm meters or solution views, and (g) never claims to treat clinical anxiety disorders.

## CXVII.3 Anxiety taxes the same WM that search needs

**FACT (WM disruption):** Ashcraft & Kirk (2001, *Journal of Experimental Psychology: General, 130*(2), 224–237, doi:10.1037/0096-3445.130.2.224) — high math-anxiety individuals show reduced working-memory spans under math demand; anxiety functions as a **transitory dual-task load**, amplifying errors especially on carry/complex items.

**FACT (education bridge + avoidance):** Ashcraft & Krause (2007, *Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, 14*(2), 243–248, doi:10.3758/BF03194059) — math beyond simple retrieval depends on WM; high math anxiety drains resources via fear preoccupation; pattern includes avoidance of elective math/careers. Hembree (1990, *JRME, 21*(1), 33–46, doi:10.5951/jresematheduc.21.1.0033) — MA binds to **avoidance**; relief treatments tend to accompany better performance.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** For HMA Maya, unguided search on a new schema is often **undesirable difficulty** (SAFE-DD): freeze + peek + “I’m not a math person.” Protective examples are load management for germane processing — not softness.

**Kill:** “Struggle first always” for anxious learners.  
**Survive:** Example-first acquisition when anxiety load + low prior knowledge would saturate WM.

## CXVII.4 Worked examples can blunt anxiety’s learning tax

**FACT (direct moderation):** Mesghina, Vollman, Trezise, & Richland (2023, *Journal of Educational Psychology*, doi:10.1037/edu0000795) — 280 fifth-graders, demanding ratio lesson; half reviewed worked examples at key solve moments. **Math anxiety** (not pandemic worries) hurt procedural/conceptual learning, raised test mind-wandering, lowered interest; **worked examples mitigated those hits on learning and engagement**.

**Applied (FOUNDER BELIEF → testable):** Learn-rail E0/E1 is commercially *required* for HMA acquisition — not optional. Withholding examples to look rigorous sells threat; never leaving examples sells ChatGPT.

**Kill:** No-example / discovery-only brand for threatened novices.  
**Survive:** Protective WE on high-load bridge lessons; measure peek/exit reduction *without* forever-E0.

**Wound:** Mesghina tested WE *presence*, not multi-session fade pacing — FADEX-* must test hold-time.

## CXVII.5 Fading still works — and prior knowledge, not WM score, often gates it

**FACT (smooth fade):** Renkl, Atkinson, Maier, & Staley (2002, *Journal of Experimental Education, 70*(4), 293–315, doi:10.1080/00220970209599510) — successive incomplete examples (complete → increasingly incomplete → to-be-solved) beat static example–problem pairs on near transfer; errors during learning mediated; **backward fading** often preferred.

**FACT (CLT transition):** Renkl & Atkinson (2003, *Educational Psychologist, 38*(1), 15–22, doi:10.1207/S15326985EP3801_3) — as intrinsic load falls with schema growth, problem-solving demands can rise without overload; fading structures that transition.

**FACT (SE + fade):** Atkinson, Renkl, & Merrill (2003, *Journal of Educational Psychology, 95*(4), 774–783, doi:10.1037/0022-0663.95.4.774) — fading + principle SE prompts → medium-to-large near/far transfer without extra time.

**FACT (WM moderation null; prior knowledge matters):** Miller-Cotto et al. (2025/2026, *British Journal of Educational Psychology, 96*(1), 108–126, doi:10.1111/bjep.12781; PMC12879535) — sixth-graders, ASSISTments geometry: **fading** largest pre→post gains vs solve-only; **WM did not significantly moderate** fading; **prior knowledge** did.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Do **not** invent WM Score™ / Anxiety Score™ fade destiny. Gate on **diagnosed grain / prior success** with **destake + longer hold** under threat — not a permanent anxiety persona. Mesghina still supports *temporary* WE under anxiety arousal.

**Kill:** Anxiety Score™ / WM Score™ as fade destiny; forever-E0 “support track.”  
**Survive:** Evidence-state fade (SAFE-EXPTIME / SAFE-FADE) + anxiety-aware *pacing*, not a soft curriculum.

## CXVII.6 Incorrect examples and soft-wrong — protect without answer dump

**FACT (incorrect WE for low prior knowledge):** Barbieri & Booth (2016, *Learning and Individual Differences, 48*, 36–44, doi:10.1016/j.lindif.2016.04.001) — algebra; **low prior knowledge** students gained most from highlighted incorrect examples vs solve-only or correct-example controls.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** For HMA + low schema, **destaked error study** (soft-wrong; explain-the-error) builds features without unaided flood *or* ChatGPT dump. Mid-ladder: “what went wrong on this step?”

**Kill:** Shame-reel mistakes; timed ACT error flood on day one.  
**Survive:** Incorrect-example / soft-wrong when full solo is still threat.

---

## CXVII.7 Safety behaviors: permanent examples are exposure’s enemy

**FACT (inhibitory learning):** Craske, Treanor, Conway, Zbozinek, & Vervliet (2014, *Behaviour Research and Therapy, 58*, 10–23, doi:10.1016/j.brat.2014.04.006) — exposure optimized via expectancy violation and removal of safety signals; success is new inhibitory learning, **not** within-session fear reduction to zero.

**Applied (reuse SAFE-EXPOSE):** Instant full Solver dump, parent rescue, and streak-protect peeks are **safety behaviors**. For HMA product design, protective E0 is allowed *as a stage*; the commercial sin is making E0 the forever brand. Fade-up after equipped success is the expectancy violation: “I feared blanking on the last join — I carried it under recoverable stakes.”

**Kill:** Habituation theater (“wait until calm, then fade”); permanent peek entitlement.  
**Survive:** Name feared outcome → protective study → completion → solo under destake → vary context/format.

## CXVII.9 Doctrine — SAFE-FADEX (provisional)

1. **Protect then fade** — for HMA + low schema, full/near-full examples are acquisition equipment (Mesghina; Ashcraft load), not the brand forever.  
2. **Destake before demand** — soft-wrong / mastery climate so fade is not threat (SAFE-DD × SAFE-EXPOSE).  
3. **Hold longer, don’t soft-track** — longer E0/E1 dwell vs low-anxiety peers; no permanent “anxious curriculum.”  
4. **Gate on grain, not meters** — prior success / Map evidence / recent solos; no Anxiety Score™ / Calm Score™ fade unlock.  
5. **Treat peek binge as safety behavior** — fade the escape, don’t monetize it (SAFE-HINT / SAFE-HELP).  
6. **Backward completion first** — blank final joins before full solo (SAFE-FADE); optional incorrect-example mid-rungs for low prior knowledge (Barbieri & Booth).  
7. **Proof = solo transfer + approach** — `solo_transfer_pass` / delayed mix / `avoided_format_approach`; not calm-to-zero or solution views.  
8. **Copy:** “We shield the first steps when worry loads the mind — then we fade as you prove the join.” Never forever-every-step; never abandon-to-prove-grit; never clinical cure claims.

**Confidence:** High — MA taxes WM / fuels avoidance (Ashcraft; Hembree); WE can blunt MA’s learning hit (Mesghina); fading beats static pairs (Renkl/Atkinson). Medium — WM is not the product fade gate (Miller-Cotto null); longer E0/E1 hold for HMA beats aggressive fade (needs FADEX-*). High — forever-E0 “anxiety support” ≡ ChatGPT; Calm/Anxiety Score™ gates ≡ therapy cosplay.

- **Copy:** “We shield early steps when worry loads the mind — then we fade as you prove the join.” Lead with protect→fade, not forever solutions or grit flooding.
