## CLVII.1 Why this chapter exists

PATHBLAME killed unconditioned dual-vs-single shame boards for *ops*. PROCURE said district revenue is gated by a **trust packet** (DPA + data map + deletion/breach + no-biometric / no-ad use) — not Bloom theater. HOLETRUST said parents get labeled incompleteness, not fake greens. The missing join is **externalization**: what a privacy officer / curriculum director / procurement lead sees when they ask “how do you know your diagnostic Map is trustworthy?”

Wrong resolutions already visible:

1. **Hide path mix** — packet lists FERPA/NDPA only; QA is “proprietary AI review” fog.  
2. **Dual Shame as proof** — LEA slide shows dual repair % redder than single as “science” (Berkeley aggregate error, Part CLII).  
3. **Hospital case-mix cosplay** — “we risk-adjust like CMS report cards” as ACT / Map readiness ads (Iezzoni wound below).  
4. **Propensity Theater™ brand** — causal-DAG splash in RFP responses without stratum tables.  
5. **Parent honesty / LEA secrecy split** — HOLETRUST for families; silence for districts when dual volume spikes under exam crunch.  
6. **Complete-visual / Dual-protects-Map guarantees** from mix packaging alone.

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** Serious LEAs prefer a one-page mix chart that says “harder diagram tickets get a second seal; within that band, here’s how we watch repair” more than a Dual Shame Score™ or a FERPA badge with no QA appendix — *if* the chart is readable without biostat costume.

**Claims we refuse:** Dual Shame Score™ / Case-Mix Score™ / Path Mix Minutes / Selection-Adjusted Repair % as NS or packet hero metrics; unconditioned dual-vs-single rankings in RFPs; hospital report-card cosplay ACT ads; hide-mix-for-conversion; complete-visual or Map-trust guarantees from mix packaging alone.

## CLVII.3 Transparency for buyers is still disclosure + clarity + accuracy

**FACT:** Schnackenberg and Tomlinson (2016, *Journal of Management, 42*(7), 1784–1810, doi:10.1177/0149206314525202) — organizational transparency is usefully treated as stakeholder perceptions of **information disclosure, clarity, and accuracy**; dimensions shape perceived ability, benevolence, and integrity in Mayer et al.’s trust model — transparency ≠ data dump volume.

**FACT:** Schnackenberg, Tomlinson, and Coen (2021, *Human Relations, 74*(10–11) / construct-validation line, doi:10.1177/0018726720933317) — measure transparency as perceived disclosure, clarity, and accuracy; the aggregate predicts perceived trustworthiness (ability, benevolence, integrity).

**FACT:** Mayer, Davis, and Schoorman (1995, *Academy of Management Review, 20*(3), 709–734, doi:10.5465/amr.1995.9508080335) — trust = willingness to be vulnerable based on ability, benevolence, integrity expectations.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):**

| Failure mode | Transparency break | LEA trust risk |
|--------------|--------------------|----------------|
| Hide path mix | Low disclosure | Integrity hit when dual volume / FILLETA bands contradict “always dual” sales talk |
| Biostat dump / propensity appendix | Low clarity | Ability fog; counsel stalls; teacher-champion abandoned |
| Dual Shame as quality proof | Low accuracy | Integrity + ability hit when stratified tables reverse the story |
| Empathy-only “we care about QA” | Disclosure without actionable clarity | Benevolence theater; no inspectable process |

**Kill:** “Transparency” as either raw repair CSV dump or soft privacy essay without mix.  
**Survive:** Short triad — path shares, why dual is larger in hard cells, what is *not* claimed yet.

## CLVII.4 Accountability-style incompleteness beats secrecy — transfer limits apply

**FACT:** Rawlins (2008, *Public Relations Journal, 2*(2), 1–21) — substantial information, participation, and accountability (vs secrecy) relate positively to trust in an employee sample. **Transfer limit:** not an LEA procurement RCT — borrow the construct, not the correlation as district WTP proof.

**Reuse (SAFE-HOLETRUST / SAFE-PROCURE):** Prefer accountability (“second seals concentrate on harder diagram tickets; we do not claim dual is proven superior from raw path bars”) over secrecy or Dual Shame Score™.

**Kill:** Secrecy-as-conversion in district packets. **Survive:** Accountable routing + optional conditioned table.

## CLVII.5 Aggregation still lies — LEA slides must not revive Dual Shame

**FACT:** Bickel, Hammel, and O’Connell (1975, *Science, 187*(4175), 398–404, doi:10.1126/science.187.4175.398) — Berkeley graduate admissions: aggregate rates suggested anti-woman bias; department-stratified rates reversed the story because of **application mix** across harder/easier departments.

**Reuse (SAFE-PATHBLAME):** Dual may show higher raw repair because it **applies into harder strata**. An LEA slide ranking paths on raw repair is the same Berkeley-aggregate error as procurement theater.

**Kill:** Dual Shame boards in RFP decks. **Survive:** Mix chart + within-stratum optional drill-down. **Wound:** Borrow *stratify before claim*, not Title IX cosplay.

## CLVII.6 Risk-adjustment method choice changes the story — do not cosplay hospital report cards

**FACT:** Iezzoni (1997, *JAMA, 278*(19), 1600–1607, doi:10.1001/jama.278.19.1600) — different severity / risk-adjustment methods frequently disagree about which hospitals look like mortality outliers; severity-adjusted mortality alone is **unlikely to isolate quality** differences; method choice changes impressions of relative performance.

**FACT:** Iezzoni, Ash, Shwartz, Daley, Hughes, and Mackiernan (1995, *Annals of Internal Medicine, 123*(10), 763–770, doi:10.7326/0003-4819-123-10-199511150-00004) — pairs of severity measures assigned very different severity levels to >20% of AMI patients; evaluations of outcomes must be sensitive to the severity measure used.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** MindCraft must **not** brand “case-mix adjusted dual quality” or “CMS-style report cards for tutoring QA” as district readiness. The lesson borrowed is **negative**: adjusted rankings are method-sensitive and easy to overclaim. Prefer transparent **promote-time strata** (DUALRATE triggers) and plain mix shares over black-box “risk-adjusted Dual Efficacy” scores. If ops later use matching/propensity aides (Austin 2011 reuse in CLII), keep them **internal** until IUA backing exists — never Case-Mix Score™ splash.

**Kill:** Hospital report-card / case-mix cosplay ACT or Map-trust ads. **Survive:** Named strata + dated mix. **Wound:** Clinical mortality ≠ FormatId seal QA — analogy only.
