## CXIII.1 Why this chapter exists

Edtech defaults: Discord rooms as “community learning,” one-grade group worksheets, Collaboration Score™ / Group Minutes KPIs, togetherness-as-identity without solo proof, and free-riding framed as character failure. Research spine: cooperative/CSCL methods often beat traditional baselines *when structured*; group-to-individual transfer is real when discussion is structured; but **group scores are not valid individual competence** — high group scores can hide copying, being told, or free-riding.

MindCraft’s North Star already privileges `solo_transfer_pass`. Open commercial question: can collab *serve* solo FEI, or will “learn together” SKUs wash it out?

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** Mathematical identity is personal competence under challenge — collaboration is a scaffold that must leave the student able to stand alone.

**Claims we refuse as doctrine:** Collaboration Score™ / Group Minutes NS; Discord≡CSCL learning; group-correct≡ready; free-rider-blind unlocks; always-collab dogma; collab ACT guarantees.

## CXIII.2 Constructs (keep group production ≠ individual mastery)

| Construct | Research meaning | MindCraft analogue | Failure mode |
|-----------|------------------|--------------------|--------------|
| **Cooperative / collaborative PS** | Structured peer work with interdependence + accountability | Optional pair/classroom missions with roles + solo gate | Open chat room cosplay |
| **CSCL** | Computer-supported collab with tech+pedagogy moderators | Scripted co-solve in-app (not raw Discord) | Tool-presence≡science |
| **Individual accountability** | Each member assessed / responsible for learning | Solo FEI item after group phase | One shared green check |
| **Group-to-individual transfer** | Learning in group shows up on later individual tests | Delayed `solo_transfer_pass` after collab | End on group board |
| **Free-riding / social loafing** | Credit without contribution when effort dispensable | Detect silent / copy / “tell me the answer” | Character-shame ads |
| **SAFE-COLLAB** | Collab that densifies PEERX then proves solo | This chapter | Collaboration Score™ |

**Operational definition (HYPOTHESIS):** A MindCraft path is *SAFE-COLLAB compliant* when (a) collab is optional or classroom-scoped, never the only path to progress, (b) structure includes positive interdependence *and* individual accountability (roles, scripts, or individual checks — not vibes), (c) help given/received follows PEERX building + apply rules, (d) every collab episode ends with a **solo** attempt on a near/far transfer item before unlock, (e) marketing never equates room presence or group correctness with FEI identity, and (f) free-riding is treated as a product design failure (missing accountability), not a grit slogan.

## CXIII.3 CSCL and cooperative math — positive average effects, not a blank check

**FACT (STEM CSCL meta):** Jeong, Hmelo-Silver, & Jo (2019, *Educational Research Review, 28*, 100284, doi:10.1016/j.edurev.2019.100284) — 316 outcomes from 143 studies (2005–2014); overall STEM CSCL effect size ≈ **0.51** (moderate); largest on **process** outcomes, then knowledge, then affective; moderated by technology type, pedagogy, educational level, and domain — interactions matter; “CSCL works” is incomplete without those moderators.

**FACT (CSCL elements meta):** Chen, Wang, Kirschner, & Tsai (2018, *Review of Educational Research, 88*(6), 799–843, doi:10.3102/0034654318791584) — collaboration per se aids knowledge gain (ES ≈ 0.42), skill acquisition (≈ 0.64), perceptions (≈ 0.38) in computer-based conditions; computer use in collab contexts also positive; extra tools/environments and supporting strategies add further knowledge-gain effects (≈ 0.55 / ≈ 0.38). Collaboration is not magic — scaffolding and design matter.

**FACT (math cooperative meta):** Capar & Tarim (2015, *Educational Sciences: Theory & Practice, 15*(2), 553–559, doi:10.12738/estp.2015.2.2098) — 26 studies / 36 comparisons (1988–2010); cooperative learning vs traditional on math achievement **d++ ≈ 0.59** (medium); attitudes **d++ ≈ 0.16** (small).

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Ship *structured* collab for classrooms/optional peers — average metas do **not** license Discord-as-CSCL or group-correct≡ready. Moderators are the product; presence is not.

**Kill:** “Collaboration always beats solo” / room-presence≡CSCL.  
**Survive:** Structured collab as a *conditional* channel under FEI gates.

## CXIII.4 Slavin’s hinge — group goals + individual accountability

**FACT (best-evidence / process programs):** Slavin & Lake (and related Slavin line; see Slavin’s elementary math best-evidence synthesis, ERIC ED527642) — cooperative learning raises learning when groups have a **common goal they can achieve only if all members do well on independent assessments**; teammates know their success depends partly on others’ learning (see also Johnson & Johnson; Rohrbeck et al.; Webb & Palincsar reviews cited therein). Unstructured “work together” without that hinge is a different animal.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Any collab SKU must wire **individual accountability** — solo transfer, Map update, or contribution check — not one shared green. Densifies SAFE-PROOF / SAFE-INSTRUMENT: North Star remains solo evidence.

**Kill:** Shared worksheet unlock; team XP without solo proof.  
**Survive:** Group goal + independent assessment as design law.

---

## CXIII.5 Group-to-individual transfer is real — when discussion is structured

**FACT (experimental):** Yager, Johnson, & Johnson (1985, *Journal of Educational Psychology, 77*(1), 60–66, doi:10.1037/0022-0663.77.1.60) — second-graders; structured oral discussion (summarize + monitor others’ summaries) vs unstructured cooperative vs individualistic; cooperative conditions beat individualistic on daily accuracy; **structured** cooperative students scored higher on *individual* posttests and retention than unstructured cooperative and individualistic — evidence that **group-to-individual transfer** occurs and that structured summarization/monitoring contributes.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Force structured building moves (inferential explain, monitor peer summary) — densifies SAFE-PEERX — then measure transfer on a **solo** item. Unstructured chat tracks the weaker arm.

**Kill:** Open voice lobby as “transfer science.”  
**Survive:** Structured discussion → solo check as the transfer unit.

---

## CXIII.6 Webb — group scores ≠ individual competence

**FACT (assessment validity):** Webb (1993, *Educational Assessment, 1*(2), 131–152, doi:10.1207/s15326977ea0102_3; CRESST Tech Report 352) — group math performance was **much higher** than individual; without process data, group scores cannot distinguish solvers, learners-via-collab, and copy/told students — **all can score high in group**; do not infer individual competence from group scores.

**FACT (process → learning):** Webb, Troper, & Fall (1995, *Journal of Educational Psychology, 87*(3), 406–423, doi:10.1037/0022-0663.87.3.406) — **constructive activity** (using received explanations to solve) predicts learning beyond merely being told (SAFE-PEERX).

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Never report group board correctness as mastery. After collab help, require apply-on-this-item *and* delayed solo mix. Parent dashboards must not stalk group greens as proof (SAFE-PDASH).

**Kill:** Group-correct≡ready; room leaderboard as competence.  
**Survive:** Process-aware collab + solo FEI as the only mastery claim surface.

## CXIII.7 Free-riding — design failure, not grit sermon

**FACT (motivation loss):** Kerr & Bruun (1983, *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 44*(1), 78–94, doi:10.1037/0022-3514.44.1.78) — free-rider effects when member effort is perceived as dispensable; group motivation losses are predictable under certain task structures.

**SPECULATION (product):** Unstructured Discord rooms and shared unlocks maximize perceived dispensability → free-riding + copy culture; MindCraft will recreate Webb’s invalid-inference problem at scale.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Treat free-riding as missing individual accountability / role scripts / solo gates — fix design. Do not ship “no free riders — show grit” shame copy (conflicts SAFE-STRUCTURE / SAFE-ERRCLIMATE).

**Kill:** Free-rider moralizing; silent-presence≡participation.  
**Survive:** Accountability architecture as anti-loafing.

## CXIII.8 Product law — collab as scaffold, solo FEI as proof

**HYPOTHESIS (product law):** Default MindCraft practice remains **solo FEI**. Optional collab (classroom or consented peer) follows:

1. **Scope:** Classroom / invited peer — not anonymous public lobby as learning core.  
2. **Script:** Roles or inferential explain/monitor prompts (Yager structure; PEERX building).  
3. **Apply:** Receiver uses help on *this* item (Webb constructive activity).  
4. **Solo gate:** Near-item then delayed mix — unlock requires `solo_transfer_pass` (or equivalent), never group board alone.  
5. **Instrument:** `collab_episode` + `solo_after_collab` + FEI events; demote Group Minutes / Collaboration Score™ / Discord DAU.

**Banned patterns:**
1. Discord≡CSCL / “community of practice” without enterprise+repertoire (SAFE-CoP).  
2. Shared green unlock without solo.  
3. Collaboration Score™ / Group Minutes NS.  
4. Always-collab brand that demotes solo struggle as antisocial.  
5. Collab ACT-point guarantees.  
6. Parent stalk of peer chat as competence.

**Kill:** Togetherness theater; group≡individual mastery.  
**Survive:** Scaffolded collab → solo proof loop.

## CXIII.13 Doctrine — SAFE-COLLAB (provisional)

1. **Collab is conditional** — meta-gains exist; moderators (scripts, scaffolds, pedagogy) are the product (Jeong et al.; Chen et al.; Capar & Tarim).  
2. **Group goals + individual accountability** — Slavin hinge; no shared unlock without personal proof.  
3. **Structure transfer** — Yager-style summarize/monitor (or PEERX building) before claiming group-to-individual transfer.  
4. **Group ≠ individual competence** — Webb assessment law; never sell group greens as mastery.  
5. **Constructive use** — apply help on this item (Webb et al.); densify PEERX/HELP.  
6. **Anti-free-ride by design** — Kerr & Bruun; fix dispensability; no grit sermons.  
7. **Solo FEI gate** — every collab episode ends in solo proof; instrument `solo_after_collab`.  
8. **Densify, don’t fork** — PEERX / CoP / PROOF / HELP / INSTRUMENT / ERRCLIMATE.  
9. **No Collaboration Score™ / Group Minutes NS / Discord≡learning / group-correct≡ready / free-rider-blind unlocks / always-collab dogma / collab ACT guarantees**.

**Confidence:** High that unstructured group scoring and Discord-presence theater are commercially unsafe relative to FEI. Medium that scripted collab + solo gate beats solo-only on delayed transfer for some populations — instrument COLLAB-* before collab marketing. High confidence to kill Collaboration Score™, group-correct≡ready, and Discord≡CSCL ads regardless.
