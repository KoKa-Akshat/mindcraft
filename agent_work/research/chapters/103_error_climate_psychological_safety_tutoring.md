## CIII.1 Why this chapter exists

Brand temptation: “We’re a psychologically safe space for math.” Competitor temptation: public leaderboards, red-X theater, or Bermuda-triangle redirection that abandons the student who erred. Organizational literature (Edmondson) made “psychological safety” a buzzword; classroom literature (Steuer/Dresel/Tulis/Soncini) names a sharper construct for MindCraft: **error climate** — how errors are perceived, evaluated, and used in the learning setting.

MindCraft already destakes some stakes (soft-wrong, C4 hide-correctness). The open commercial question is whether tutors and product *behaviors after an error* systematically look like a positive error climate — or like empty kindness / shame / redirect-away — and whether parents/tutors can witness that without a Safety Score™.

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** Identity as a mathematical thinker requires interpersonal risk (admit confusion, try a wrong path, ask for help). That risk is only rational when the environment treats errors as diagnostic fuel, not as verdicts on worth — *and* still demands standards via FEI proof.

**Claims we refuse as doctrine:**
1. Psychological Safety Score™ / Comfort % / “vibes” as North Star.  
2. “Psychologically safe” marketing without observable error-handling behaviors.  
3. Destaking ≡ lowering standards / never grading / never timed practice.  
4. Shame reels, public wrong-answer stalk, family leaderboard as error theater.  
5. Bermuda-triangle redirect (leave the erring student; crown the next hand).  
6. Empty “mistakes are beautiful” posters without corrective feedback / Map route.  
7. Therapy/CBT cosplay or Calm Score™ as error climate (already SAFE-EXPOSE).  
8. Guaranteed ACT points from “safe tutoring.”

## CIII.2 Constructs (keep mechanisms distinct)

| Construct | Research meaning | MindCraft analogue | Not the same as |
|-----------|------------------|--------------------|-----------------|
| **Team psychological safety** | Shared belief the setting is safe for interpersonal risk-taking (Edmondson) | Tutor–student microclimate: ask, admit miss, try again | Warmth / niceness alone |
| **Classroom error climate** | Perception, evaluation, utilization of errors as learning elements (Steuer et al.) | Soft-wrong + stay-with-student repair + Map classify | Poster slogans |
| **Adaptive affective-motivational reaction** | Maintain motivation/functional affect after error | Soft-wrong copy; destaked framing | Empty praise |
| **Adaptive action reaction** | Effort, strategy change, work-it-out after error | `retry_120s`; Map route; AAR grain | Ignore-and-move-on |
| **Positive error handling** | Constructive teacher responses that invite learning from the error (Soncini et al.) | Tutor script: stay, diagnose, scaffold, re-attempt | Redirect / ignore / “No!” only |
| **SAFE-ERRCLIMATE** | Product+HITL doctrine for error climate under FEI | This chapter | Safety Score™ |

**Operational definition (HYPOTHESIS):** A session or Practice path is *SAFE-ERRCLIMATE compliant* when (a) errors trigger stay-with-student diagnosis (not Bermuda-triangle redirect), (b) evaluation stakes are separated from learning stakes where designed (soft-wrong / C4), (c) tutor/coach responses avoid ridicule and avoid empty “you’re fine” without Map/feedback, (d) adaptive *action* is instrumented (`retry_120s`, challenge_accept, misconception recurrence) — not comfort surveys alone, (e) high-stakes dose still exists on the exam rail (SAFE-EXAM), and (f) parents see proof age / repair artifacts — not live wrong stalk (SAFE-PDASH).

## CIII.3 Psychological safety enables learning behavior — it is not the product

**FACT:** Edmondson (1999, *Administrative Science Quarterly, 44*(2), 350–383, doi:10.2307/2666999) — team psychological safety = shared belief that the team is safe for interpersonal risk-taking; in a multimethod field study of 51 manufacturing teams, psychological safety associated with learning behavior; learning behavior mediated the link to team performance; team efficacy did not predict learning when controlling for psychological safety.

**FACT (boundary):** Edmondson’s construct is group-level and organizational. Direct port to 1:1 tutoring or solo Practice is a **HYPOTHESIS**, not a license to sell corporate safety surveys as math pedagogy.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** For MindCraft, the *portable* piece is the micro-calculus students run before risk: Will admitting “I don’t get the bridge” cost status? If yes, they hide, peek, or freeze (SAFE-HELP / SAFE-RETRIEVE). Tutor briefing and product copy should make interpersonal risk cheap *during learning*, then demand competence evidence *after*.

**Kill:** Safety Score™ / engagement-as-safety.  
**Survive:** Risk-taking behaviors (ask, retry, expose misconception) as process signals under FEI.

## CIII.4 Error climate is the classroom-specific spine — eight facets, one commercial job

**FACT:** Steuer, Rosentritt-Brunn, & Dresel (2013, *Contemporary Educational Psychology, 38*(3), 196–210, doi:10.1016/j.cedpsych.2013.03.002) — N=1,116 students in 56 German secondary math classrooms; validated multidimensional *perceived error climate* (eight subdimensions → superordinate factor); predicted adaptivity of individual error reactions above classroom goal structures and personal motivation; effort effects partially mediated through those reactions.

**FACT (product checklist):** Facets include teacher error tolerance; irrelevance of errors for assessment in learning contexts; teacher support after errors; absence of negative teacher/classmate reactions; analysis of errors; functionality of errors for learning — jointly: errors as integral vs threat.

**FACT:** Dresel, Daumiller, Spear, Janke, Dickhäuser, & Steuer (2024, *British Journal of Educational Psychology*, doi:10.1111/bjep.12697) — 2-year longitudinal N=1,641 in 69 math classrooms (grades 5–6, five waves); adaptive affective-motivational and action reactions to errors *declined* on average; decline varied by classroom; more positive perceived error climate buffered the decline. Teacher support for error adaptivity is load-bearing.

**Commercial implication:** Error climate is a **designed** property of tutor ops + Practice UX — not a natural developmental gift. Longitudinal decline without climate support is a GTM argument for HITL briefing quality (SAFE-HITL), not Comfort Score™ ads.

**Kill:** Poster-only “mistakes welcome.”  
**Survive:** Facet-mapped behaviors in playbooks and soft-wrong.

## CIII.5 Teacher/tutor responses are causal levers — not just correlates

**FACT:** Tulis (2013, *Teaching and Teacher Education, 33*, 56–68, doi:10.1016/j.tate.2013.02.003) — observational/video studies of teachers’ error-management routines; responses shape beliefs about learning from errors and domain emotions; redirecting away from the erring student (“Bermuda triangle of error correction,” Oser & Spychiger) wastes the learning opportunity.

**FACT:** Soncini, Matteucci, & Butera (2021, *European Journal of Psychology of Education, 36*, 719–738, doi:10.1007/s10212-020-00494-1) — experimental pre–post (N=108 fifth-graders): positive vs neutral error-handling scripts; positive handling (stay-and-support) increased perceived trustful error climate vs ignore / redirect / bare “incorrect.” First experimental demo that manipulating teacher error strategies shifts climate perception.

**FACT:** Soncini, Visintin, Matteucci, Tomasetto, & Butera (2022, *Learning and Instruction, 80*, 101627, doi:10.1016/j.learninstruc.2022.101627) — N=563 Italian middle-schoolers, 32 math classes; multilevel path: positive error climate → adaptive reactions → math grades (direction commercial; magnitude uncertain).

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Tutor QA should score *error episodes*, not warmth ratings: stay / diagnose / scaffold / re-attempt — or redirect / dump / shame? AI coach: soft-wrong + short principle (SAFE-EXPLAIN) + retry gate beats monologue + green-check theater.

**Kill:** Warmth NPS ≡ error climate.  
**Survive:** Stay-diagnose-scaffold-reattempt as the HITL unit.

## CIII.6 Learning from errors needs corrective feedback and destaked practice — not error romance

**FACT:** Metcalfe (2017, *Annual Review of Psychology, 68*, 465–489, doi:10.1146/annurev-psych-010416-044022) — review: error avoidance is common in classrooms but often counterproductive for typical learners; errorful learning *with corrective feedback* benefits learning; high-confidence errors are especially correctable (hypercorrection); teachers also gain diagnostic information from errors; if optimal high-stakes performance is the goal, encourage commit-and-correct in *low-stakes* learning situations rather than avoid errors at all costs.

**HYPOTHESIS (product):** SAFE-ERRCLIMATE pairs with SAFE-EXAM dual rail: destake-to-learn (Practice soft-wrong, C4 diagnostic) / dose-to-prove (timed mixed sets). Error climate without corrective Map feedback is kindness costume; feedback without destaking for anxious Maya is flooding (SAFE-EXPOSE / SAFE-DD).

**Contradiction to watch:** Celebrating errors without standards can become low-rigor brand. Red Team kills “mistakes are the curriculum” when FEI proof disappears.

**Kill:** Error romance / never-correct / forever-low-stakes.  
**Survive:** Low-stakes commit→correct; high-stakes prove later.

## CIII.7 Boundary conditions: standards, public exposure, AI sycophancy

**HYPOTHESIS:** Positive error climate ≠ absence of evaluation forever. Steuer’s “irrelevance of errors for assessment” applies to *learning episodes*, not to deleting ACT reality. Public classmate ridicule facets do not license private shame dashboards for parents (SAFE-PDASH).

**HYPOTHESIS (AI risk):** Sycophantic “great try!” without diagnosing the misconception is *negative* error climate dressed as warmth (SAFE-REPAIR / Part XXXIII). Fluent wrongness plus praise destroys both safety *and* learning.

**HYPOTHESIS (anxiety):** Destaking helps approach; it does not replace graded exposure ladders when avoidance is entrenched (SAFE-EXPOSE). Dose GLAs/PF carefully (SAFE-GENERATE / SAFE-PF).

**Kill:** Comfort-only AI; public error humiliation; standards-free “safe space.”  
**Survive:** Destaked diagnosis + standards on FEI.

## CIII.11 So what for MindCraft commercially

- **Copy:** “Mistakes get diagnosed — then you prove the fix alone.” Never Psychological Safety Score™ or “never wrong here.”  
- **Product:** Soft-wrong + stay-with-repair + Map misconception route; hide-correctness where designed; no parent live-wrong stalk.  
- **Positioning:** Against shame apps and against comfort-only AI — MindCraft is the FEI gym where errors are fuel under standards.  
- **Metric:** `retry_120s`, challenge_accept, misconception recurrence, tutor error-episode fidelity — demote comfort surveys.  
- **Kill list:** Safety Score™; poster-only safe space; Bermuda-triangle redirect; shame reels; praise-fog AI; standards-free destake; ACT guarantees from climate branding.  
- **Growth / vision:** Parent/tutor demo = one repaired bridge grain (before/after Map) — identity evidence from risk taken and corrected.

## CIII.12 Doctrine — SAFE-ERRCLIMATE (provisional)

1. **Error climate > safety slogan** — facets and behaviors, not vibes.  
2. **Stay with the student who erred** — no Bermuda-triangle redirect.  
3. **Destake learning episodes; keep FEI standards** — dual rail with SAFE-EXAM.  
4. **Adaptive action beats comfort survey** — instrument retry / repair / transfer.  
5. **Corrective diagnosis required** — densifies SAFE-MISCON / SAFE-EXPLAIN; no praise-fog.  
6. **Tutor QA on error episodes** — densifies SAFE-TALK / SAFE-HITL / SAFE-AAR.  
7. **Private repair; no shame reel / live wrong stalk** — densifies SAFE-PDASH.  
8. **No Psychological Safety Score™ / Comfort % / safe-space ACT ads**.

**Confidence:** High on error-climate construct validity, longitudinal buffering, and experimental handling→climate perception. Medium that 1:1 MindCraft scripts match classroom effect sizes — instrument ERRCLIM-* before psychologically-safe marketing.
