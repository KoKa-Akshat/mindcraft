## CXLIII.1 Why this chapter exists

SAFE-PROMOTE defined *how* an item earns C4. SAFE-HOLETRUST / SAFE-STUDHOLE defined *how* families see holes. SAFE-KNOWNETA defined finite ETAs for **late tutors**. The missing join:

> Parents who buy labeled holes ask **when** a FormatId cell becomes diagnosable. Ops who refuse play-count promote still owe a **clock story** that is neither fog nor a lie that forces rushed soft-pass admits.

Wrong resolutions already visible in edtech and marketplace tutoring:

1. **“Coming soon” forever** — Maister uncertain wait applied to bank fill.  
2. **Optimistic fill dates** — announce “this week,” miss, repeat “almost” (pilot insult).  
3. **Play-count / % complete fill bars** — engagement theater as ETA.  
4. **SLA theater that soft-passes** — checklist burned to hit a parent-facing date; Map poison.  
5. **Apology-primary without magnitude** — thin coverage apology, no band, no Practice path.  
6. **FillETA Score™ / Backlog Minutes NS** — vanity that rewards short announced ETAs.

Without SAFE-FILLETA, HOLETRUST becomes permanent apology and PROMOTE becomes idle backlog or date-driven corruption. The trust brand that labels holes must also **time-box the fill path honestly**.

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** Serious parents prefer a **known-finite verify band** (“about 2–4 weeks for sealed diagram diagnosis on this concept, capacity permitting”) plus sealed Practice now, over “soon” *or* a guaranteed calendar date that ops cannot defend without soft-pass.

**Claims we refuse:**
1. “Coming soon” / “almost ready” as default hole-fill copy.  
2. Play-count, thumbs, or Practice velocity as parent-facing fill progress.  
3. Optimistic fill ETAs that systematically under-shoot actual promote latency.  
4. Parent-facing dates that authorize soft-pass / skip-checklist promote.  
5. FillETA Score™ / Backlog Minutes / Honesty Clock NS.  
6. Apology-only hole CTAs with no magnitude and no Practice alternate.  
7. ACT / “complete visual diagnostic by [date]” guarantees from fill packaging.

## CXLIII.3 Uncertain fill waits feel longer than known, finite ones

**FACT:** Maister (1985, “The Psychology of Waiting Lines,” in Czepiel, Solomon, & Surprenant, *The Service Encounter*, Lexington Books) — **uncertain waits feel longer than known, finite waits**; “soon” produces nervous anticipation; repeated “only a few more minutes” after a miss adds insult because customers are delayed *and* not dealt with honestly.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Parent hole fill is a **multi-day/week wait**, not an 8-minute lobby — but the psychology transfers: “we’re working on diagram items” without a bound is Maister’s waiting room. “Diagnosis almost ready” after a missed week is the pilot insult at product timescale. Finite bands (“about 2–4 weeks”) let families plan sealed Practice and tutor goals without gaslight.

**Kill:** Soon-default on hole fill.  
**Survive:** Finite band when estimable; honesty > optimism.  
**Wound:** Airport wait ≠ bank QA weeks — grain differs; uncertainty tax still loads.

## CXLIII.4 Duration information beats empty reassurance — at intermediate horizons

**FACT:** Hui & Tse (1996, *Journal of Marketing, 60*(2), 81–90, doi:10.1177/002224299606000206) — waiting-**duration** information vs queuing information; effects on acceptability/affect mediate service evaluation; duration information matters especially in **intermediate** waits; very short waits gain little; very long waits need more than prettier clocks.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Typical promote latency (days–few weeks) sits in an *intermediate product horizon* where **duration bands help**. Queue-position cosplay (“you are #47 in diagram QA”) is weak for parents and risks shame/competition — prefer duration bands + one Practice CTA. For chronically long backlogs, escalate: hire/capacity plan + labeled scarcity (SAFE-COVER), not fake-short ETAs.

**Kill:** Skip fill ETA because “we already labeled the hole.”  
**Survive:** Duration band for intermediate promote latency; escalate when backlog is structural.  
**Wound:** Lab wait minutes ≠ school calendar — calibrate bands on ops data (FILLETA-1).

## CXLIII.5 Informing about anticipated delay changes behavior — accuracy matters

**FACT:** Whitt (1999, *Management Science, 45*(2), 192–207, doi:10.1287/mnsc.45.2.192) — informing customers about anticipated delays shifts **reneging** toward **balking**; when providers choose to inform, **accurate prediction** of delays matters for managing the system.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Parent-facing fill ETAs change behavior: some families start sealed Practice now (good balk into the Practice rail); some delay purchase/renewal if the band is absurdly long (honest balk). Optimistic false short bands produce later reneging (churn + trust debt) when the date slips. Commercially prefer accurate-conservative bands that induce **Practice alternate uptake** over short lies that spike short-run conversion.

**Kill:** Fake-short fill dates for conversion.  
**Survive:** Accurate-enough bands; instrument honesty vs conversion separately.  
**Wound:** Call-center queue ≠ edtech bank — transfer as decision architecture, not throughput formula in ads.

## CXLIII.6 Affect mediates delay evaluation — control and filled time matter

**FACT:** Taylor (1994, *Journal of Marketing, 58*(2), 56–69, doi:10.1177/002224299405800205) — delays affect service evaluations **mediated by negative affect** (anger, uncertainty); perceived provider **control** and **filled time** influence evaluations indirectly via those affects.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Hole-fill delay must (1) cut uncertainty (finite band or labeled ignorance), (2) fill time with a **session-related** alternate (sealed Practice for that FormatId/sibling — STUDHOLE/HOLETRUST CTA), (3) attribute control honestly (“seal/verify capacity,” not “Maya isn’t visual enough”). Fear scarcity (“buy before holes widen”) recreates CRUNCHMSG/WTP kills.

**Kill:** Character-blame + empty wait.  
**Survive:** Uncertainty↓ + Practice fill + ops-capacity attribution.  
**Wound:** Airline delay field ≠ parent MoC — mechanism transfer only.

## CXLIII.7 Progress information beats apology loops — without fake progress bars

**FACT:** Munichor & Rafaeli (2007, *Journal of Applied Psychology, 92*(2), 511–521, doi:10.1037/0021-9010.92.2.511) — in telephone waits, **queue-location / progress information** beat music and **apologies** on abandonment and evaluations; sense of **progress** (not only perceived duration) mediated reactions.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Parent hole UX may show **ops-progress class** signals that are true: “in verify queue,” “blocked on figure repair,” “promoted — ready for diagnosis” — not apology spam. **Ban** play-count bars and % “almost diagnosed” meters that are engagement cosplay. True progress = ticket state transitions under SAFE-PROMOTE, optionally coarse (“queued / in review / blocked / shipped”), never vanity throughput %.

**Kill:** Apology-primary; play-count fill bar.  
**Survive:** Coarse true ticket-state progress + duration band.  
**Wound:** Phone-queue position ≠ promote ticket depth — do not show raw “#12” if it induces rank shame; prefer state + band.

## CXLIII.8 Transparency triad still governs the clock story

**Reuse (SAFE-HOLETRUST):** Schnackenberg & Tomlinson (2016) — transparency ≈ disclosure + clarity + accuracy. Soft-pass-to-hit-date and play-count clocks break **accuracy/integrity**; jargon SLA dumps break **clarity**; hiding the clock while showing holes breaks **disclosure**.

**Kill:** Clock theater that sacrifices seals.  
**Survive:** Short triad — what’s not diagnosed, verify band or labeled unknown, one Practice CTA, seals unchanged.

## CXLIII.10 Product surface — SAFE-FILLETA claim contract

1. Hole CTA may show a **finite verify band** when ops estimator has capacity signal; else **labeled uncertainty**.  
2. Band source = backlog depth × reviewer capacity × median promote latency — **never** play counts / thumbs.  
3. Grain = days/weeks (coarse); ban second-precision countdown theater.  
4. Prefer **conservative** bands; under-promise/over-deliver on *ship date*, never on learning claims.  
5. On slip: new band + timestamp; ban repeating “almost / any day now.”  
6. Coarse true ticket **state** optional (“queued / in review / blocked / shipped”); ban % play-progress.  
7. **Seals unchanged by date pressure** — no soft-pass / skip-checklist to hit FillETA.  
8. Always offer **one Practice alternate** when sealed stock exists; if none, “not open yet” without fake greens (STUDHOLE).  
9. Parent and student bands **parity** — no dual script.  
10. Telemetry: `fill_eta_honesty_ok`, `fill_eta_slip_count`, `softpass_for_date_flag`, CTA→Practice — demote FillETA Score™ / Backlog Minutes NS.

## CXLIII.11 Doctrine — SAFE-FILLETA (provisional)

1. **Hole honesty includes a clock story** — finite band or labeled unknown (Maister × HOLETRUST).  
2. **Bands are capacity-grounded** — backlog SLA feeds the estimator; marketing calendars do not.  
3. **Internal SLA ≠ parent guarantee** — ops targets may be tighter; external copy stays conservative bands.  
4. **KNOWNETA refresh rules** apply at product timescale — no optimistic slip loops.  
5. **Progress = ticket state**, not engagement meters (Munichor without play-count cosplay).  
6. **Filled wait = Practice alternate** (Taylor filled time × STUDHOLE CTA).  
7. **PROMOTE integrity > ETA optics** — never soft-pass for the clock.  
8. **No FillETA Score™ / Backlog Minutes / Honesty Clock NS**.  
9. **No ACT / complete-visual-by-date guarantees**.  
10. Copy spine: “Diagram diagnosis for this topic: not sealed yet. We’re verifying items — about 2–4 weeks at current capacity. Meanwhile, try sealed Practice with the answer shown when available.”

**Confidence:** High on wait-information spines with stated transfer limits. Medium on exact band widths, estimator formula, and when to show state vs band-only (FILLETA-*). High that soon/play-count/soft-pass-for-date are commercially toxic under FEI + Map trust.
