**Product:** The Desk by MindCraft  
**Surface:** Create · Write a book about what you love  
**Canon:** `BRAND_BOOK.md` §1/§2/§8. Agent is **Jesse**. Mic is **press to talk**, centered. No em dashes.

Dan McCreary’s book-build pipeline (course description → learning graph → chapters → MicroSims → publish) is the spine. We do not claim he built this screen. We learned the order from his open textbooks.

---

## Why this screen exists

A blank Create studio is a deck tool. Students freeze. The value is a **guided first book** around a hobby, not “talk it through, ship the page.”

Maya does not need a prompt bar. She needs four questions, in order, with the page filling as she answers.

---

## Frames (1440×810, plus iPad 1180)

### 1. Pick a passion
Cream dotted grid. Five chips: Skate · Baking · Piano · Photos · Something else.  
Jesse press disc **centered under the chips**. Line: “Name it. I’ll ask what a friend needs first.”

### 2. Chapter map (the graph, human)
Left rail: 3–5 chapter titles Jesse proposed from the passion.  
Center: page 1 of chapter 1, serif, one photo slot.  
Bottom center: Jesse press. Guide chip: “What should a friend understand after this page?”

### 3. Talk a page
Press Jesse. Transcript becomes the paragraph. Student can edit.  
If the idea needs a picture, paperclip. If it needs a moving idea, offer a MicroSim slot (iframe, credited).

### 4. Spine
Cover, title in their words, **their name**. Binder, not a feed.

---

## The five questions (do not skip)

1. What do you love enough to explain?
2. What must a friend already know?
3. What is chapter 1 in one sentence?
4. Where do words fail (photo or sim)?
5. Whose name goes on the spine? (always theirs)
