## CLIX.3 Score resolution is a designed process — method choice changes outcomes

**FACT:** Johnson, Penny, Fisher, and Kuhs (2003, *Applied Measurement in Education, 16*(4), 299–322, doi:10.1207/s15324818ame1604_3) — when raters disagree, agencies must **resolve** before reporting; pass/fail and reliability/validity coefficients **differ across resolution methods** (average originals; average originals + adjudicator; match adjudicator to closest original). Matching the adjudicator to the closest original most often yielded the **lowest** validity coefficients in their design; averaging originals with the adjudicator yielded slightly higher reliability.

**FACT:** Johnson, Penny, and Gordon (2000, *Applied Measurement in Education, 13*(2), 121–138, doi:10.1207/s15324818ame1302_1) — common resolution forms from a national survey of agencies affect **interrater reliability** of operational scores and can **inflate** reliability estimates when adjudication is conditional on disagreement; choice of method can change the percentage of papers defined as passing.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** MindCraft’s “poison label” is closer to a **use decision** than a student score — but the Johnson line still applies: **how** you resolve disagreement (and how fast you force a label) changes what the system *claims* about quality. Auto-poison at timeout is a resolution method with **no validity study**. Prefer a documented re-seal / gold step whose outcome *writes* the label — not a clock that invents one.

**Kill:** Clock≡blame as default “resolution.” **Survive:** Named re-seal/gold procedure before gen/rater labels. **Wound:** Borrowing GRE/TOEFL adjudicator-average formulas as brand (student-score context ≠ seal poison).

## CLIX.4 Third ratings are necessary but not sufficient quality control

**FACT:** Myford and Wolfe (2002, *Journal of Applied Measurement, 3*(3), 300–324) — examined the third-rater discrepancy resolution procedure used in scoring the Test of Spoken English; results for 1,446 adult examinees show that **implementing a discrepancy resolution procedure is not sufficient in itself** for quality-control monitoring — many anomalous rating patterns remain undetected by disagreement flags alone.

**FACT:** Wind and Walker (2019, *Assessing Writing, 39*, 25–38, doi:10.1016/j.asw.2018.12.002) — traditional rater-agreement flagging for third ratings and IRT person-fit criteria flag **many but not all** of the same profiles; resolved ratings improve person fit for **most but not all** flagged students — complementary monitoring matters.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Dual re-seal SLA is the MindCraft analogue of “third rating on disagreement” — necessary for ambiguous POISONSEP tickets, **not** a complete QA story. Pair with GOLDSET / REPAIRSTRAT monitors (Myford–Wolfe sufficiency wound). Do not sell “we always third-rate” as Map-complete.

**Kill:** Dual-on-ambiguous ≡ full integrity proof. **Survive:** SLA’d re-seal + gold/path monitors.

## CLIX.5 Operational programs already treat adjudication as a thresholded workflow

**FACT (transfer-limited):** Large-scale writing programs (GRE/TOEFL lines documented in ETS R&D and e-rater evaluation reports) typically route essays to a **third human** when two ratings differ beyond a **predetermined threshold** (often more than one score point); resolution rules vary by program (adjudicator becomes score; average adjacent pair; etc.). Adjudication thresholds also appear when automated scores check humans — discrepancy beyond threshold triggers additional human review (e.g., Ramineni et al. / ETS e-rater evaluation reports for GRE and TOEFL prompts).

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Borrow **threshold → additional independent judgment → documented combination rule**, not e-rater cosplay or “GRE inside” ACT ads. MindCraft thresholds are on **seal discord / mixed poison indicators**, not holistic essay points. The SLA is the **ops twin** of KNOWNETA: finite, honest duration for the *hold*, not optimistic “adjudicating soon.”

**Kill:** ETS adjudication cosplay ACT ads; e-rater-as-second-seal brand. **Survive:** Predetermined discord → independent re-seal with a written rule for what the second seal may conclude.
