Well, does it actually work?

Literature on tutoring shows consistent, substantial gains from high-quality 1:1 and small-group instruction (pooled ~0.37 SD across PreK–12 RCTs).1 Peer models at college, like Macalester SciQ, put students in both seats: learner and teacher.

The idea grew from SciQ: students help students. Roles reverse. Continuity sticks.
