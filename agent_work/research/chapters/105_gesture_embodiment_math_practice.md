## CV.1 Why this chapter exists

Gesture research is one of the few embodiment literatures with **causal math experiments** (not just philosophy). Founders and designers love the slogan “get kids moving.” Competitors ship AR overlays, kinesthetic playlists, and “whole-body math.” MindCraft’s risk is either (a) ignoring a real transfer lever on FormatId / equivalence / geometry grains, or (b) branding motion as identity while measuring engagement theater.

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** Hands can carry strategy information speech misses — but only when the motion *represents the math idea*, not when the product forces any movement for dopamine.

**Claims we refuse as doctrine:**
1. Embodiment Score™ / Gesture Score™ / Motion Minutes as learning NS.  
2. Kinesthetic / “hands-on learner” meshing quizzes as placement.  
3. Always-add-gesture / mandatory enactment on every item.  
4. Manipulative action ≡ gesture ≡ abstraction (false; transfer differs).  
5. Camera / pose / motion-capture “embodiment AI” as empathy or mastery.  
6. AR/VR body immersion ≡ FEI identity.  
7. Forced produce-gesture for spontaneous non-gesturers with no alternative scaffold.  
8. Guaranteed ACT points / “move to learn” splash as science-backed readiness.

| Construct | Research meaning | MindCraft analogue | Failure mode |
|-----------|------------------|--------------------|--------------|
| **Gesture** | Representational hand movement that can encode strategy without manipulating objects | Coach prompt: “show the groups with your hands”; tutor model | Flailing for engagement |
| **Action on objects** | Physically move tokens / tiles | Rare in SPA; optional desk tokens | Concrete lock-in; weak transfer |

**Operational definition (HYPOTHESIS):** A Practice/coach path is *SAFE-GESTURE compliant* when (a) any prompted motion encodes a named strategy or FormatId structure (not “wiggle”), (b) produce vs observe is chosen with learner readiness in mind, (c) transfer/`solo_transfer_pass` is the success metric (not motion compliance), (d) non-producers get an alternate scaffold (manipulative analogy, draw, or speech SE), (e) no camera pose scoring, and (f) marketing never claims kinesthetic-style personalization.

## CV.3 Gesture can create ideas — not just express them

**FACT:** Goldin-Meadow, Cook, & Mitchell (2009, *Psychological Science, 20*(3), 267–272, doi:10.1111/j.1467-9280.2009.02297.x) — children required to produce **correct** gestures during a math lesson learned more than those producing partially correct gestures, who learned more than no-gesture controls; the effect was mediated by children incorporating gesture-only strategy information into later speech. Mechanism claim: learners can extract meaning from their own hand movements.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** MindCraft coach prompts that specify a *correct strategy gesture* (e.g., grouping for equivalence) can seed speechable strategy — fluff “use your hands!” without strategy content will not.

**Kill:** Generic “embodied warm-up” before every mission.  
**Survive:** Strategy-specified gesture prompts on selected grains (esp. when speech is stuck).

## CV.4 Gesture can make learning last

**FACT:** Cook, Mitchell, & Goldin-Meadow (2008, *Cognition, 106*(2), 1047–1058, doi:10.1016/j.cognition.2007.04.010) — children who gestured while learning a math concept showed better **retention** than speech-only learners even when immediate post-lesson gains looked similar; gesture offered an embodied alternate representation that supported consolidation.

**Applied (FOUNDER BELIEF → testable):** For high-value concepts on the ACT path, a short produce-gesture or finger-trace step at encoding may raise delayed `transfer_pass` more than adding another AI sentence (densifies SAFE-EXPLAIN / SAFE-SCHED).

**Kill:** Motion Minutes / streak-of-gestures as retention proof.  
**Survive:** Sparse gesture at encoding + scheduled return check (SAFE-FORGET honesty).

---

## CV.5 Gesture beats action for transfer — action is not “more embodied = better”

**FACT:** Novack, Congdon, Hemani-Lopez, & Goldin-Meadow (2014, *Psychological Science, 25*(4), 903–910, doi:10.1177/0956797613518351) — third-graders taught a mathematical-equivalence strategy via (a) action on objects, (b) concrete gesture miming that action, or (c) abstract gesture: all helped on trained problems, but **only gesture conditions** supported generalization to novel problems better than would be expected from action alone; gesture promoted transfer better than direct action on objects.

**Commercial implication (HYPOTHESIS):** Selling “hands-on manipulatives forever” or AR drag-tokens as the embodiment hero can **hurt** the identity thesis if students stay glued to concrete moves. Abstract/representational gesture (or structural draw) is often the better product bet for far transfer.

**Kill:** Manipulative theater / “move the blocks = mastery” ads.  
**Survive:** Representational gesture or FormatId sketch that abstracts the same strategy.

## CV.6 Individual differences: forced gesture can harm non-gesturers

**FACT:** Congdon, Wakefield, Novack, Hemani-Lopez, & Goldin-Meadow (2024, *Cognitive Science, 48*(7), e13479, doi:10.1111/cogs.13479) — whether children spontaneously gestured before a math lesson predicted who benefited from seeing vs doing gesture during instruction; for spontaneous gesturers, both seeing and doing gesture beat a comparison manipulative action on generalization/retention; for **non-spontaneous gesturers, doing gesture was less effective** than the comparison action (deficit specific to gesture — they still benefited from the manipulative action).

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** One-size mandatory “do this hand move” UX is product-hostile for a slice of Maya. Offer: observe tutor/coach gesture, produce gesture, **or** alternate structural action/draw — never force produce-only.

**Kill:** Forced gesture compliance gates / Gesture Score™.  
**Survive:** Optional strategy-motion with alternate FormatId scaffold (densifies SAFE-FORMAT).

## CV.7 Embodiment is real in teaching — and easy to costume

**FACT (review / theoretical synthesis):** Alibali & Nathan (2012, *Journal of the Learning Sciences, 21*(2), 247–286, doi:10.1080/10508406.2011.611446) — learners’ and teachers’ gestures evidence that mathematical knowledge is grounded in perception/action and environment; pointing, representational, and metaphoric gestures are instructional signals, not decoration.

**FACT (geometry / proof line):** Nathan & Walkington (2017, *Cognitive Research: Principles and Implications, 2*, 9, doi:10.1186/s41235-016-0040-5) — grounded and embodied mathematical cognition (GEMC): action can foster insight, but valid proof needs action coordinated with language that propositionalizes general properties. Nathan, Schenck, Vinsonhaler, Michaelis, Swart, & Walkington (2021, *Journal of Educational Psychology, 113*(5), 929–948, doi:10.1037/edu0000638) — dynamic depictive gestures (enact transformational ops) associate with geometry intuition, insight, and proof beyond speech alone, controlling for expertise and spatial ability.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** HITL tutors should **model** strategy gestures (SAFE-APPRENTICE) then fade; coach copy for diagram FormatId can invite “show the transformation,” then require a one-sentence general claim (gesture + language), not mute waving.

**Kill:** AR immersion / body-tracking as proof of learning.  
**Survive:** Tutor modeling + student optional enact + verbalize general rule.

## CV.8 Link to FormatId, dual coding, and generative draw

**HYPOTHESIS (product seam):** Gesture and finger-trace are most load-honest on **diagram / coordinate_graph / number_line** FormatIds and on bridge grains where spatial structure is the object — not on every symbolic multiple-choice. Densifies SAFE-DUAL (structural figures) and SAFE-GENERATE (guided draw): a student-produced sketch or trace is often the SPA-native embodiment, without cameras.

**Contradiction to watch:** Extra motion + story wrap + figure can explode extraneous load (SAFE-STORYLOAD / CLT). Red Team: if gesture prompts raise quit or lower accuracy without delayed transfer gains, cut them.

**Kill:** “We teach every format with full-body VR.”  
**Survive:** Sparse, FormatId-triggered motion/draw scaffolds with transfer checks.

## CV.12 So what for MindCraft commercially

- **Copy:** “Show the structure — then solve without the props.” Never Embodiment Score™, kinesthetic-style placement, or “move to learn = ACT ready.”  
- **Product:** Optional strategy-gesture / finger-trace / draw on selected FormatId and bridge grains; observe/produce/draw choice; no pose cameras; tutor model→fade.  
- **Positioning:** Against learning-styles and AR immersion theater; for representational motion that earns transfer.  
- **Metric:** Delayed `transfer_pass` / `solo_transfer_pass`, format-hop success, quit under motion prompts — demote Motion Minutes / gesture compliance %.  
- **Kill list:** Embodiment Score™; styles meshing; always-gesture; action≡gesture≡abstraction; motion-capture SKU; AR body hero; forced produce-only; ACT guarantees from gesture kits.  
- **Growth / vision:** Embodiment is a **scaffold channel** for identity evidence (solo proof), not a brand costume.

## CV.13 Doctrine — SAFE-GESTURE (provisional)

1. **Strategy-specific motion only** — encode the math idea; no flailing theater.  
2. **Gesture ≠ action on objects** — prefer representational gesture/draw when transfer is the goal (Novack).  
3. **Retention is a reason, not a streak** — sparse encode-time gesture; check delayed return (Cook).  
4. **Respect non-gesturers** — offer observe / produce / draw|action alternates (Congdon).  
5. **Language closes the loop** — gesture + propositionalize (GEMC); densifies SAFE-GENERATE / SE.  
6. **FormatId-triggered, load-honest** — densifies SAFE-DUAL / SAFE-FORMAT / SAFE-STORYLOAD.  
7. **HITL models then fades** — densifies SAFE-APPRENTICE; no permanent motion coach.  
8. **No Embodiment Score™ / styles meshing / camera pose SKUs / ACT-from-motion ads**.

**Confidence:** High that strategy-linked gesture can improve learning, retention, and transfer in controlled math tasks, and that action≠gesture for generalization. Medium that SPA prompts will replicate lab gains without load/quit costs — instrument GESTURE-* before embodied-learning marketing. High confidence to kill styles meshing and surveillance embodiment SKUs regardless.
