Stealing the long-term key later does NOT decrypt past sessions.
Versus TLS 1.2: two round trips before any application data, a longer menu of options and key-exchange modes, and several footguns (static RSA key exchange with no forward secrecy, renegotiation, weak cipher suites). TLS 1.3 removed them — fewer options, faster, secure by default.
