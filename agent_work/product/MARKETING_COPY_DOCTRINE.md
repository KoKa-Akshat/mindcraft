## Thesis

MindCraft is the memory between the student and the tutor.  
Front door now: ACT Math. Not an open global marketplace.

## Hard rules for any generator

- No em dashes in shipped copy  
- No score guarantees, Bloom 2-sigma, streak-as-learning, “tutoring is free,” grit posters  
- No inventing puzzle answers, citations, or testimonials  
- Student lines may be identity-led; parent/ACT lines stay purchase/clarity-led  
- ChatGPT is never the hero of the sentence
