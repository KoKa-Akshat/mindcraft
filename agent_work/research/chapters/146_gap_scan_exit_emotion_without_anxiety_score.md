## CXLVI.1 Why this chapter exists

SAFE-STUDHOLE locked **what** teens see after incomplete FormatId probes (parity holes, no fake greens, one CTA). The remaining failure mode is **how the exit feels**: products often “manage affect” by (a) scoring anxiety, (b) consoling fixed ability, (c) painting ego grades next to comments, (d) flooding with calm-app theater, or (e) hiding holes so the exit feels “complete.” SAFE-PRIVACY already bans face/voice Emotion AI and Anxiety Score™ as North Star. SAFE-EXPOSE bans flooding and Calm Score™. This chapter binds those bans to the **post-gap-scan exit surface** — the moment commercial conversion and identity risk collide.

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** Parents and teens will pay for a Desk that says “this format wasn’t measured yet — try one sealed Practice when ready” more than for a Desk that diagnoses their child’s anxiety percentile or comforts them out of diagrams forever.

**Claims we refuse:**
1. Anxiety Score™ / Calm Score™ / Mood Minutes / Exit Emotion Score™ as North Star.  
2. Face/voice emotion AI or mandatory mood gate after gap-scan (SAFE-PRIVACY).  
3. Consoling ability talk: “It’s ok — not everyone is a visual / graph person.”  
4. Ego grades / Completeness % / “you got 3/5 formats” as the emotional closer beside hole honesty.  
5. Fake-complete greens or hide-holes as affect management.  
6. Day-one flooding timed ACT dump sold as “exposure therapy” exit.  
7. Empty growth-mindset / “mistakes are good!” poster without grain + CTA.  
8. ACT / identity / calm-to-zero guarantees from exit packaging.

## CXLVI.3 Math anxiety is real — productizing it as a Score is the trap

**FACT:** Ashcraft (2002, *Current Directions in Psychological Science, 11*(5), 181–185, doi:10.1111/1467-8721.00196) — math anxiety is tension/fear related to math that predicts **avoidance** of elective math coursework and math-heavy careers; online timed arithmetic can show anxiety effects even when broad achievement tests look similar; anxiety can compromise **working memory** during math performance; causes are not fully determined; some teaching styles that publicly expose incompetence are plausible risk factors.

**FACT:** Ashcraft & Krause (2007, *Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, 14*(2), 243–248, doi:10.3758/BF03194059) — high math anxiety functions like a dual-task load: preoccupation with math fears consumes working-memory resources needed for non-retrieval arithmetic/math.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** MindCraft must take anxiety **seriously as a design constraint** (destake stakes, avoid public humiliation theater, prefer graded approach — SAFE-EXPOSE) **without** turning the gap-scan exit into a clinical dashboard. Scoring “how anxious you are after your scan” invents a North Star that (a) conflates state affect with trait/construct measurement, (b) invites Goodhart gaming and stigma, and (c) sells empathy theater instead of FEI attempts. Measurement instruments exist for research (e.g., abbreviated scales); that does **not** license Anxiety Score™ in the student product chrome.

**Kill:** Anxiety Score™ / emotion-AI empathy brand on exit.  
**Survive:** Destaked stakes + honest holes + one attempt path; research AMAS/AAI stay off the marketing splash.

## CXLVI.4 Consoling comfort demotivates — confidence without ability excuses

**FACT:** Rattan, Good, & Dweck (2012, *Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, 48*(3), 731–737, doi:10.1016/j.jesp.2011.12.012) — instructors holding more of an **entity** (fixed) theory of math ability were quicker to judge low ability from early scores and more likely to offer **comfort-oriented** feedback (“It’s ok — not everyone can be good at math”) and “kind” practices unlikely to promote engagement; students receiving comfort feedback inferred low expectations and reported **lower motivation and lower expectations** for future performance vs strategy-oriented feedback that communicated high standards.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Post-hole exit lines like “It’s fine if graphs aren’t your thing” or “Not everyone is a visual learner” are **entity comfort** dressed as kindness. They feel warm in the moment and commercially toxic afterward: they license FormatId avoidance, contradict SAFE-FORMAT / SAFE-DUAL (no learning-style meshing), and train parents to hear holes as destiny. Prefer Rattan-aligned pattern: acknowledge difficulty of the *task* (“Diagram items need a sealed figure before we hide keys”), express expectation of growth via practice, offer **strategy** (one sealed Practice), never excuse the person.

**Kill:** Consoling ability talk after holes or soft-wrongs.  
**Survive:** High-standards + strategy CTA (densifies SAFE-SOFTMSG / SAFE-FBLIT / SAFE-STUDHOLE).

## CXLVI.5 Ego marks steal the exit — comments alone beat grade+comment theater

**FACT:** Butler (1988, *British Journal of Educational Psychology, 58*(1), 1–14, doi:10.1111/j.2044-8279.1988.tb00874.x) — among fifth/sixth graders, **task-involving individual comments** produced higher interest and performance than **ego-involving numerical grades** or **grades plus comments**; grades+comments tended to promote ego-involvement similarly to grades alone (comments were often ignored when marks were present).

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Gap-scan exits that show Completeness %, “3 of 5 formats ready,” letter-like belts, or rainbow mastery meters beside thoughtful hole copy recreate Butler’s grade+comment washout: teens attend to the ego mark (“I’m incomplete / I’m a C visual”) and discard the where-next. SAFE-STUDHOLE already bans Completeness % as NS; SAFE-EXITEMO forbids ego chrome as **affect management**. Transfer limit: Butler’s school tasks ≠ consumer diagnostic — borrow task-vs-ego, not “no numbers ever” dogma for FEI telemetry elsewhere.

**Kill:** Exit Completeness % / format letter grades as emotional closer.  
**Survive:** Hole facts + one CTA; no ego meter competing with feed-forward.

## CXLVI.6 Destake ≠ flood; destake ≠ soft standards

**Reuse (SAFE-EXPOSE / SAFE-ERRCLIMATE / SAFE-COLD):** Graded approach and expectancy violation under lowered stakes; destake mistakes without lowering standards; humble cold start without fireworks.

**HYPOTHESIS:** Exit destake means: (1) no timed ACT flood as the first post-scan move, (2) no classmate ranks on the exit, (3) no shame stalk of soft-wrongs to parent phones in the same beat, (4) language that separates **measurement limit** from **person worth**, (5) invitation to a **small sealed attempt** — not a calm-app meditation hero, not a forever-E0 protect track. Destake is situational stakes design, not therapy cosplay.

**Kill:** Flooding≡care; Calm Score™; forever protect without fade path.  
**Survive:** Mild stakes on exit + standards held on Practice grain.

## CXLVI.7 Privacy spine — optional check-in is not an Anxiety Score™

**Reuse (SAFE-PRIVACY):** Self-authored short-TTL affect → pedagogy modifiers only; ERT ban; contextual integrity; refusal-safe; FERPA≡ethics floor not doctrine.

**HYPOTHESIS:** If product offers a post-scan “how are you feeling about math right now?” check-in, it must be: optional, skippable without penalty, not written to durable Anxiety Score™ profiles, not sold, not shown on parent shame dashboards, and used only for soft pedagogy modifiers (e.g., slightly softer first Practice stake — never skip challenge forever). Inferring affect from face/voice/keystroke “empathy AI” remains banned.

**Kill:** Mandatory mood gate; emotion-AI exit; sell-affect.
