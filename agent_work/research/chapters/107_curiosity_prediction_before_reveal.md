## CVII.1 Why this chapter exists

Edtech loves cliffhangers: tease the answer, drip lore, celebrate “curiosity.” Competitors ship instant green checks, spoilers-as-help, and Curiosity Score™ dashboards. Classroom and lab literatures converge on a sharper claim: **curiosity and surprise help when the learner has a stake in an outcome** — typically by *generating an explicit prediction* before feedback. Passive watch / instant dump / post-hoc “were you surprised?” theater underdeliver.

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** Soft-wrong and predict-before-reveal are identity technology when they create a felt knowledge gap the student then *repairs* — not when they maximize dopamine for the next swipe.

**Claims we refuse as doctrine:**
1. Curiosity Score™ / Engagement Score™ / self-rated curiosity as learning NS.  
2. Cliffhanger / lore tease without a committed prediction on the math outcome.  
3. Instant reveal / instant coach dump as “curiosity-friendly UX.”  
4. Post-hoc surprise rating ≡ prediction-induced surprise.  
5. Prediction theater (guess for XP) without encoding the correct join.  
6. Trivia-curiosity cosplay as ACT readiness.  
7. Always-predict dogma on every item (load + freeze risk).  
8. Curiosity kits / “we spark wonder” ACT-point guarantees.

## CVII.2 Constructs (keep deprivation, commit, and surprise distinct)

| Construct | Research meaning | MindCraft analogue | Failure mode |
|-----------|------------------|--------------------|--------------|
| **Information-gap curiosity** | Cognitively induced deprivation from a perceived knowledge gap | Soft-wrong commit; “what will the Map say?” before reveal | Trivia tease; lore FOMO |
| **Epistemic curiosity (state)** | Desire to know an answer; reward anticipation for information | Anticipation after prediction, before KR | Trait Curiosity Score™ |
| **Explicit prediction** | Learner commits to an outcome before feedback | Choice / numeric / qualitative commit on this item | Silent watch; postdiction |
| **Expectancy violation / surprise** | Outcome ≠ prediction; arousal that can aid updating | Soft-wrong → contrast card | Shame flood; ignore update |
| **SAFE-PREDICT** | Commit → curiosity/surprise → encode repair → transfer | This chapter | Cliffhanger SKU |

**Operational definition (HYPOTHESIS):** A Practice/Diagnostic path is *SAFE-PREDICT compliant* when (a) on learn-rail soft-wrongs and selected bridge/format probes, the student commits a prediction or answer *before* correctness/coach reveal, (b) cliffhangers without commit are banned, (c) expectancy-violating feedback is paired with a short repair encode (SE / soft-wrong contrast), not only a green/red flash, (d) curiosity is never a North Star metric, (e) predict is mode-gated (not every item; respect freeze/anxiety), and (f) marketing never sells wonder kits as exam points.

## CVII.3 Curiosity is an information-gap drive — and it is transient

**FACT (review):** Loewenstein (1994, *Psychological Bulletin, 116*(1), 75–98, doi:10.1037/0033-2909.116.1.75) — curiosity as cognitively induced deprivation from perceiving a gap in knowledge; intense yet often transient and easily satisfied; situational determinants matter more than trait slogans.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Product should *open a gap the student cares about* (commit to “what will this expression simplify to?” / “which FormatId is needed?”) rather than decorate with wonder copy. Densifies Insight 6 (history frames) only when the gap is mathematical, not franchise trivia (SAFE-STORYLOAD).

**Kill:** Trait Curiosity Score™ / perpetual-wonder brand.  
**Survive:** Situational gaps created by prediction commits.

## CVII.4 State curiosity predicts memory for surprising answers

**FACT (empirical + fMRI):** Kang, Hsu, Krajbich, Loewenstein, McClure, Wang, & Camerer (2009, *Psychological Science, 20*(8), 963–973, doi:10.1111/j.1467-9280.2009.02402.x) — curiosity while reading trivia questions correlated with caudate activity (reward anticipation); higher curiosity predicted willingness to spend scarce resources for answers; curiosity enhanced memory-related activity after incorrect guesses; higher initial curiosity predicted better recall of surprising answers 1–2 weeks later.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Soft-wrong + delayed reveal is not cruelty theater — it can be the curiosity window Kang et al. document, *if* the student has a prediction stake. Densifies SAFE-FBTIME (micro-delay after commit) and SAFE-MISCON (productive wrong).

**Kill:** Instant dump that collapses the gap before encoding.  
**Survive:** Commit → brief anticipation → informative repair.

## CVII.5 Generating a prediction stimulates curiosity (vs generating an example)

**FACT:** Brod & Breitwieser (2019, *npj Science of Learning, 4*, Article 17, doi:10.1038/s41539-019-0056-y) — in a numerical-facts task, generating a **prediction** before the answer raised the share of high-curiosity ratings vs generating an **example**; high curiosity associated with better memory for the correct answer; pupillary data showed larger dilation during anticipation (and further enhancement when predicting).

**Nuance (FACT from Brod’s later review):** Brod (2021, *Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, 28*, 1839–1848, doi:10.3758/s13423-021-01904-1) — predicting is more than active retrieval; benefits also track how the ensuing feedback is processed (curiosity/surprise pathways). Overall retention gaps between predict vs example can be small even when curiosity rises — so product must optimize **encoding of the correct outcome**, not curiosity ratings alone.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Prefer “predict the result / choose the trap answer” over “give an example of the concept” as the pre-reveal generative move when the goal is curiosity→memory for the join. Still require repair encode (SAFE-EXPLAIN length discipline).

**Kill:** Curiosity ratings as success; example-prompt as curiosity substitute.  
**Survive:** Prediction commit + measured delayed recall of the repaired join.

## CVII.6 Surprise needs a prior prediction — postdiction is not the same

**FACT:** Brod, Hasselhorn, & Bunge (2018, *Learning and Instruction, 55*, 22–31, doi:10.1016/j.learninstruc.2018.01.013) — generating predictions (vs post-hoc evaluations) facilitated geography learning; pupillometry showed expectancy-violating outcomes produced a surprise response **only when a prediction was made beforehand**, and surprise strength related positively to learning; soccer-match prediction similarly improved memory specifically for expectancy-violating events.

**FACT (follow-on):** Theobald & Brod / Brod line (2022, *Psychonomic Bulletin & Review*, doi:10.3758/s13423-022-02124-x) — explicitly predicting outcomes enhances learning of expectancy-violating information relative to post-hoc evaluation; U-shaped expectedness–memory relation under prediction; pupillary surprise linked to successful learning of highly unexpected outcomes.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Soft-wrong UX must capture the commit *before* reveal. Asking “were you surprised?” after dumping the key is postdiction cosplay. Densifies SAFE-FBTIME and SAFE-MONITOR (confidence×outcome needs a prior judgment).

**Kill:** Post-hoc surprise theater / reveal-first “engagement.”  
**Survive:** Explicit prediction → expectancy-violating feedback → encode.

## CVII.7 Classroom grain: predict before demo beats passive watch

**FACT:** Crouch, Fagen, Callan, & Mazur (2004, *American Journal of Physics, 72*(6), 835–838, doi:10.1119/1.1707018) — students who passively observe classroom demonstrations understand underlying concepts no better than students who see no demonstration; students who **predict the outcome before seeing it** show significantly greater understanding.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Story-world “show the magic” beats and Solver worked-example reveals that play without a student prediction inherit the passive-demo failure mode. Densifies SAFE-STORYLOAD / SAFE-FADE / SAFE-EXPTIME: prediction or attempt before full reveal.

**Kill:** Spectacle-first pedagogy; watch-the-demo ≡ learning.  
**Survive:** Predict (or attempt) before reveal on demos, figures, and coach dumps.

## CVII.8 Mode gates: when NOT to force predict

**HYPOTHESIS (product seam):** Always-predict is load and anxiety risk. Gate prediction commits to: (1) learn-rail soft-wrongs and bridge/format discrimination items, (2) story/demo beats where the math outcome is the stake, (3) optional “predict the trap” on misconception-rich stems. Skip or soften when SAFE-RETRIEVE freeze is active, exam prove-rail needs immediate KR under labeled stakes (SAFE-FBTIME), or novice acquisition needs short worked-example before struggle (SAFE-EXPTIME / SAFE-PF guided default).

**Contradiction to watch:** Prediction commits can raise quit if shame-framed (“you were wrong”) or if every item becomes a cliffhanger tax. Red Team: if predict-before-reveal raises abandon without delayed transfer gain, narrow to soft-wrong subset; keep ban on Curiosity Score™ and cliffhanger-without-commit.

**Kill:** Always-predict dogma / shame-reveal.  
**Survive:** Mode-conditional predict + destaked soft-wrong climate (SAFE-ERRCLIMATE).

- **Copy:** “Commit before the reveal — then keep the repair.” Never Curiosity Score™, wonder-kit ACT points, or lore cliffhangers without a math stake.

## CVII.13 Doctrine — SAFE-PREDICT (provisional)

1. **Gap before reveal** — open a knowledge gap the student cares about; do not collapse it with instant dump (Loewenstein; Kang).  
2. **Commit is the wick** — explicit prediction/answer before feedback; postdiction does not create the same surprise (Brod et al.).  
3. **Encode the repair** — curiosity without encoding the correct join is incomplete (Brod 2021 nuance; SAFE-EXPLAIN / soft-wrong contrast).  
4. **Demo/figure predict** — no passive spectacle pedagogy (Crouch et al.).  
5. **Mode gates** — soft-wrong / bridge / format / story-math stakes; soften under freeze and labeled prove-rail (SAFE-RETRIEVE / SAFE-FBTIME / SAFE-EXPTIME).  
6. **Destake the miss** — wrong predictions feed climate of diagnosis, not shame (SAFE-ERRCLIMATE).  
7. **Densify, don’t fork** — FBTIME delay, MONITOR prior judgment, MISCON productive wrong, PF generate→consolidate.  
8. **No Curiosity Score™ / cliffhanger-without-commit / postdiction theater / guess-for-XP / curiosity ACT ads**.

**Confidence:** High that information-gap curiosity and prediction-before-feedback are real levers (Loewenstein; Kang; Brod line; Crouch/Mazur). Medium that SPA soft-wrong predict gates will raise delayed transfer without quit — instrument PREDICT-* before curiosity marketing. High confidence to kill Curiosity Score™, cliffhanger-without-commit, and postdiction theater regardless.
