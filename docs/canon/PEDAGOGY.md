## 1. What we are optimizing for

**HYPOTHESIS (company):** Students become confident mathematical thinkers when three conditions compound:

1. **Affective permission** — math stops registering as social threat.
2. **Competence evidence** — undeniable, self-attributed successes at the right grain (including *why* and *which strategy*, not only accuracy).
3. **Identity re-storying** — “I’m not a math person” becomes a workable self-story that allows struggle.

**North Star metrics (ship these; Constitution SAFE-INSTRUMENT / Part XXI):**

- `retry_120s` — returns to the hard thing soon
- `challenge_accept` — takes the next stretch problem
- `transfer_pass` — succeeds on a varied / delayed item
- `solo_transfer_pass` — same without scaffolding

**Killed as North Stars:** DAU/streak/XP, “got it,” tip volume, immersion minutes, item-count, Belief/Identity/Fade/Struggle Scores™, ACT-point guarantees.

## 3. Deterministic spine, LLM bookends

**FOUNDER BELIEF → product law:** The engine owns structure (diagnosis, path, card selection, mastery updates). LLMs own language (classify, explain, wrap) under gates.

- Never let fluent AI write pedagogy structure (Bastani: unguarded chat can raise concurrent performance then tank solo exam — **FACT** directionally; category ≠ our ontology wrap).
- Coach wraps must be **principle-short** with QA gates (**SAFE-EXPLAINQA**). Fluency ≠ pedagogy.
- Generated bank items: **verify-before-ship**; drop-rate gate (**SAFE-GENQ**). Item-count heroes are banned (**SAFE-COVER**).

## 4. Diagnosis before practice

**Ontology as moat (SAFE-ONTOLOGY):** state × concept/ingredient graph × FEI × solo transfer — not “we have a graph file” or RAG chat.

**Gap scan (SAFE-COLD · SAFE-MONITOR · SAFE-CONFMISS):**

- Humble priors; labeled seed; real probes.
- Confidence judgments → **control** (what happens next), not a Monitoring Score™.
- Miss recovery forks by **confidence × mark type** (sure bug vs low-conf lack). No Identity Score™.
- Hide correctness on diagnostic probes when a wrong key would corrupt the graph (C4).

**Gaps to remediate (SAFE-BRIDGE · SAFE-FORMAT):**

- Bridge gaps (knows A and B, fails the join) outrank isolated node weakness.
- Format gaps (word / diagram / graph / symbolic…) need conversion evidence, not Format Personality™.

## 5. Practice design (desirable difficulty without cruelty)

Order of operations under anxiety (**SAFE-DD · SAFE-FADEX · SAFE-EXPOSE**):

1. **Equip** — enough structure to attempt
2. **Destake** — remove identity threat (no red X verdicts; soft-wrong taxonomy)
3. **Dose** — Bjork-style difficulty / interleave / spacing

**Soft-wrong science (SAFE-MISCON · SAFE-TAXON · SAFE-DISTRACTOR):**

- Typed wrongs beat “all reds equal.”
- MC distractors map to misconceptions, not beauty traps.
- Partial Credit Score™ theater is banned.

**Worked examples → solo (SAFE-FADE · SAFE-EXPTIME · SAFE-PF):**

- Completion ladder; fade to solo proof.
- Productive failure only with generate→consolidate fidelity — not fail-first cosplay.

**Help (SAFE-HELP · SAFE-HINT · SAFE-HWHELP):**

- Instrumental > executive.
- Soft peeks first; hard peeks cost construction.
- Solver (tonight) ≠ Practice (identity). Dual-rail; assisted ≠ ready.

**Spacing / forgetting (SAFE-SCHED · SAFE-FORGET · SAFE-ADAPT):**

- Horizon-matched ISI×RI; equal-ish lags over magic expanding SRS.
- Aged evidence + delayed probes; mastery fireworks ≠ durable competence.
- Calendar-first; banded inspectable adapt — no black-box “perfect interval” AI.

**Feedback timing (SAFE-FBTIME):** mode-conditional clocks; focus of feedback > instant vs delay ideology.

## 6. Story and identity (without load bombs)

**SAFE-STORYLOAD · SAFE-TRANSFER · SAFE-ANALOG · SAFE-PREDICT:**

- Stories wrap structure; they do not replace far-transfer design.
- Commit→surprise→encode beats cliffhanger entertainment.
- Analogies need co-present structure maps, not surface lore.

**Attribution language (Brand Book + SAFE-ATTRIBUTION lineage):**

- Never “wrong” / “incorrect” as person verdicts → **not yet / not this one**.
- Point at the specific thing to see differently; never bare “try again.”
- Never red X on student work for shame.

## 7. Tutors and humans

**SAFE-HITL · SAFE-TUTORGRAIN · SAFE-APPRENTICE · SAFE-MAPORCH · SAFE-TALK · SAFE-LOADSHED · SAFE-BURNWARN:**

- Hire and QA by **fidelity**, not Ivy résumé or Discord vibes.
- Session spine: Map brief → grain slice → adjust (not always-on full graph mid-attempt).
- Model → coach → fade; construction > talk-% theater.
- Exam week: acuity load-shed, not hours-booked crunch.
- Burnout early-warn from fidelity/load → org redesign, not wellness-primary Score™.

**AI vs human (SAFE-ROI · SAFE-REPAIR):** AI scaffolds; humans carry accountability and repair after hallucination. Never claim “tutoring is free” or “AI replaces tutors.”

---

## 8. Parents, exams, marketing honesty

**SAFE-PDASH · SAFE-SOFTMSG · SAFE-EXAM · SAFE-DURABLE · SAFE-WTP · SAFE-PROOF:**

- Parent messages: grain + one CTA after soft-wrong streaks — no Miss Streak / shame-push.
- Parent dashboard: proof-age and solo competence artifacts — not tonight-% shame ranks.
- Exam pressure ≠ learn affect; don’t day-one ACT flood cold-start students.
- Dual-rail GTM is fine; **no point guarantees** from packaging.
- Social proof = dated solo competence, not star walls.

Website product name: **The Desk by MindCraft**. Claims must be defendable under this file + Brand Book.

## 9. Privacy and affect

**SAFE-PRIVACY:** No emotion-recognition theater / Anxiety Score™ as product. Affective check-ins are opt-in, purpose-limited, and never substitute for pedagogy. Compliance is the floor, not the doctrine.

## 10. Agent checklist (before shipping a learning feature)

1. Which FEI metric moves if this works?
2. Which SAFE-* rule does it obey? Which killed Score™ does it accidentally recreate?
3. Does diagnosis stay engine-owned?
4. Does language avoid person-verdicts and red-X shame?
5. Is help instrumental, with a path to solo transfer?
6. Would Maya feel taken seriously — or remediated?

If unclear: open the Constitution chapter, not invent a new framework.
