## CXXX.1 Why this chapter exists

SAFE-HWHELP already forbids becoming a nicer Chegg: two labeled rails, instrumental default, solo proof as identity. SAFE-CRUNCHMSG already forbids selling infinite availability and fear-parent emails. What remains underspecified is the **button layer** — the actual CTAs students and parents see when the ACT calendar is loud and Sunday night is real.

Competitors resolve the button wrongly: (1) **single Chegg hero** (“Get the answer / Finish homework”); (2) **fear dump** (“ACT in 9 days — open Solver now”); (3) **purity freeze** (hide help, lose GTM); (4) **equal twin buttons** with no default (decision fatigue → dump); (5) **streak / tonight-%** as the CTA subcopy. MindCraft’s wedge is **dual CTAs with architecture**: help is available and labeled; Practice is visible and often the default or the mandatory follow-on; neither rail markets assisted completion as identity.

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** Crunch weeks are when dual-rail doctrine dies or becomes brand. If the primary CTA collapses to “help tonight” alone, MindCraft trains the market to buy relief and forget the gym.

**Claims we refuse as doctrine:**
1. Single Chegg-hero Solver CTA as crunch default.  
2. “Finish tonight’s homework” / tonight-% as primary brand CTA.  
3. Fear-appeal CTAs that couple exam countdown → answer dump.  
4. Dual-CTA Score™ / Help Tonight Minutes / Crunch Click NS.  
5. Silent collapse of the Practice rail when exam week starts.  
6. Assisted Solver success marketed as exam-ready / identity.  
7. Purity ban that removes instrumental help under deadline need.  
8. ACT / score guarantees from dual-rail CTA packaging.

## CXXX.3 Defaults are not neutral — crunch CTAs choose a future self

**FACT:** Johnson & Goldstein (2003, *Science, 302*(5649), 1338–1339, doi:10.1126/science.1091721) — organ-donation consent rates differ dramatically by **opt-in vs opt-out defaults** across European countries and in laboratory choice; every policy has a no-action default that imposes switching costs; defaults are not psychologically neutral.

**FACT:** Thaler, Sunstein, & Balz (2013, in E. Shafir, Ed., *The Behavioral Foundations of Public Policy*, pp. 428–439; earlier SSRN working paper “Choice Architecture”) — choice architects always set defaults, feedback, and option structure; a nudge alters behavior predictably without forbidding options or heavily changing economic incentives; ignoring architecture is still choosing one.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** A Solver screen whose only bold button is “Show solution / Finish homework” is an **opt-out-of-learning default**. Under crunch, inertia + deadline anxiety make the dump path the organ-donation analogue of sticking with the pre-checked box. SAFE-CRUNCHCTA treats the primary CTA as a **fiduciary default**: instrumental help remains available (not forbidden), but the architecture must not silently enroll Maya in executive outsourcing.

**Kill:** Single dump button as “what parents want this week.”  
**Survive:** Dual CTAs + deliberate default / primacy toward gym or labeled pair with follow-on.  
**Wound:** Organ-donation ethics ≠ homework UX — transfer *default power*, not moral theater; CRUNCHCTA-* must measure quit and help-avoidance, not claim Science *d* as ACT points.

## CXXX.4 Executive vs instrumental help — the CTA verb is the pedagogy

**FACT:** Nelson-Le Gall (1981, *Developmental Review, 1*(3), 224–246, doi:10.1016/0273-2297(81)90019-8) — distinguishes **executive help** (have the helper solve / produce the answer) from **instrumental help** (help that enables the learner to complete the task themselves); adaptive help-seeking is metacognitive and contingent, not mere dependency.

**FACT:** Bastani, Bastani, Sungu, Ge, Kabakcı, & Mariman (2025, *PNAS, 122*(26), e2422633122, doi:10.1073/pnas.2422633122) — field experiment (~1,000 high-school math students): unfettered GPT-4 “Base” access raised practice performance but **harmed** later solo performance (−17% vs never-access); safeguarded “Tutor” largely mitigated harm — design of the help interface (not mere AI presence) determines whether help is a crutch.

**Applied (FOUNDER BELIEF → testable):** CTA copy that says “Get the answer / AI solves it / Finish my worksheet” recruits **executive** help-seeking. Copy that says “Unstick with a hint / Next step only / Then prove it in Practice” recruits **instrumental** help plus a gym handoff. Crunch week does not change the distinction — it increases the temptation to market the executive verb.

**Kill:** “Answer now” / Chegg-clone verbs as hero CTA.  
**Survive:** Instrumental verbs + Bastani-style guardrails on the help rail.  
**Wound:** Bastani classroom ≠ MindCraft parent Sunday night — still refuse Base-as-hero; instrument CRUNCHCTA-*.

## CXXX.5 Fear-appeal CTAs poison the dual rail

**FACT:** Putwain & Remedios (2014, *School Psychology Quarterly, 29*(4), 360–371, doi:10.1037/spq0000048) — teacher fear appeals before high-stakes exams (messages emphasizing failure consequences) relate to **lower self-determined motivation** and **lower exam performance**, partly mediated by motivation; scare tactics are not reliable motivational tools.

**FACT:** Putwain & Best (2011, *Learning and Individual Differences, 21*(5), 580–584, doi:10.1016/j.lindif.2011.07.007) — classroom fear appeals before tests associated with higher state anxiety and worse test performance in a counterbalanced design.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Parent/student CTAs of the form “ACT in 8 days — if you don’t open Solver you’ll freeze / fail” import classroom fear appeals into product chrome. Under SAFE-CRUNCHMSG / SAFE-SOFTMSG, crunch CTAs must be **challenge-structured** (named plan, two rails, one next action) — not threat-coupled dump. Fear copy also collapses the dual rail psychologically: Practice reads as “extra,” Solver reads as “survival.”

**Kill:** Countdown → dump as default creative.  
**Survive:** “Exam week: unstick tonight *or* prove a grain alone — we protect thinking minutes either way.”  
**Wound:** Putwain teacher speech ≠ push notification — still ban fear-hero CTAs pending CRUNCHCTA-QUAL.

## CXXX.8 Product surface — SAFE-CRUNCHCTA claim contract

1. **Home / crunch card:** Two CTAs — Help tonight (instrumental subcopy) + Practice gym (prove-alone subcopy).  
2. **Solver open:** No “show full solution” as first primary; fade/hint ladder; after unstick, **one-tap Practice grain** for the same edge.  
3. **Parent crunch message:** Dual-rail verbs; protected thinking minutes; never tonight-% stalk (SAFE-PDASH / CRUNCHMSG).  
4. **Default rule (HYPOTHESIS):** If acuity = due-HW triage → help rail primary *with* follow-on Practice chip; if acuity = FEI / exam durable → Practice primary, help secondary. Never dump-only.  
5. **Telemetry:** `rail_chosen`, `cta_default_shown`, `followon_start`, FEI events — demote Help Tonight Minutes.  
6. **Copy bank (examples):** “Unstuck tonight ≠ ready for the test.” / “Help to move; Practice to prove.” / “Exam week we still show both doors.”

## CXXX.9 Doctrine — SAFE-CRUNCHCTA (provisional)

1. **Two visible rails under crunch** — never silently drop Practice for exam month.  
2. **Instrumental verbs only on help CTAs** — ban “get the answer / finish worksheet” heroes (Nelson-Le Gall; Bastani).  
3. **Defaults are fiduciary** — dump must not be the no-action / primary enrollment path (Johnson & Goldstein; Thaler et al.).  
4. **Follow-on Practice grain** after help when feasible — dual rail is a *path*, not two SKUs.  
5. **No fear-appeal CTAs** — challenge structure over countdown dump (Putwain).  
6. **Parent honesty** — dual-rail copy; no tonight-% / live stalk (SAFE-CRUNCHMSG / SAFE-SOFTMSG).  
7. **No Dual-CTA Score™ / Help Tonight Minutes / Crunch Click NS**.  
8. **Copy:** “Help tonight or prove it in Practice — crunch week still shows both, and help never means ‘we did it for you.’” Never Chegg hero or ACT guarantees from CTA packaging.

**Confidence:** High on method sources (defaults; executive/instrumental help; Bastani guardrails; fear-appeal harms). Medium on MindCraft-specific default primacy rules and follow-on compliance rates (needs CRUNCHCTA-*). High that single Chegg-hero / fear-dump / Dual-CTA Score™ / assisted≡ready are commercially toxic under FEI + HWHELP.
