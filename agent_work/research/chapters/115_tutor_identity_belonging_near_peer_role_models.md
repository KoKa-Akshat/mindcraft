## CXV.1 Why this chapter exists

Parents ask for tutors who “look like” Maya, share her language, or “get” being the only girl in AP Calc. Growth wants representation photos. Ops already defaults to **trained near-peers** (SAFE-TUTORGRAIN). The commercial trap is **identity theater**: splash-page diversity without playbook fidelity, forced demographic match with unattainable superstars, or empty praise that skips recognition-of-competence (SAFE-PROOF / SAFE-GENDER).

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** Near-peer tutors can inoculate belonging *when* competence is visible and success feels attainable — but matching demographics without FEI structure is costume, not pedagogy.

**Claims we refuse as doctrine:** Representation Score™ / Diversity Minutes NS; token STEM costume ≡ belonging; demographic match ≡ tutoring quality; celebrity unmatched role models as default; empty “math girl/boy like you” praise ≡ recognition; Belonging Score™ NS; we-close-the-STEM-gap from matching ads; soft-standards for matched pairs.

## CXV.2 Constructs (model ≠ mentor ≠ match ≠ mastery)

Keep distinct: **role model** (success updates “people like me can”), **mentor** (ongoing scaffold — Dennehy & Dasgupta), **match** (assignment heuristic), **mastery evidence** (solo transfer / FEI — not likeness). Belonging = membership/acceptance in the domain (Good, Rattan, & Dweck), not a Diversity Minutes counter.

**Operational definition (HYPOTHESIS):** SAFE-ROLEMODEL compliant when (a) hire still passes SAFE-TUTORGRAIN fidelity drills, (b) bios show **attainable near-peer competence**, (c) optional preference matching without outcome promises, (d) recognition is practice-based (SAFE-PROOF), (e) no Representation Score™ / costume-only marketing / match-waives-challenge.

## CXV.3 Stereotype inoculation — contact with same-group experts can buffer STEM self-concept

**FACT (stereotype inoculation model):** Stout, Dasgupta, Hunsinger, & McManus (2011, *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 100*(2), 255–270, doi:10.1037/a0021385) — experiments + longitudinal calculus: exposure to **female STEM experts** promoted more positive implicit STEM attitudes and stronger implicit identification; subjective identification with experts predicted self-efficacy, domain identification, and STEM career commitment. Benefits held even while stereotypes remained active.

**FACT (policy synthesis):** Dasgupta & Stout (2014, *Policy Insights from the Behavioral and Brain Sciences, 1*(1), 21–29, doi:10.1177/2372732214549471) — access to same-sex STEM role models/mentors is a recurring lever for girls’/women’s STEM self-concept; faculty scarcity is structural, not student-deficit.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Near-peer tutors can act as **ingroup experts** only if students identify with them and see domain competence. Stock photos are not inoculation.

**Kill:** Costume representation without identification pathway.  
**Survive:** Visible near-peer experts as optional belonging inoculators *inside* fidelity ops.

## CXV.4 Competence of the model matters more than mere presence

**FACT (female role models under threat):** Marx & Roman (2002, *Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin, 28*(9), 1183–1193, doi:10.1177/01461672022812004) — three studies: women’s math performance under stereotype-relevant conditions was **protected** when a **competent** female experimenter administered the test or when women learned about her math competence; Study 2 showed **perception of competence**, not physical presence alone, safeguarded performance; Study 3: competence information buffered self-appraised math ability, which predicted successful challenging-test performance.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Tutor bios and first-session cues should foreground **math competence + near-peer attainability** (courses recently survived, Map diagnosis skill), not demographic checkboxes alone. An incompetent same-group tutor can fail Marx’s mechanism and still violate SAFE-TUTORGRAIN.

**Kill:** Presence-as-enough / “someone who looks like you” without competence signal.  
**Survive:** Competence-visible models; perception of competence as the buffer ingredient.

---

## CXV.5 Attainability — superstars can inspire or demoralize

**FACT (superstars and the self):** Lockwood & Kunda (1997, *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 73*(1), 91–103, doi:10.1037/0022-3514.73.1.91) — relevant outstanding role models provoke **self-enhancement and inspiration when success seems attainable** (time remaining; malleable ability beliefs) but **self-deflation when success seems unattainable** (missed window; fixed ability). Domain relevance is required for impact.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Default marketing should prefer **near-peer ladders** (“I took Calc last year; here’s how I recovered from blanking on systems”) over Ivy-Olympiad cosplay that makes Maya’s success feel closed. Densifies SAFE-TUTORGRAIN (near-peer default) and SAFE-EXPECTANCY (no trait genius theater).

**Kill:** Unattainable superstar default; genius-résumé dump as belonging.  
**Survive:** Attainable near-peer stories; growth-framed path (Lockwood attainability + Good et al. growth messages below).

## CXV.6 Peer mentors at transition — belonging and retention, not grades-as-everything

**FACT (field RCT):** Dennehy & Dasgupta (2017, *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 114*(23), 5964–5969, doi:10.1073/pnas.1613117114) — incoming women engineering students (n = 150) randomly assigned female peer mentor, male peer mentor, or none for one year; **female (not male) mentors** protected belonging, self-efficacy, motivation, major retention, and career aspirations; benefits endured a year after mentoring ended. **Year-1 grades were not associated with retention**; belonging and self-efficacy were.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Near-peer matching can matter for **identity persistence** beyond session accuracy — especially at transitions. Do not read as “grades don’t matter”; read as “retention levers include belonging.” Mentors were **trained peers in a structured program**, not Discord buddies (SAFE-CoP / SAFE-TUTORGRAIN).

**Kill:** Grade-only parent dash as the whole retention story; untrained peer chat ≡ mentoring RCT.  
**Survive:** Structured near-peer mentoring as belonging densifier; instrument FEI persistence, not Belonging Score™.

## CXV.7 Belonging in math — environment messages, not posters

**FACT (sense of belonging to math):** Good, Rattan, & Dweck (2012, *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 102*(4), 700–717, doi:10.1037/a0026659) — validated belonging-to-math scale predicts intent to pursue math; longitudinal calculus: messages that math ability is **fixed** plus gender stereotypes eroded women’s (not men’s) belonging; belonging mediated future intent and grades; **growth messages** protected belonging under stereotype.

**FACT (social belonging intervention):** Walton & Cohen (2011, *Science, 331*(6023), 1447–1451, doi:10.1126/science.1198364) — brief social-belonging intervention framing adversity as common and transient raised African American students’ GPA over years and halved the minority achievement gap in their sample; mechanism = preventing adversity from being read as an indictment of belonging.

**FACT (cultural stereotypes as gatekeepers):** Cheryan, Master, & Meltzoff (2015, *Frontiers in Psychology, 6*, Article 49, doi:10.3389/fpsyg.2015.00049) — stereotypes of who belongs in CS/engineering (male-oriented, isolating, brilliance) reduce girls’ belonging and interest; **broadening representation of people, work, and environments** increases belonging/interest.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Tutor identity work sits inside **cue hygiene** (SAFE-GENDER / SAFE-ELL): growth-framed briefs, non-stereotypical story worlds, adversity-as-common onboarding — not a pink tutor SKU. Walton-style belonging messages are **brief construal interventions**, not Diversity Minutes dashboards.

**Kill:** Belonging Score™ NS; stereotype-heavy “genius tutor” brand; pink-track matching.  
**Survive:** Growth + common-adversity framing; diversified who-represents-math cues; practice recognition over posters.

## CXV.8 Product law — hire fidelity first, then optional identity match

**HYPOTHESIS (SAFE-ROLEMODEL stack):**

1. **Hire bar unchanged** — SAFE-TUTORGRAIN fidelity drills before any matching preference.  
2. **Competence-visible bios** — recent path, Map/playbook skill, struggle→recovery; Marx competence signal.  
3. **Attainable near-peer default** — Lockwood: avoid demoralizing superstar cosplay in default cards.  
4. **Optional preference, not outcome promise** — offer gender/language/background preference where supply allows; never claim match ⇒ ACT points or “we close gaps.”  
5. **Recognition = competence evidence** — session praise cites attempts/transfer (SAFE-PROOF), not identity slogans.  
6. **Brief belonging construal** — adversity-common, growth-capable (Walton; Good et al.); no mandatory identity confession.  
7. **Cue hygiene** — Cheryan: who represents the field in photos/story/tutor cards; ban single-mold “math person.”  
8. **Densify** — SAFE-GENDER / ELL / TUTORGRAIN / HITL / EXPECTANCY / CoP / IDMEASURE.  
9. **Ban** Representation Score™ / Diversity Minutes / costume≡belonging / match≡quality / empty likeness praise / Belonging Score™ / matching ACT guarantees.

**Kill:** Match-first marketplace; tokenism marketing.  
**Survive:** Fidelity-first roster with optional inoculating matches and attainable bios.

## CXV.13 Doctrine — SAFE-ROLEMODEL (provisional)

1. **Fidelity before likeness** — SAFE-TUTORGRAIN hire bar never waived for demographic match.  
2. **Competence must be perceptible** — Marx & Roman: competence signal, not presence alone.  
3. **Prefer attainable near-peers** — Lockwood & Kunda: inspiration requires attainable success.  
4. **Ingroup experts can inoculate** — Stout / Dasgupta line; optional same-group exposure as belonging support.  
5. **Structured peer mentoring ≠ chat** — Dennehy & Dasgupta: trained mentors; belonging/retention levers beyond year-1 grades.  
6. **Belonging via environment messages** — Good et al. growth vs fixed+stereotype; Walton adversity-as-common; Cheryan cue diversification.  
7. **Recognition is practice evidence** — SAFE-PROOF / SAFE-GENDER; no empty “math person like you.”  
8. **Densify, don’t fork** — SAFE-GENDER / ELL / TUTORGRAIN / HITL / EXPECTANCY / CoP / IDMEASURE.  
9. **No Representation Score™ / Diversity Minutes NS / costume≡belonging / match≡quality / Belonging Score™ / matching ACT guarantees**.

**Confidence:** High that costume-only matching and unattainable-star marketing are unsafe vs belonging+FEI; high that competence-visible near-peers are the right default grain; medium on how strongly HS tutoring preference-match replicates college engineering mentor RCTs — instrument ROLEMODEL-* before hard match SLAs; high to kill Representation Score™ / match≡quality / empty likeness praise regardless.
