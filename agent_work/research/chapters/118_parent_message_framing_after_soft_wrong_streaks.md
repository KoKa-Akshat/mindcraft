## CXVIII.1 Why this chapter exists

SAFE-PDASH already kills tonight-% / streak / shame-rank parent portals. SAFE-FBLIT already kills delivery≡uptake. The remaining commercial hazard is the **message after a soft-wrong streak**: Maya misses three destaked items; the product must tell a paying parent something true without (a) converting soft-wrong into a **shame telemetry product**, (b) pushing controlling “sit with her until it’s fixed” scripts that transmit parent math anxiety (Maloney; Retanal), or (c) shipping empty growth-mindset posters that change parent beliefs but not practices (MacDonald et al.).

Competitors resolve this wrongly: gradebook apps push miss counts and ranks; “encouragement” apps send “mistakes are good!” with no next grain; homework-stalk apps invite parents to watch every wrong live; mindset kits sell belief change as the product.

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** Soft-wrong is for the learner’s climate. Parent copy after soft-wrong must be **failure-enhancing + autonomy-supportive + one named return** — not a correctness scoreboard and not a vibe.

**Claims we refuse as doctrine:**
1. Soft-wrong streak push / Miss Streak as parent North Star.  
2. Live wrong-answer stalk digests.  
3. Shame/guilt SMS (“she’s falling behind”) after destaked practice.  
4. Empty “mistakes are good!” / growth-mindset poster without named next attempt.  
5. Controlling “fix it tonight / check every problem” scripts as default parent help.  
6. Parent Correctness Score™ / Soft-Wrong Score™ vanity metrics.  
7. Growth-mindset parent letter ≡ FEI / ACT lift.  
8. ACT-point guarantees from parent messaging kits.

**Operational definition (HYPOTHESIS):** A parent message after soft-wrong is *SAFE-SOFTMSG complete* when it (a) **names grain** (concept / ingredient / FormatId / bridge — not a vibe), (b) states that the miss is **usable information** under destake (failure-enhancing, not ability verdict), (c) offers **one** autonomy-supportive next attempt or return mission (SAFE-PDASH improvement CTA), (d) does **not** expose live item keys, ranks, or miss-streak vanity, (e) steers anxious parents away from controlling takeover toward structure + invitation (Retanal; Jiang), and (f) never claims messaging alone raises ACT scores.

## CXVIII.3 Intrusive involvement hurts math — especially under control

**FACT (math-specific meta):** Jiang, Shi, Zheng, & Mao (2023, *Frontiers in Psychology, 14*, 1218534, doi:10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1218534; N≈16,338) — supportive parental homework involvement (SPI) links weakly positively to math achievement (r≈0.076); **intrusive involvement (IPI) links negatively** (r≈−0.153). SPI effects strongest when measured as **autonomy support**; IPI harm strongest in high school.

**FACT (affective homework climate):** Wu, Barger, Oh, & Pomerantz (2022, *Child Development, 93*(5), 1347–1364, doi:10.1111/cdev.13774) — parents’ math homework involvement is more affectively negative than math activities; **more negative homework affect** predicts poorer later math motivation and achievement.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Soft-wrong streak digests that spike parental anxiety and invite intrusive checking recreate the IPI path. Commercial default must be **autonomy-supportive structure**, not frequency-of-help or live monitoring.

**Kill:** Live wrong-answer stalk; “check every miss tonight” as product default.  
**Survive:** Sparse, grain-named, improvement-framed digests (Kraft & Rogers lineage via SAFE-PDASH).

## CXVIII.4 Math-anxious parents + controlling help transmit harm

**FACT (intergenerational homework channel):** Maloney, Ramirez, Gunderson, Levine, & Beilock (2015, *Psychological Science, 26*(9), 1480–1488, doi:10.1177/0956797615592630) — when parents are more math-anxious, children learn less math and show more math anxiety **only if** those parents report **frequent math homework help**; less-frequent help breaks the association; reading achievement unaffected.

**FACT (mechanism = controlling style):** Retanal, Johnston, Di Lonardo Burr, Storozuk, DiStefano, & Maloney (2021, *Education Sciences, 11*(10), 620, doi:10.3390/educsci11100620) — parents’ math anxiety relates to both autonomy- and controlling-supportive styles; **controlling-supportive help partially mediates** the anxiety→child achievement link; autonomy support did not mediate in their path model.

**Applied (FOUNDER BELIEF → testable):** After soft-wrong streaks, MindCraft must **not** recruit math-anxious parents into more frequent controlling help. Message should reduce pressure (“you do not need to re-teach every miss”) and point to Map grain / tutor / next solo attempt.

**Kill:** Parent-as-substitute-teacher after every soft-wrong.  
**Survive:** “Name the join; invite her next attempt; escalate to tutor/Map — don’t take the pencil.”

## CXVIII.5 Uninvited help + fixed mindset = sharper achievement drag

**FACT (moderation):** Park, Gunderson, Maloney, Tsukayama, Beilock, Duckworth, & Levine (2023, *Developmental Psychology, 59*(7), 1249–1267, doi:10.1037/dev0001522) — across early elementary (N=563) and secondary (N=1,613) samples, **intrusive homework support more strongly predicted achievement decline for children with fixed mindsets**.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Soft-wrong streak SMS that imply “she can’t” plus invite unsolicited takeover are especially toxic for fixed-mindset Maya. Parent copy must avoid ability verdicts and uninvited monitoring — even when the digest is “honest.”

**Kill:** Ability-framed miss digests (“shows she’s not ready”).  
**Survive:** Process/grain frame + invited support only.

## CXVIII.6 Failure mindsets beat intelligence-mindset posters

**FACT (visible failure beliefs):** Haimovitz & Dweck (2016, *Psychological Science, 27*(6), 859–869, doi:10.1177/0956797616639727) — parents’ **failure mindsets** (failure-as-enhancing vs debilitating) predict parenting practices and children’s intelligence mindsets more than parents’ own intelligence mindsets; children accurately perceive failure mindsets; parents who see failure as debilitating focus on performance/ability rather than learning.

**FACT (belief change ≠ practice change):** MacDonald, Oh, Barger, Cimpian, & Pomerantz (2024, *Developmental Psychology*, doi:10.1037/dev0001768) — inducing growth-oriented math mindsets in parents **sustained parent belief change** but **did not improve math parenting practices or children’s math adjustment** relative to an informative active control (Common Core information). Belief kits alone are not the product.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Parent soft-wrong copy should operationalize **failure-is-enhancing** as a concrete next attempt (“this miss maps the bridge to retry”), not as a poster slogan — and must not treat mindset induction as FEI proof.

**Kill:** Growth-mindset parent letter as learning NS; empty “mistakes are good!” without grain+CTA.  
**Survive:** Failure-enhancing + named return (SAFE-FBLIT uptake for the household).

**Wound:** Haimovitz/Dweck are not MindCraft soft-wrong RCTs — SOFTMSG-* must test digest frames in-product.

## CXVIII.7 Soft-wrong is learner climate — parent honesty still needs a contract

**Reuse (doctrine, not new FACT):** Soft-wrong / hide-correctness protects diagnostic integrity and destakes threat (SAFE-MISCON / SAFE-ERRCLIMATE / SAFE-CALIB). That does **not** license silence to parents (SAFE-PDASH honesty) nor license converting soft-wrong into a private gradebook.

**HYPOTHESIS — message tiers:**
1. **Default digest (sparse):** proof-age / MoC + one soft-wrong grain summary (“three attempts showed a FormatId gap on linear systems”) + one return CTA.  
2. **No live stalk:** no item keys, no real-time wrong feed, no Miss Streak counter as hero.  
3. **Anxious-parent mode:** extra line reducing controlling pressure; invite tutor/Map; ban “sit until correct.”  
4. **Exam-rail exception:** prove-rail KR honesty differs from learn-rail soft-wrong (SAFE-EXAM / SAFE-FBTIME) — label the rail so parents don’t treat destaked practice as ACT readiness.

**Kill:** Soft-wrong ≡ secret from parents; soft-wrong ≡ parent miss leaderboard.  
**Survive:** Honest grain without shame chrome.

## CXVIII.9 Doctrine — SAFE-SOFTMSG (provisional)

1. **Grain before vibe** — name concept/ingredient/FormatId/bridge; no fog praise.  
2. **Failure-enhancing, not ability verdict** — miss = next join information (Haimovitz & Dweck); never “she can’t.”  
3. **One improvement CTA** — autonomy-supportive return mission (SAFE-PDASH); no CTA spam.  
4. **Autonomy > control** — tip language invites attempt; bans uninvited takeover (Jiang; Retanal; Park).  
5. **Do not recruit anxious parents into more homework control** — reduce pressure; route diagnosis to Map/tutor (Maloney).  
6. **No miss-streak / live-wrong hero** — honesty without shame telemetry.  
7. **Belief posters ≠ product** — mindset letters without practice change are incomplete (MacDonald et al.).  
8. **Copy:** “A miss showed the next join — here’s one return when she’s ready.” Never guilt streaks; never empty “mistakes are magic”; never ACT guarantees from texts.

**Confidence:** High — IPI hurts math more than SPI helps (Jiang); frequent help from math-anxious parents transmits harm (Maloney); controlling style mediates (Retanal); intrusive help × fixed mindset worsens drag (Park); failure mindsets shape children’s beliefs (Haimovitz & Dweck); parent growth-mindset induction alone often fails to move practices/child outcomes (MacDonald). Medium — exact soft-wrong digest wording and cadence (needs SOFTMSG-*). High — Miss Streak / live stalk / guilt SMS as parent NS are commercially toxic under this stack.

## CXVIII.11 So what for MindCraft commercially

- **Copy:** “A miss showed the next join — here’s one return when she’s ready.” Lead with grain + invitation, not guilt streaks or poster slogans.  
- **Product:** Soft-wrong parent digest = named grain + failure-enhancing frame + one CTA + optional anti-control tip; no live wrong feed; no Miss Streak hero.  
- **Positioning:** Against gradebook stalk, mindset-poster kits, and nag SMS edtech; for honest household feedback literacy.  
- **Metric:** `parent_softmsg_open`, `parent_return_cta_start`, intrusion-tip exposure, return→FEI co-gates — demote Parent Correctness Score™ / Miss Streak NS.  
- **Kill list:** Shame soft-wrong pushes; live stalk; controlling default scripts; empty mistake-magic; ACT guarantees from parent texts.  
- **Growth:** Parent decks sell *clarity without surveillance*; tutors brief parents on invite-not-takeover after soft-wrong weeks (SAFE-HITL).
