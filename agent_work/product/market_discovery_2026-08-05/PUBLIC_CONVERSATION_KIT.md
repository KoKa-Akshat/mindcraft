# Public conversation kit — MindCraft field discovery

**Why this exists:** Research lab still needs **10 Maya + 10 parent interviews** (`NEXT_LAB.md`). Your BETA showcase **Math Help Snapshot / Pulse** already proved a good public instrument. This kit upgrades that form into something you can run **in a college town** (cafés, library, student union, parents’ weekend, tutoring centers) without sounding like a clipboard survey — and without breaking research doctrine (SAFE-WTP, SAFE-PDASH, buyer ≠ user).

**Sources this extends (do not reinvent):**
- Showcase card: front Snapshot + optional Pulse back (this folder)
- Constitution **Appendix B** — Maya interview protocol
- Part **XXVII** — parent: what did you buy / what would make you cancel
- Part **LIX / SAFE-WTP** — no Likert dollars; choice + real next step
- Part **LXXXIII / SAFE-PDASH** — proof age / relief, not surveillance
- Part **LXXI / SAFE-TUTORGRAIN** — near-peer vs expert hire language (campus tutors)

---

## 0. How you stand out in public (30 seconds)

You are **not** selling. You are running a **listening desk**.

**Opener (say out loud):**
> “I’m building a math map that shows the exact gaps kids get stuck on — not another homework app. Got 90 seconds for a raffle card? No pitch. Or eight minutes if you’ve got a story.”

**Props that beat a clipboard:**
1. One printed **Snapshot** card (raffle optional).
2. Phone notes open to the **8-minute script** for their role.
3. Optional: one blank “Map of stuck spots” sticky — they circle Algebra / Word problems / Tests / Confidence (visual, memorable).

**Never lead with:** AI, streaks, guaranteed ACT points, “identity transformation,” or “how much would you pay?”

**College-town twist:** You will meet **four roles** more than Edina parents alone:
| Role | Why they’re gold here | Route below |
|------|----------------------|-------------|
| Parent / guardian (visiting or local) | Buyer | Card → Parent 8-min → schedule 25-min |
| High-school student (sibling/friend) | User (Maya) | Card → Maya 8-min → schedule Appendix B |
| College student who tutors / ed major | Near-peer supply + mirror of Maya | Tutor grain card |
| Teacher / coach / advisor | Trust + distribution | Teacher card |

Tag every note: `role · town · date · length (90s / 8m / 25m) · contact Y/N`.

## 2. Tier B — 8-minute street / coffee chat (stand-out layer)

Use when they finish the card and say “I have a minute.” Pick **one** role script. Phone voice memo OK if they consent (“Can I keep notes so I don’t butcher your words?”).

### Shared warm-up (all roles, 60s)
1. “What’s your connection to high-school math right now?”  
2. “Who actually feels the pain day to day — and who would pay if something worked?”

### B1 — Parent / guardian (buyer) — Part XXVII + SAFE-PDASH

**Job:** Learn cancel triggers, proof they trust, and what “relief” means — not a price number.

1. “Walk me through the last time math stressed the house. What happened that night?”  
2. “What have you already tried (tutor, app, YouTube, teacher)? What failed — specifically?”  
3. “If something worked for two weeks, what would you *see* that made you trust it?”  
   *(Listen for: fewer fights, asks for help, test calm, clear next topic — Pulse section B.)*  
4. “What would make you cancel or ghost a math program after a month?”  
5. **Choice, not Likert (SAFE-WTP):**  
   “If you had to pick one — (A) exact gap map + short weekly parent note with ‘what’s due to return,’ or (B) unlimited AI chat that answers homework, or (C) more human tutor hours with no map — which, and why? You can say none.”  
6. Close: “Want a 20-minute follow-up when we have a parent preview? Email/text?”

**Ban:** “How much would you pay?” / guaranteed points / live wrong-answer feed as a selling point.

### B2 — Student / Maya (user) — Appendix B compressed

**Job:** Map when math feels dangerous vs when competence felt real.

1. “Tell me about a time math felt dangerous or humiliating.”  
2. “What did your body do? What did you do next?”  
3. “Tell me about a time you surprised yourself in math. Did anyone see it?”  
4. “When you’re stuck, do you want comfort, a hint, or the answer — in what order?”  
5. “If an app celebrated you, would that feel real or fake? Why?”  
6. “What would make you take a harder math class next year?”  
7. Close: optional schedule full Appendix B (15–25 min).

**Coding:** threat markers · witness · wants comfort/hint/answer order · fake praise detectors.

### B3 — College tutor / ed major / near-peer (SAFE-TUTORGRAIN)

**Job:** Hire-bar language + what “good session” means on campus.

1. “Who do you tutor, and what do they actually struggle with — concept, format, or panic?”  
2. “When a student says ‘I get it’ in session, how often is that real vs polite?”  
3. “What would you want on a one-page brief before a session so you don’t re-explain the wrong thing?”  
4. “Parents vs students: who do you feel accountable to, and what do they ask for?”  
5. **Choice:** “Better tutor = (A) stronger credentials, (B) better diagnosis map, (C) warmer vibe — rank 1–2–3.”  
6. Close: “Open to a paid pilot session later this term?”

### B4 — Teacher / advisor / coach

1. “Where do you see kids lose the thread — missing old skills, word problems, test transfer?”  
2. “What do parents ask you for that you can’t give at scale?”  
3. “What would a useful outside tool show you in one screen — without becoming another gradebook?”  
4. “Hard no: what would make you refuse to recommend something to families?”

## 3. Tier C — 25-minute scheduled interview (the real research n=10)

Book these. Cards and 8-min chats are **recruitment + theme discovery**; Tier C fills the Constitution queue.

### C1 — Full Maya (Appendix B + two add-ons)

Run Appendix B questions 1–7 verbatim, then:

8. “When practice feels like training vs attack — what was different?” (SAFE-DD / Part XLI)  
9. “Have you ever known a topic on homework but missed it when the question looked different?” (transfer / format — SAFE-FORMAT)

### C2 — Full parent (XXVII + LIX message probe)

1. What did you think you bought last time you paid for math help?  
2. What would make you cancel?  
3. Show or describe three parent-update styles (no product UI required):  
   - (A) Tonight % / streak  
   - (B) “Last proved 12 days ago · return due before Saturday · one mission”  
   - (C) Classmate rank heatmap  
   Ask: which builds trust / which feels like surveillance / which would you pay to keep.  
4. Cheap-talk (SAFE-WTP): “We’re not setting price today. If a pilot opened at a real monthly fee for diagnosis + weekly honest note + limited human minutes, would you (a) join waitlist with email, (b) want a free month first, (c) not interested?”  
5. Structural check (SAFE-STRUCTURE): time, devices, quiet space, money stress — anything that kills “just practice more”?

### C3 — Tutor grain deep dive

Expand B3 with: pour vs prompt habits, wait time, what “quality held for a semester” means (SAFE-WORKFORCE / SAFE-TALK).

## 6. Hard bans (say these to yourself before every shift)

- No “How much would you pay?” as the money question → use **choice** or waitlist.  
- No “We’ll raise ACT by N points.”  
- No selling emotion AI / live stalk / Family Leaderboard.  
- No counting street cards as “interviews completed” toward the 10+10.  
- No pitching the Map until Tier C close — listening first.
