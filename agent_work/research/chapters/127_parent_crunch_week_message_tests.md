## CXXVII.1 Why this chapter exists

SAFE-LOADSHED already says: publish what pauses so FEI minutes survive; kill infinite-availability cosplay. SAFE-WTP already says: CBC over Likert WTP. LOADSHED-5 named the fork — “protect thinking minutes” vs “24/7 availability.” What remains underspecified is the **message + choice experiment contract**: which words open wallets without training parents to expect pour-mode human Chegg, and which words survive when sessions are deferred, capped, or AI-first.

Competitors resolve the fork wrongly: (1) **always-on brand**; (2) **fear-appeal crunch**; (3) **hours theater**; (4) **wellness fog**; (5) **abandonment silence** (shed without explaining why). MindCraft’s wedge is **expectation-aligned honesty**: name protected behaviors (thinking minutes, micro-debrief, bridge work) and paused electives, then prove parents will pay — or kill the doctrine.

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** Parents will trade some raw access for **credible protection of learning quality** when the trade-off is visible — if copy is plain, the pause list is real, and FEI evidence remains visible. If 24/7 always wins CBC *and* later churn when fidelity collapses, refuse pour-mode anyway and redesign packaging (AI-rail clarity, not fake human infinite).

**Claims we refuse as doctrine:**
1. 24/7 / infinite-availability as default parent value prop.  
2. Hours-booked / “we took every call” as crunch-week North Star.  
3. Likert “would you pay for thinking minutes?” as serious WTP.  
4. Fear-appeal parent emails as default motivation (“or they fail”).  
5. Availability Score™ / Crunch Message Score™ / Open-Late Minutes NS.  
6. Shed-without-explanation (honesty that feels like ghosting).  
7. Soft standards / answer-dump sold as “exam support.”  
8. ACT / score guarantees from crunch messaging or late-night packaging.

## CXXVII.3 Expectation gaps punish overpromise — availability ads set the wrong bar

**FACT (gaps model):** Parasuraman, Zeithaml, & Berry (1985, *Journal of Marketing, 49*(4), 41–50, doi:10.1177/002224298504900403) — service quality problems arise from gaps between consumer expectations and management perceptions, specs, delivery, and communications; external communications that raise expectations beyond delivery capacity widen the quality gap.

**FACT (SERVQUAL):** Parasuraman, Zeithaml, & Berry (1988, *Journal of Retailing, 64*(1), 12–40) — service quality is commonly assessed as the gap between expectations and perceived performance across reliability, responsiveness, assurance, empathy, and tangibles; **reliability** (doing what was promised) is structurally central.

**FACT (disconfirmation → satisfaction):** Oliver (1980, *Journal of Marketing Research, 17*(4), 460–469, doi:10.1177/002224378001700405) — satisfaction follows from expectation and expectancy disconfirmation; over-raised expectations that performance cannot meet predict dissatisfaction and weaker purchase intention cascades.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** “24/7 tutors / always book tonight” is an **expectation-raising communication** (Gap 4 in the gaps model). Under SAFE-LOADSHED, delivery *must* constrain access. The commercial question is not whether to constrain — it is whether to **advertise constraint-as-care** or advertise infinity and then disconfirm. SAFE-CRUNCHMSG prefers under-promise on raw access and over-deliver on **reliability of the learning method** (attempts happen, soft-wrongs typed, micro-debrief protected).

**Kill:** Infinite-availability brand that ops cannot keep in Protect+probe.  
**Survive:** Crunch copy that states response windows, acuity rules, and what parents can still expect tonight (Practice + coach / AI rail / scheduled HITL).

## CXXVII.4 Measure trade-offs with CBC — do not ask parents to invent a price for “thinking minutes”

**FACT (stated choice):** Louviere, Hensher, & Swait (2000, *Stated Choice Methods*, Cambridge) — multi-attribute choice experiments recover relative utilities under random-utility assumptions better than unstructured preference talk.

**FACT (hypothetical bias):** Murphy, Allen, Stevens, & Weatherhead (2005, *Environmental and Resource Economics, 30*(3), 313–325, doi:10.1007/s10640-004-3332-x) — hypothetical WTP often exceeds actual; median hypothetical/actual ratio ~1.35 with heavy skew; treat absolute dollars as biased.

**FACT (tutoring-adjacent DCE):** Gambi & De Witte (2024, *Higher Education*, doi:10.1007/s10734-024-01358-z) — DCE recovers WTP for tutoring attributes; high stated WTP can coexist with low private uptake; information and SES frictions matter. **Wound:** HE students ≠ K-12 parents — method transfers, coefficients do not.

**FACT (market skew):** Kim, Goodman, & West (2024, CESifo WP 11251 / IZA DP 17178) — U.S. private tutoring centers concentrate in high-income, high-parent-education areas; one national “parent wants 24/7” story will misfire.

**Applied (FOUNDER BELIEF → testable):** Run crunch CBC (LOADSHED-5 / CRUNCHMSG-1):

| Attribute | Levels (draft) |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Access policy** | Unlimited same-day / acuity queue (48h) / AI-first then HITL |
| **Session promise** | More minutes / protected FEI + micro-debrief / score-drill pack |
| **Transparency** | Silent / plain pause list / pause list + FEI weekly proof |
| **Price** | Calibrated span + **neither** (Khan/GPT/status quo) |

**HYPOTHESIS:** Anxious-middle + HMA segments prefer **protected FEI + transparency** over unlimited access at equal/slightly higher price when comprehension is high. Score-chasers may overweight unlimited + drill — latent-class, don’t average.

**Kill:** Survey “how valuable is thinking minutes (1–7)?” as packaging gospel.  
**Survive:** Part-worths → crunch SKU copy; cash/waitlist validation before national ads.

## CXXVII.5 Fear-appeal crunch copy is a threat to motivation — and to parent control climate

**FACT:** Putwain & Remedios (2014, *School Psychology Quarterly, 29*(4), 360–373, doi:10.1037/spq0000048) — teacher fear appeals (failure-consequence messages) before high-stakes exams: higher frequency and threat appraisal predicted **lower self-determined motivation** and **lower exam performance**, partly mediated by motivation; extrinsic focus on avoiding failure is not a free motivational lever.

**FACT (appraisal contingency):** Putwain, Remedios, & Symes (2016, *British Journal of Educational Psychology, 86*(1), 16–31, doi:10.1111/bjep.12132) — challenge vs threat appraisals of fear appeals diverge; threat path links to worse engagement/performance patterns; challenge path can look different — **do not treat scare tactics as uniformly “motivating.”**

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Parent emails that say “ACT is in 10 days — if we don’t book every night they will freeze” import classroom fear appeals into the home, raising **controlling parental pressure** (SAFE-SOFTMSG / SAFE-REWARD) and homework conflict. SAFE-CRUNCHMSG defaults to **challenge-framed structure**: named plan, protected attempts, one CTA — not failure theater.

**Kill:** Scare-parent default; “grind or fail” landing heroes.  
**Survive:** “Here’s the plan for the next 14 days: fewer dump sessions, more equipped attempts + closes.”

## CXXVII.8 Doctrine — SAFE-CRUNCHMSG (provisional)

1. **Sell reliability of method, not infinity of access** — crunch brand = protected FEI + micro-debrief, not 24/7 (Oliver / Parasuraman gap logic).  
2. **CBC over chatter** — force trade-offs with price + neither; ban standalone Likert WTP for “thinking minutes” (SAFE-WTP).  
3. **Name the shed** — pause list in parent copy or honesty reads as ghosting (SAFE-LOADSHED).  
4. **No fear-appeal default** — challenge-framed plans beat failure scare to parents (Putwain line).  
5. **Autonomy-supportive CTAs** — one next action; no controlling “sit until booked every night” (SAFE-SOFTMSG / SAFE-REWARD).  
6. **Segment** — score-chaser vs HMA-anxious vs homework-peace; do not average part-worths into one parent.  
7. **Ads must match ops** — if playbook sheds HW dumps, creative cannot promise human Chegg.  
8. **Copy:** “Exam week we protect thinking minutes — fewer dump sessions, clearer waits, proof of real attempts.” Never Availability Score™ or ACT guarantees from “we stayed open.”

**Confidence:** High on method sources (gaps/SERVQUAL/Oliver; stated choice; Putwain). Medium on MindCraft win of shed-honesty vs 24/7 (needs CRUNCHMSG-*). High that infinite-availability / hours-hero / fear-crunch / Likert-WTP / Availability Score™ are commercially toxic under FEI + LOADSHED.
