## CIV.1 Why this chapter exists

MindCraft already has two surfaces that collide in parental speech: “Help my kid finish tonight’s homework” and “Make my kid a mathematical thinker.” Chegg / Course Hero / ChatGPT optimized the first. Identity products that refuse the first lose GTM. Identity products that *become* the first lose the thesis.

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** Homework help is a legitimate acquisition and retention job — but it is not the North Star. If Solver completion % rises while `solo_transfer_pass` falls, MindCraft has become a nicer Chegg.

**Claims we refuse as doctrine:**
1. Homework Completion Score™ / tonight-% submitted as learning NS.  
2. “We finish your worksheet” / unlimited answer dump as brand.  
3. Solver-open accuracy ≡ exam readiness / identity.  
4. Ban all homework help (purity theater that loses parents).  
5. Shame parents who need deadline relief.  
6. Practice and Solver as one undifferentiated “AI tutor” SKU.  
7. Bastani GPT Tutor cosplay as MindCraft Solver license (already XXXIII RT).  
8. Guaranteed grades / ACT points from Solver sessions.

**Operational definition (HYPOTHESIS):** A Solver path is *SAFE-HWHELP compliant* when (a) UI/copy labels help vs practice rails, (b) default help is instrumental (SAFE-HELP / SAFE-FADE), (c) full reveal is costly and logged, (d) every help episode offers / requires a follow-on Practice or solo grain when feasible, (e) parent messaging separates “unstuck tonight” from “proved alone,” and (f) product never markets assisted completion as identity transformation.

## CIV.3 Homework can help achievement — when students do the work

**FACT:** Cooper, Robinson, & Patall (2006, *Review of Educational Research, 76*(1), 1–62, doi:10.3102/00346543076001001) — synthesis of U.S. homework research 1987–2003; despite design flaws across study types, evidence generally supports a positive homework→achievement influence, stronger in grades 7–12 than K–6, and stronger when students (not parents) report homework time.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** The commercial lesson is *not* “more homework widgets.” It is that the achievement link assumes **student cognitive work**. Answer marketplaces sever that assumption while preserving the grade signal.

**Kill:** Tonight-submitted-% as MindCraft learning NS.  
**Survive:** Homework as a *context* parents care about — routed into instrumental help + Practice proof.

---

## CIV.4 When solutions are outsourced, HW grades decouple from exams

**FACT (course evidence):** DeGoede (2020, ASEE Annual Conference, doi:10.18260/1-2--33979) — engineering dynamics sections: pre–web-solutions era showed strong homework–exam correlations (reported R² ≈ 0.74–0.98); after extensive self-reported Chegg-style solution use, homework–exam correlation collapsed (R² ≈ 0.08) while homework scores stayed high / rose and exam performance weakened on average — correctness-graded HW without process control stopped indexing learning.

**FACT (integrity audit):** Pickering & Schuller (2024, *Journal of Academic Ethics*, doi:10.1007/s10805-024-09551-6) — audit in an Australian engineering school: Chegg content implicated across a majority of audited units; ~50% of questions answered within ~1.5 hours — fast enough for assignment and online-exam misuse; authors distinguish “homework-helper” branding from cheat-vulnerable business models.

**FACT (quasi-experimental conference evidence — label carefully):** Roshak (AEA 2023 poster / working paper, “Technology Undermining Education: The Case of Chegg”) — finance course; ~25% of students (incl. ~15% of high scorers) copied obviously wrong Chegg answers; DMCA takedown variation reducing solution availability associated with better subsequent exam performance especially among high-scoring students. **Not** a peer-reviewed journal RCT — treat magnitude as provisional; mechanism (temptation to shirk process work) aligns with DeGoede / Pickering.

**Applied (FOUNDER BELIEF → testable):** Parent delight at “homework done” is orthogonal to durable identity. If MindCraft Solver raises assisted completion without solo proof, it recreates the Chegg grade–learning split inside our product.

**Kill:** Solver completion NPS / homework-finished toast as FEI.  
**Survive:** Instrumental unstuck + required prove-alone follow-through.

## CIV.5 Generative help recreates the crutch at AI speed

**FACT:** Bastani, Bastani, Sungu, Ge, Kabakcı, & Mariman (2025, *PNAS*, doi:10.1073/pnas.2422633122) — ~1,000 high-school math students; GPT Base raised practice grades (~+48%) but harmed later unassisted exams (~−17% vs never-AI); GPT Tutor (hint safeguards + teacher keys in prompt) largely neutralized harm but did **not** beat no-AI on learning; students used unfettered GPT as a crutch; perception ≠ exam learning.

**Already company law (XXXIII RT):** Bastani Tutor ≢ MindCraft PWC; guards ≠ gain. This chapter densifies the *product-surface* implication: **Solver that behaves like GPT Base is commercially toxic to the identity claim**, even if parents love tonight’s answers.

**HYPOTHESIS:** Deadline-mode Solver without fade/solo gates will show Bastani Base signature: high assisted score, low `solo_transfer_pass`.

**Kill:** Unlimited ChatGPT-style homework hero.  
**Survive:** Guarded instrumental Solver + Practice gym (SAFE-FADE / SAFE-HELP / PWC).

## CIV.6 Instrumental vs executive help is the identity fork inside Solver

**FACT:** Nelson-Le Gall (1981, *Developmental Review, 1*(3), 224–246, doi:10.1016/0273-2297(81)90019-8) — distinguishes **executive** help-seeking (get the goal done for me) from **instrumental** help-seeking (get the minimal process help so I can finish myself); continued executive reliance risks dependency; instrumental help is a path from other-regulation to self-regulation.

**FACT (ITS line, already SAFE-HELP):** Aleven / Roll / Baker — on-demand help abuse (hint racing to bottom-out) often correlates with worse learning; help avoidance is the twin failure (summarized in Roll, Aleven, McLaren & Koedinger, 2011, *Learning and Instruction, 21*(2), 267–280, doi:10.1016/j.learninstruc.2010.07.004; Aleven et al., 2016 IJAIED help-seeking review).

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Homework-help mode is where executive pressure peaks (due tonight). Product must make instrumental the default path and executive the expensive exception — not ban help, not shame need, not celebrate dump.

**Kill:** “Never stuck” answer-complete Solver ads.  
**Survive:** Principle → completion → (costly) reveal; then Practice grain.

## CIV.7 Parent messaging: two jobs, two sentences

**FOUNDER BELIEF:** Parents buy relief *and* identity. Conflating them produces either Chegg competitors (relief-only) or purity products parents bounce from (identity-only).

**HYPOTHESIS (copy):** Winning CBC pair (test in HWHELP-3 / densifies SAFE-WTP):
1. Help rail: “Get unstuck without pasting the answer.”  
2. Practice rail: “Then prove the same join alone.”  

**Kill:** Single splash “AI homework tutor that does it for them.”  
**Survive:** Dual-rail honesty (mirrors SAFE-DURABLE exam/durable dual-rail).

**Contradiction to watch:** If instrumental Solver is too slow under deadline, students flee to ChatGPT. Red Team: measure time-to-unstuck *and* solo transfer — not purity of friction.

## CIV.11 So what for MindCraft commercially

- **Copy:** “Unstuck without the paste — then prove it alone.” Never Homework Completion Score™ or “we do the worksheet.”  
- **Product:** Labeled Help vs Practice; Solver defaults to instrumental fade; costly full reveal; optional/forced Practice follow-on grain; instrument assisted vs solo separately.  
- **Positioning:** Against Chegg dumps and against purity products that abandon Sunday-night parents — MindCraft is the dual-rail FEI gym.  
- **Metric:** `solo_transfer_pass`, `help_recruit_then_solo`, time-to-unstuck, ChatGPT-escape proxy — demote tonight-% / Solver NPS as learning NS.  
- **Kill list:** Completion Score™; dump hero; assisted≡ready; purity ban on help; parent homework stalk; Bastani-cosplay Solver ads; grade/ACT guarantees from Solver.  
- **Growth / vision:** Acquisition may start on Help; retention and brand proof live on Practice identity evidence.

## CIV.12 Doctrine — SAFE-HWHELP (provisional)

1. **Two rails, two jobs** — Help = instrumental unstuck; Practice = FEI identity gym.  
2. **Executive completion is not learning** — densifies SAFE-HELP / Nelson-Le Gall.  
3. **Assisted ≠ unassisted** — always separate metrics (Bastani signature watch).  
4. **Default instrumental; costly dump** — densifies SAFE-FADE / SAFE-HINT.  
5. **Help episodes should recruit solo proof** — densifies SAFE-PROOF / SAFE-ROI.  
6. **Parent honesty without shame** — dual-rail copy; no live stalk (SAFE-PDASH).  
7. **No Chegg-clone brand / Bastani Base hero / Completion Score™**.  
8. **No purity theater that bans all deadline help**.

**Confidence:** High that outsourcing solutions and unguarded generative help can inflate assisted scores while harming or failing to improve solo learning. Medium that MindCraft’s dual-rail UX will beat dump competitors on parent WTP *and* solo transfer simultaneously — instrument HWHELP-* before homework-helper splash campaigns.
