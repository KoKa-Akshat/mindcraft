Can a chapter animate itself?

Real concepts from this chapter

What's real vs. illustrative: the CIS values, E(c) formula, CISmax=661, and tier thresholds are the chapter's actual numbers. The four unlabeled leaf sub-scores in the video are illustrative — the chapter doesn't enumerate that subtree. The spec extraction here was done by hand once; an LLM doing that extraction automatically is the unproven step for a real pipeline.

The GeoGebra panel keeps a fixed light "instrument" background in both themes — it's a third-party embedded canvas with its own hardcoded plot colors, styled once rather than re-issued per theme.
