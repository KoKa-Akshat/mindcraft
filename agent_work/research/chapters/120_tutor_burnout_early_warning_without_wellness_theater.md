## CXX.1 Why this chapter exists

SAFE-WORKFORCE already kills headcount NS, tenure≡quality, and wellness theater as pedagogy — and requires burnout→load redesign (WORK-3). What remains commercially underspecified is the **early-warning instrumentation**: what to watch *before* exit interviews, how to act without turning tutors into an Anxiety Score™ cohort, and how to sell “we protect tutor quality” without mindfulness-app cosplay.

Competitors resolve this wrongly: marketplace volume that ignores fidelity drift; “tutor wellness Fridays” that leave caseloads intact; emotion-AI dashboards on tutor cameras; retention-% hero metrics that hide pour-relapse; grit posters for exhausted near-peers.

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** The cheapest honest early warning is **behavioral fidelity under load** (brief skip, pour relapse, soft-wrong avoid, QA starve) — not a proprietary Burnout Score™ — and the honest fix is **demand/resource redesign**, not a meditation SKU.

**Claims we refuse as doctrine:**
1. Burnout Score™ / Wellness Score™ as tutor or parent North Star.  
2. Face/voice emotion AI on tutors as “care.”  
3. Mandatory mood gates before every session.  
4. Wellness module / mindfulness add-on as primary burnout strategy when caseload is the demand.  
5. Retention-% without fidelity@30/90d as quality proof.  
6. Trait emotion labels in tutor cards (SAFE-EXPECTANCY collision).  
7. Sell tutor affect data / “burnout analytics” as a district SKU.  
8. ACT guarantees from “wellness-supported tutors” packaging.

## CXX.3 JD-R — demands predict exhaustion; resources predict engagement

**FACT (JD-R founding):** Demerouti, Bakker, Nachreiner, & Schaufeli (2001, *Journal of Applied Psychology, 86*(3), 499–512, doi:10.1037/0021-9010.86.3.499) — job demands primarily predict **exhaustion**; (lack of) job resources primarily predict **disengagement**; pattern held across occupational groups with self-report and observer ratings (N=374).

**FACT (JD-R theory stock-take):** Bakker & Demerouti (2017, *Journal of Occupational Health Psychology, 22*(3), 273–285, doi:10.1037/ocp0000056) — dual pathways: health-impairment (demands→burnout) and motivational (resources→engagement); later evidence links demands→absence duration via burnout and resources→commitment/engagement; interventions must address both sides of the ledger.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** MindCraft’s early-warning board should surface **demand spikes** (FEI-heavy concurrent students, repair escalations, exam-week density, unpaid emotional labor after hallucinations) and **resource deficits** (coach sample lag, missing Map tools, schedule chaos) — not a single composite Burnout Score™. Redesign = cut demands and/or add resources.

**Kill:** Person-only coping apps as the default response to demand spikes.  
**Survive:** Demand/resource dashboards that ops can act on this week.

**Wound:** JD-R is general occupational theory — not a MindCraft RCT. BURNWARN-* must test which console signals lead quit and drift in *this* pipeline.

## CXX.4 Burnout predicts quit and harms the interaction students feel

**FACT (attrition meta, reuse):** Madigan & Kim (2021, *Teaching and Teacher Education, 105*, 103425, doi:10.1016/j.tate.2021.103425) — burnout dimensions relate positively to quit intent (exhaustion r+ ≈ .41, depersonalization ≈ .32, reduced accomplishment ≈ .21); satisfaction protects less than burnout risks (sat r+ ≈ −.40); together ≈ 27% of quit-intent variance.

**FACT (exhaustion → instructional quality → students, reuse):** Klusmann, Aldrup, Roloff, Lüdtke, & Hamre (2022, *Journal of Educational Psychology, 114*(6), 1442–1460, doi:10.1037/edu0000703) — large multilevel sample; teacher emotional exhaustion associated with worse student self-concept, interest, and (German) achievement; paths via lower emotional support and classroom organization.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Early warning that only tracks quit intent after the tutor has already poured for six weeks is too late for Maya. Prefer leading indicators on SAFE-WORKFORCE drift channels: brief skip, pour relapse, “got it?” closers, soft-wrong avoidance, QA starvation — each is a **fidelity** early warning that also predicts exhaustion→interaction harm.

**Kill:** Retention-% hero without fidelity trend.  
**Survive:** Fidelity@30/90d + drift-channel alerts as the early board.

## CXX.5 Org-directed interventions beat person-directed coping theater

**FACT (physician meta):** Panagioti et al. (2017, *JAMA Internal Medicine, 177*(2), 195–205, doi:10.1001/jamainternmed.2016.7674) — controlled interventions reduce burnout overall (SMD ≈ −0.29); **organization-directed** interventions (SMD ≈ −0.45) outperform **physician-directed** ones (SMD ≈ −0.18); supports framing burnout as an organizational problem, not only an individual deficit.

**FACT (org interventions meta):** Bes, Shoman, Al-Gobari, Rousson, & Guseva Canu (2023, *International Archives of Occupational and Environmental Health*, doi:10.1007/s00420-023-02009-z) — organizational interventions targeting workload/resources and participatory redesign are central levers for exhaustion; JD-R imbalance (demands without recovery/resources) is the theoretical spine; person-only approaches are incomplete when workload is the driver.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** When MindCraft detects a BURNWARN pattern, the default playbook is WORK-3-style: **caseload cap, schedule redesign, hire pause until coach bandwidth recovers** — optionally offer coping resources, never as the primary fix. Wellness modules that leave caseload unchanged fail Panagioti’s org>person lesson.

**Kill:** Mindfulness add-on as primary burnout strategy; wellness theater in sales decks.  
**Survive:** Documented redesign SLA after fidelity/load alerts.

## CXX.8 Doctrine — SAFE-BURNWARN (provisional)

1. **Fidelity dips before exit** — instrument drift channels as leading indicators (SAFE-WORKFORCE).  
2. **JD-R ledger** — alert on demand spikes and resource deficits, not a mystical composite score (Demerouti; Bakker & Demerouti).  
3. **Org redesign first** — caseload/schedule/coach bandwidth beat person-directed coping as primary response (Panagioti; Bes et al.).  
4. **No Burnout Score™ / Wellness Minutes NS** — already company-adjacent via SAFE-WORKFORCE; this chapter densifies the ban for early-warn UX.  
5. **Privacy parity for tutors** — no ERT; no mandatory mood gate; no parent/district tutor-affect dashboards (SAFE-PRIVACY).  
6. **Voluntary check-in ≠ surveillance SKU** — optional, coach-private, short TTL.  
7. **Hire gated by coach capacity** — surge hire without QA is a demand bomb, not a fix.  
8. **Copy:** “We watch whether tutors still make students think — and we cut load when fidelity slips.” Never “our AI wellness coach keeps tutors happy.”

**Confidence:** High — JD-R demand/resource pathways; Madigan quit links; Klusmann exhaustion→instruction path; Panagioti org>person. Medium — exact console thresholds and SLA days for MindCraft near-peer tutors (needs BURNWARN-*). High — Burnout Score™ / wellness-theater-as-primary / emotion-AI as commercially toxic under this stack.
