## CLI.3 Switching rules exist so intensity tracks quality history

**FACT:** ISO 2859-1:1999 defines **switching rules** among normal, tightened, and reduced inspection for continuing lots: severity tracks accept/reject history; reduced inspection is an *incentive* after consistently good quality (authority discretion); tightened / discontinuation protect consumers when quality deteriorates (§1.2, §9.3).

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Treat inherit admits as a continuing series. Default after TMPTRUST G1–G6: reduced-like sample %. Two critical inherit failures in a short window → tighten (raise %; optionally pause inherits). Calm under tightened → normal. Reduce only after pre-registered calm streak + authority — never during surge theater. Exact ISO “2-of-5 / 10 consecutive” numbers are analogy — set bands in ops policy; pre-register REVOKE-*.

**Kill:** Frozen 5% forever; forever-trust latch. **Survive:** History-triggered tighten/revoke. **Wound:** Borrow switching logic, not Ac/Re tables as ACT law.

## CLI.4 Detect shifts early — do not wait for a quarterly IRR ceremony

**FACT:** Shewhart (1931, *Economic Control of Quality of Manufactured Product*, D. Van Nostrand) — control charts distinguish **chance-cause** fluctuation from **assignable-cause** shifts; points outside limits demand investigation and corrective action, not averaging into a seasonal “quality score.”

**FACT:** Page (1954, *Biometrika, 41*(1–2), 100–115, doi:10.1093/biomet/41.1-2.100) — **cumulative sum (CUSUM)** continuous inspection schemes accumulate evidence across the process history to detect persistent mean shifts sooner than single-point Shewhart rules alone.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Windowed inherit_solo repair rate and sample-dual discord are the process characteristics. SAFE-REVOKE uses **simple Shewhart-style thresholds** (and optional CUSUM on weekly inherit repair) to fire tighten/revoke — not a launch-week κ plaque (REPAIRSTRAT) and not a Template Trust Score™ that hides assignable causes. When CUSUM/window trips, clear `template_trust` or move to `tightened` with 100% sample until re-qualify.

**Kill:** One-shot IRR certificate as trust health. **Survive:** Ongoing shift detection → revoke/tighten. **Wound:** Charts stay ops-behind-login — no CUSUM-hero ads.

## CLI.5 Sample size for agreement is about precision — not a magic 5% brand

**FACT:** Flack, Afifi, Lachenbruch, & Schouten (1988, *Psychometrika, 53*(3), 321–325, doi:10.1007/BF02294215) — give methods to choose sample size so that a confidence interval for two-rater κ meets a prespecified width (or so that a hypothesis test about κ has planned power); agreement monitoring is **precision-planned**, not vibes-planned.

**Reuse (REPAIRSTRAT):** Myford & Wolfe (2009, *Journal of Educational Measurement, 46*(4), 371–389, doi:10.1111/j.1745-3984.2009.00088.x) — rater performance drifts over a campaign; monitoring must be longitudinal.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Sample-dual % is a **capacity × detection** tradeoff. Too thin → discord never fires (false safety). Too thick → CAPPLAN burns dual minutes and inheritance’s point disappears. Plan sample size from expected discord prevalence and desired CI width on path agreement — then map to a % of inherit volume. Under repair spikes, prefer **temporary 100% sample or revoke** over tiny % “theater audits.” Ban Sample Dual % as marketing hero.

**Kill:** “Industry-standard 5% audit” as brand. **Survive:** Precision-aware bands + spike override.

## CLI.6 Clone variability makes revoke a feature, not an apology

**Reuse (TMPTRUST):** Glas & van der Linden (2003, *Applied Psychological Measurement, 27*(4), 247–261, doi:10.1177/0146621603027004001) — item cloning induces **parameter variability** across clones; multilevel models treat clones as draws from a family distribution.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Inheritance assumed family stability after blueprint. A repair spike on inherit_solo is evidence the family draw process drifted (generator bug, rater soften, seal leak). Revoke is the honest IUA move: stop claiming reduced inspection until a new dual blueprint re-qualifies the model. Selling “template trusted” while repairs accumulate is Kane-incoherent.

**Kill:** Soft-ignore inherit repairs to protect FILLETA dates. **Survive:** Revoke → re-arm dual → honest fill-band slip.
