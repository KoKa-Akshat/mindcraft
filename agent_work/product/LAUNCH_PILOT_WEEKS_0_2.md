## 4-week protocol (minimum viable)

| Week | Student | Tutor | Parent | Capture |
|------|---------|-------|--------|---------|
| **0** | Gap scan (low shame) | Briefed on map | Role: witness/scheduler, not teacher | Baseline: “I’m a math person?” 1–7; anxiety note; Joe’s goal on record |
| **1** | Practice on gap + 1 live session | Start on mapped gap only | 1 signal: what clicked / stuck | Clip or quote after session 1 |
| **2** | Same | Same | Mid check-in | Mid video/note from parent or Joe |
| **3** | Raise challenge slightly if retry healthy | Name strategy wins out loud | Keep out of homework teaching | Tutor written note (2–3 sentences) |
| **4** | Transfer check + celebrate real progress | Recap growth witnessed | Final gratitude capture | Demo-ready video + map before/after |

**Instrumentation (even if manual):**
- Retried after a miss? (Y/N)
- Chose harder work when offered? (Y/N)
- Parent: Sunday-night fight better/worse/same
- One sentence from Joe about politics/math fear
