Questions, answered

The stuff everyone asks first.

Why is The Desk free right now?

We're inviting a small group in first, so pricing and the mentor marketplace get built around real use, not guesses. A few seats remain each round — apply and we'll tell you where you land. It's early access, not a permanent freebie.

What happens to my account when it's not free anymore?

Early members hear first, and get first pick of what a paid plan looks like before it's public. Your notes, your map, your record — those stay yours regardless of what tier you're on.

Is this just another planner or AI homework bot?

No. Planners hold what you type. Chat bots answer tonight and forget tomorrow. The Desk is an operating system for your work: file it, map it, practice it, prove it transferred, then brief a human mentor.

What can I drop on the desk?

Photos, PDFs, docs, screenshots, scans of handwritten work. If it's school, it has a home here.

Where do my files go?

Into your private binder. Your files stay yours, we never sell your data, and you can delete anything anytime.

Does it do my homework for me?

No. Solver walks you through steps and asks you questions back. The Desk makes you faster, not fake.

Who are the mentors?

Real college students who remember exactly what junior year felt like. They open your map before the session, so the hour goes to teaching instead of detective work. Your first session is free.

Is it only for the ACT?

It's built for the road to the ACT, but the binder, the planner, Resume Help, and story practice work all year for regular classwork too.
