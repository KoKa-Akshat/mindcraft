Questions, answered

The stuff everyone asks first.

Why is The Desk free right now?

A small group first, so we build around real use. A few seats each round. Early access, not a forever freebie.

What happens to my account when it's not free anymore?

Plus is $12 a month. Family is $10 more. Early members hear first. Notes, map, record stay yours on every tier.

Is this just another planner or AI homework bot?

Planners hold what you type. Chat forgets tomorrow. The Desk files, maps, practices, then briefs a person.

What can I drop on the desk?

Photos, PDFs, docs, screenshots, scans of handwritten work. If it's school, it has a home here.

Where do my files go?

Into your private binder. Your files stay yours, we never sell your data, and you can delete anything anytime.

Does it do my homework for me?

No. Solver walks you through steps and asks you questions back. The Desk makes you faster, not fake.

Who are the mentors?

Real college students who remember exactly what junior year felt like. They open your map before the session, so the hour goes to teaching instead of detective work. Your first session is free.

Is it only for the ACT?

Built for the ACT road. The desk works all year for class too.

What do parents and teachers get?

Family is one parent view of one student: the record, what is hard this week, what to do next. Institutional is one dashboard for the class. Custom homework for each student. AI drafts it. The teacher decides. A person is still close when someone needs one.
