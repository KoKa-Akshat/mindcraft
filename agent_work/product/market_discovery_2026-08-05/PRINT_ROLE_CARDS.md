# Pocket role cards (print 4-up, laminate)

**Opener:** “Math gap map research — 90s raffle card, no pitch. Or 8 minutes if you’ve got a story.”

---

## PARENT (8 min)
1. Last house-stress math night — what happened?  
2. Tried already — what failed, specifically?  
3. Two weeks worked — what would you *see*?  
4. What makes you cancel after a month?  
5. Pick one or none: (A) gap map + honest weekly note (B) unlimited AI homework chat (C) more tutor hours, no map — why?  
6. Book 20-min parent interview?

**Ban:** how much would you pay / ACT points / live stalk.

---

## STUDENT / MAYA (8 min)
1. Time math felt dangerous or humiliating.  
2. Body? Next move?  
3. Time you surprised yourself — did anyone see?  
4. Stuck: comfort → hint → answer order?  
5. App celebration: real or fake?  
6. What would make you take harder math next year?  
7. Book full 20-min chat?

---

## COLLEGE TUTOR / ED MAJOR (8 min)
1. Who you tutor — concept, format, or panic?  
2. “I get it” — real or polite?  
3. Ideal one-page pre-session brief?  
4. Accountable to parent or student — what they ask?  
5. Rank: credentials / diagnosis map / warm vibe.  
6. Paid pilot later this term?

---

## TEACHER / ADVISOR (8 min)
1. Where kids lose the thread?  
2. What parents ask that you can’t scale?  
3. One-screen useful outside tool (not a gradebook)?  
4. Hard no — what you refuse to recommend?

---
