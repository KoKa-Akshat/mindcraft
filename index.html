Appendix · Questions, answered

The stuff everyone asks first.

Is this just another planner or AI homework bot?

No. Planners hold what you type. Chat bots answer tonight and forget tomorrow. MindCraft is an operating system for knowledge: file, map, train, prove transfer, brief a human tutor.

What does “gym membership for math” mean?

You still do the reps. The desk is the floor and the trainer: it points at the exact weak lift, tracks what transferred, and keeps the next session honest.

What can I drop on the desk?

Photos, PDFs, docs, screenshots, scans of handwritten work. If it is school, it has a home here.

Where do my files go?

Into your private binder. Your files stay yours, we never sell your data, and you can delete anything anytime.

Does it do my homework for me?

No. Solver walks you through steps and asks you questions back. The desk makes you faster, not fake.

Who are the tutors?

Real college students who remember exactly what junior year felt like. They open your map before the session, so the hour goes to teaching instead of detective work. Your first session is free.

Is it only for the ACT?

It is built for the road to the ACT, but the binder, the planner, and story practice work all year for regular math class.

What does it cost?

The desk is free while we are in beta. Tutoring and Summer ACT prep are paid, and your first tutor session is free so you can decide after you meet.
