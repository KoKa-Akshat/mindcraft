## CXXXVIII.1 Why this chapter exists

SAFE-HCELIG decided *admission*. SAFE-SPLITPOOL decided *which pool*. Scarcity still breaks product judgment:

1. **Symbolic pad** — fill a “diagram” slot with a text-only stem so the scan looks complete.  
2. **Quota theater** — force one item per FormatId even when only a single sealed graph exists (over-expose; under-generalize).  
3. **Breadth ads** — “we diagnose every format” while C4 stock covers 1–2 FormatIds.  
4. **Silent underrepresentation** — skip FormatId entirely, then sell concept-only Map as format-flexible (Messick underrepresentation).  
5. **Hole denial** — omit `not_probed` flags so parents/tutors read absence as mastery.

Composition is the commercial knife-edge: honesty without paralysis. The company must ship gap-scans that are **defensible under review** when sealed FormatId stock cannot yet fill a pretty matrix.

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** A short, sealed, hole-labeled FormatId slice beats a long, padded, “balanced” scan that teaches the Map lies.

**Claims we refuse:**
1. Symbolic (or Practice-only) items may fill C4 FormatId blueprint cells to avoid holes.  
2. Equal FormatId quotas regardless of sealed-stock depth.  
3. Over-reuse of a single sealed stem as “format coverage.”  
4. Composition Score™ / Format Balance % / Scan Completeness Minutes as North Star.  
5. “We diagnose all formats” marketing under thin C4 stock.  
6. Absence of FormatId probe ≡ format flexibility / mastery.  
7. ACT / identity guarantees from balanced-composition packaging.

## CXXXVIII.3 Uses scale with composition ambition

**FACT:** Kane (2013, *Journal of Educational Measurement, 50*(1), 1–73, doi:10.1111/jedm.12000) — validate interpretations and **uses**; more ambitious claims need more support; rejecting a use need not kill a weaker interpretation.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):**

| Composition | Ambition of use | Allowed when stock is scarce? |
|-------------|-----------------|-------------------------------|
| Concept-only C4 (no FormatId cells claimed) | Medium | Yes — if copy does not claim format diagnosis |
| Partial FormatId slice + labeled holes | Medium–high | Yes — preferred under scarcity |
| “Full FormatId diagnosis” / every FormatId green | High | **No** until sealed depth meets blueprint |
| Parent “we mapped every visual gap” | High | Same bar as full diagnosis |

**Kill:** Thin stock + high-ambition FormatId claims.  
**Survive:** Lower ambition + honest holes, or delay FormatId C4 cells until promote-to-C4 grows stock (SAFE-SPLITPOOL / id 140).

## CXXXVIII.4 Underrepresentation vs irrelevant pad — both kill

**FACT:** Messick (1995, *American Psychologist, 50*(9), 741–749, doi:10.1037/0003-066X.50.9.741) — two primary threats: **construct underrepresentation** (too narrow; missing facets) and **construct-irrelevant variance** (contamination by extraneous difficulty). Both can operate at once.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Scarcity creates a fork:

- **Underrepresentation** — never probe sealed diagrams → Map pretends format flexibility from symbolic-only evidence.  
- **CIV pad** — force a decorative/text-as-diagram or unsealed visual into the cell → miss reflects the wrong construct, then hide-correctness locks the lie in.

SAFE-SCANCOMP refuses both: **omit + label** beats **pad**; **probe sealed facets you have** beats **silence that markets as complete**.

**Kill:** “Better a weak diagram than a hole.”  
**Survive:** Prefer holes; invest GENQ/FIGKEY/promote pipeline to grow real cells.

## CXXXVIII.5 Domain representation is not equal quotas

**FACT:** Sireci and Faulkner-Bond (2014, *Psicothema, 26*(1), 100–106, doi:10.7334/psicothema2013.256) — content-related validity evidence includes **domain definition**, **domain representation**, **domain relevance**, and appropriateness of construction procedures; representation asks whether items adequately reflect the defined domain — not whether every marketing checkbox got one token item.

**FACT:** Raymond and Grande (2019, *Medical Teacher, 41*(8), 854–861, doi:10.1080/0142159X.2019.1595556) — a test blueprint allocates emphasis across knowledge/skill domains; blueprinting supports validation by making intended coverage and weights explicit before assembly.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** MindCraft’s FormatId gap-scan needs a **blueprint** (which FormatIds matter for this exam track / concept set; target weights) *and* a **scarcity rule**: when sealed stock cannot meet a weight, **shrink the claim**, do not manufacture items. Equal 1-of-each quotas under N=1 sealed graphs are **representation theater**, not Sireci representation.

**Kill:** Forced equal FormatId counts as “science-backed balance.”  
**Survive:** Weighted blueprint + stock-gated fulfillment + hole labels for unmet weights.

## CXXXVIII.6 Thin pools need assembly constraints — not vibes

**FACT:** van der Linden, Ariel, and Veldkamp (2006, *Journal of Educational and Behavioral Statistics, 31*(1), 81–99, doi:10.3102/10769986031001081) — CAT pools assembled without content/psychometric constraints can force algorithms into suboptimal, constraint-violating, or poorly exposed selections; principled assembly (here, as sets of linear forms meeting specs) improves control of content and exposure properties.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS, careful transfer):** MindCraft is not shipping high-stakes CAT. Transferable: **composition is constrained assembly**. Under scarcity, the selector must encode hard constraints (`hcelig_pass` only; max exposure; cool-down; no cross-pool pad) and soft targets (blueprint weights). When constraints cannot be met, **fail the cell openly** — do not relax into Practice-only or symbolic fillers (SAFE-SPLITPOOL).

**Kill:** “Just pick whatever tagged FormatId is in the bank.”  
**Survive:** Constraint-first selector; telemetry on constraint violations and hole rates.

## CXXXVIII.8 Product surface — SAFE-SCANCOMP claim contract

1. Maintain a **gap-scan FormatId blueprint** (exam-track × FormatId weights) separate from raw bank counts.  
2. Fill cells **only** from `hcelig_pass` / `pool=c4_hcelig`.  
3. If stock = 0 for a cell → emit **`format_not_probed`**; do not pad; do not write format-gap mastery for that FormatId.  
4. If stock = 1–2 → allow at most **one** exposure per student per cool-down; do not pretend breadth.  
5. Prefer probing **sealed FormatIds with depth** over sprinkling token singles across five FormatIds.  
6. UI: scan summary shows **probed vs not_probed** FormatIds (student + tutor + parent surfaces — densifies COVER; parent copy → id 139).  
7. Admin/coverage matrix: C4 FormatId depth vs Practice FormatId depth (SPLITPOOL dual tables).  
8. Telemetry: `blueprint_cell`, `fulfilled|hole`, `item_id`, `exposure_count` — demote Composition Score™ / Format Balance %.  
9. Copy: “We only hide the key on sealed visuals — empty slots mean we have not diagnosed that format yet.”  
10. Ban marketing that converts hole rates into “complete visual diagnostic” claims.
