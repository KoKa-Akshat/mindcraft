## CXLII.1 Why this chapter exists

SAFE-HOLETRUST locked **parent** hole honesty. Students still see a different risk surface: hide holes so teens “feel complete”; paint unprobed cells green (absence≡mastery — SAFE-COLD kill); frame holes as visual talent deficits; dump jargon matrices; or plaster empty “mistakes make you stronger!” without a next attempt. Parent-only honesty fails commercially when Maya opens the same scan and sees fake greens while a tutor later says “we never probed diagram.”

**FOUNDER BELIEF under audit:** Teens trust a shorter honest map (“diagram not checked yet”) plus one Practice mission more than a complete-looking rainbow that collapses the first time a tutor opens Map.

**Claims we refuse:**
1. Parent-only hole labels; student summary hides `format_not_probed`.  
2. Fake greens / “you’re flexible across formats” for unprobed cells.  
3. Ability / visual-talent shame (“you’re weak at graphs”).  
4. Empty growth-mindset poster without grain + CTA.  
5. Student Hole Score™ / Completeness % / Honesty Minutes as North Star.  
6. Multi-link maze or forced marathon before leaving summary.  
7. ACT / identity guarantees from student hole packaging.

## CXLII.3 Transparency still = disclosure + clarity + accuracy — for the learner as stakeholder

**FACT:** Schnackenberg and Tomlinson (2016, *Journal of Management, 42*(7), 1784–1810, doi:10.1177/0149206314525202) — transparency is perceived quality of intentionally shared information along **disclosure, clarity, and accuracy**, theorized as antecedents to trustworthiness (ability, benevolence, integrity) in Mayer, Davis, and Schoorman’s trust model.

**FACT:** Mayer, Davis, and Schoorman (1995, *Academy of Management Review, 20*(3), 709–734, doi:10.5465/amr.1995.9508080335) — trust is willingness to be vulnerable based on expectations of ability, benevolence, and integrity.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Teens are stakeholders of the diagnostic, not merely subjects of a parent portal. Hide (low disclosure), jargon dump (low clarity), and fake greens (low accuracy) each break a different trustworthiness path — especially integrity when Map later contradicts the rainbow.

| Failure mode | Transparency break | Student trust risk |
|--------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Hide holes | Low disclosure | “They lied / later Map shock” |
| Coverage % fog | Low clarity | Abandon reading; ask parent what it means |
| Fake greens | Low accuracy | Inflated confidence; tutor contradiction |
| Mindset poster only | Clarity without actionable accuracy | Belief unchanged; no FEI attempt |

**Kill:** Teen-facing “complete” theater while parents see holes (or the reverse).  
**Survive:** Same facts, age-appropriate clarity, one next step.

## CXLII.4 Formative use of incomplete evidence — do not grade the hole

**FACT:** Black and Wiliam (1998, *Phi Delta Kappan, 80*(2), 139–148) — formative assessment is assessment whose evidence is used to adapt teaching and learning; the point is feedback that modifies next action, not a finished grade of the person.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** A `format_not_probed` flag is **incomplete formative evidence**, not a score. Student copy must recruit the next Practice adaptation (“try a sealed diagram mission when ready”) rather than close a judgment (“you failed visuals”). Transfer limit: classroom AfL ≠ consumer app — borrow the *use* of evidence, not Black–Wiliam effect-size ads (SAFE-FA ban still holds).

**Kill:** Hole-as-grade / Completeness % as student NS.  
**Survive:** Hole-as-open formative slot + feed-forward CTA.

## CXLII.5 Feedback levels: task/process/where-next — not self-level ability talk

**FACT:** Hattie and Timperley (2007, *Review of Educational Research, 77*(1), 81–112, doi:10.3102/003465430298487) — feedback addresses where am I going / how am I going / where to next, at task, process, self-regulation, and self levels; **self-level** feedback (person praise/criticism) is typically least effective for learning.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Prefer task/process language: “Diagram format: not checked yet (we only hide keys on verified visuals). Next: one sealed Practice item on linear graphs.” Avoid self-level: “You’re not a visual learner” / “You’re so smart you don’t need diagram practice” (both poison).

**Kill:** Self-level hole shame or self-level fake praise.  
**Survive:** Task fact + where-next Practice grain (densifies SAFE-FBLIT / SAFE-SOFTMSG).

## CXLII.6 Ability labels and empty “growth” talk both fail — strategy + attempt, not posters

**FACT:** Mueller and Dweck (1998, *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 75*(1), 33–52, doi:10.1037/0022-3514.75.1.33) — intelligence praise (vs effort/process) orients children toward performance goals and, after failure, more low-ability attributions, less persistence, and worse performance.

**FACT:** Yeager, Hanselman, Walton, et al. (2019, *Nature, 573*, 364–369, doi:10.1038/s41586-019-1466-y) — a short growth-mindset intervention can help some students’ challenge-seeking and grades **conditionally** (notably lower-achieving students in supportive peer norms); it is not a universal poster fix.

**Applied (HYPOTHESIS):** Framing holes as fixed visual talent recreates Mueller/Dweck ability-label damage. Framing holes as “just believe you can grow” without a sealed Practice CTA is empty mindset theater already banned in lab standing rules. Yeager et al. license **challenge-seeking scaffolds in supportive contexts**, not “Growth Hole Score™” splash copy. Student hole UX should invite a concrete attempt, not a belief sermon.

**Kill:** Ability-shame holes; poster-only growth.  
**Survive:** Process invitation — one sealed attempt when stock exists.

## CXLII.7 Calibration honesty: do not inflate greens to “raise confidence”

**Reuse (SAFE-CALIB / SAFE-COLD):** Appropriate confidence beats inflated confidence; humble priors + labeled evidence age beat day-one mastery fireworks.

**HYPOTHESIS:** Painting unprobed FormatIds green to soothe post-scan affect is a **confidence inflation** product bug. It manufactures false strength that CONFMISS recovery and Map later have to unwind — and teaches teens the product lies. Prefer destaked honesty: “Not measured” chips that do not update mastery.

**Kill:** Fake greens as affect management.  
**Survive:** Unprobed ≠ weak and ≠ strong; no mastery write from absence.

## CXLII.9 Product surface — SAFE-STUDHOLE claim contract

1. Gap-scan end screen shows **Checked** vs **Not checked yet** FormatIds (same flags as parent HOLETRUST).  
2. One teen-readable **seal reason** (“We only use verified visuals when we hide the answer”).  
3. Unprobed chips are **neutral / open**, never green-mastery or red-failure.  
4. Copy frames **product hole**, not visual talent; ban classmate FormatId ranks on this screen.  
5. Exactly **one** primary CTA: sealed Practice grain for a hole (or nearest sealed sibling) — soft-require pattern (SAFE-SOFTGATE), not hard lock-in by default.  
6. If no sealed Practice stock → “Not open yet — we’re verifying more” without fake-precision ETA (KNOWNETA discipline).  
7. **Parity:** student and parent hole lists must not contradict; tutor brief uses the same facts.  
8. Ban empty mindset splash; if encouragement appears, it must sit beside grain + CTA.  
9. Telemetry: `student_hole_shown`, `fake_green_flag`, `shame_frame_flag`, `cta→practice_start`, `dual_script_mismatch` — demote Student Hole Score™ / Completeness %.  
10. No mastery / flexibility Map write from `not_probed`.

## CXLII.10 Doctrine — SAFE-STUDHOLE (provisional)

1. **Students see holes** — never parent-only honesty.  
2. **Triad copy** — disclosure + clarity + accuracy (Schnackenberg & Tomlinson).  
3. **Hole ≠ grade** — formative open slot (Black & Wiliam use), not person score.  
4. **Task/process + where-next** — no self-level ability talk (Hattie & Timperley).  
5. **No ability shame / no poster-only growth** (Mueller & Dweck; Yeager conditionality).  
6. **No fake greens** — unprobed ≠ mastery (SAFE-COLD / SAFE-CALIB).  
7. **One Practice CTA**; soft-require default; parity with HOLETRUST.  
8. **No Student Hole Score™ / Completeness % / Honesty Minutes NS**.  
9. **No ACT / identity guarantees** from hole packaging.  
10. Copy spine: “We checked what we can seal. Empty slots mean we have not measured that format yet — not that you failed it. When a sealed Practice item is ready, try one.”

**Confidence:** High on feedback/transparency spines with stated transfer limits. Medium on exact teen wording and CTA compliance (needs STUDHOLE-*). High that hide/pad/shame/fake-green are commercially toxic once Map/tutor contradict the student summary.
